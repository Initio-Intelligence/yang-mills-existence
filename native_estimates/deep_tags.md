The Landau representative is characterised by averaged conditions: [R] p.80–81 (source), "The restrictions on gauge transformations are now given by the conditions (R₀u^j)(y) = 1, y ∈ Λ_j … The set Σ_k is not contained in 𝔅_k(𝔅_k, V) because the gauge transformations u do not satisfy the necessary conditions (1.14)." ### 2.2 What the obstruction of [Deep retained tags and boundary rotations] §6 really says

Prop. N and Example 6.2 of [Deep retained tags and boundary rotations] are correct deduction. They show: *if* a record F(Φ;η) is evaluated at the actual pair Φ(z) = Φ_L(z)^{u(z)^{−1}} with the slots η **held fixed in the frame of Φ(z)**, so that F(Φ(z);η) = F(Φ_L(z); u(z)·η) (Lemma 2.1 of [Deep retained tags and boundary rotations], which needs full (Cov)), then continuing the right-hand side through the printed (U,J)-domain of the *new* space needs a radius that decays like L^{−δ} in the hidden depth. Both hypotheses — full (Cov) at the deep slots, and the new space as the space on which the record is analytic with its slots — fail for the actual tag record. §2.3–2.4 give the first point, §3 the second.

### 2.3 The source has no gap: the slot rotation is a change of variables there

[Deep retained tags and boundary rotations] §2.2 and §10.1 claim that [III] p.276 ("we apply the formulas (3.6), (3.7) [I]") has "the same gap" as [Local deformation of propagators and the minimizer] §6.1 for B-terms with slots. It does not. The source applies the T-step identity to the *integrated* operation. For an old operation T^{(j)} with fluctuation variables A_j, quadratic form Q_Φ(A_j) at the current background and Ad-invariant characteristic functions χ,

 T^{(j)}[B(X, Φ(z), ·)] = ∫dA_j χ(A_j) e^{−Q_{Φ(z)}(A_j)} B(X, Φ(z), A_j) = ∫dA_j χ(A_j) e^{−Q_{Φ_L(z)}(A_j)} B(X, Φ_L(z), A_j),

by (Cov) of B and of Q (Q_{Φ^{g}} = Ad_g Q_Φ Ad_{g^{−1}}, an algebraic identity), Ad-invariance of χ, and the change of variables A_j → u(z)^{−1}·A_j, which preserves the Lebesgue measure on 𝔤^{Σ}. The right-hand side continues in z with A_j real and Φ_L(z) in the domain. So the source's T step is correct for slotted B-terms. The "gap" is created only by ★, which freezes A_j and so forbids the change of variables. LFR I p.197 (source) says this in so many words: "Changing the gauge of U₀ in the integral (1.76) requires the corresponding compensating gauge transformations of the variables V_h, the gauge transformation restricted to ∂⁺(Z_h^∼)^{(h)}, and of the variables V′, the gauge transformation in the adjoint representation. The expressions in the integral (1.76) are invariant with respect to these transformations. We can also choose from the beginning the configuration U₀ in the AL-gauge."

### 2.4 The slot rotation is intrinsic to the law, and no re-parametrisation of the family removes it

Let η be a frozen variable (a measurable function of the pre-step fields) and z the current coordinate. Any re-coordinatisation η ↦ T(η;𝔣) with T depending on frozen data only generates the same σ-field, hence the same conditional law of z; it changes only the formula. A map η ↦ c(z)·η with c depending on z is not a re-coordinatisation of a frozen variable. Therefore, if the joint density of (z,η) is G(z,η) = F(Φ(z);η) with Φ(z) = Φ_L(z)^{u(z)^{−1}} and F fully (Cov), the rotation u(z) in F(Φ_L(z); u(z)·η) cannot be removed by any choice of representative for the family z ↦ Φ(z): writing Φ(z) = Φ′(z)^{c(z)^{−1}} gives F(Φ′(z); c(z)·η) with the same z-dependent rotation at the slots. What *is* at the disposal of the construction is the frozen-measurable part: which frame the frozen variables are expressed in at the base point. The content of §2.5 is that for the deep slots of a tag the source's construction fixes that frame, and it is not the frame of Φ.

**Terminology for this note.** Fix a tag Y of birth b (= the R step k of the quotations; Retain the letter k for the current T step and write b for the birth where needed) and the old determining set 𝔅⁰ := B″_k(Y₁) of (1.61). Its blocks are the old blocks B_j(y) of its layers. Call a block **hidden** if its data in (1.61) are hidden raw data V″ (these are the blocks in (Ω″^{∼2}_{h+1})^c∩Y₁, layers j ≤ h), and **exposed** otherwise (data derived from the current background: M′(U_0^{(AL)}), the boundary-layer data, V″↾_{Λ^c} = the current data by (1.50)). On every hidden block U_{1,2} = U₂ because (Ω″^{∼2}_{h+1})^c ⊂ (Ω″^{∼}_{h+1})^c. The hidden 𝔤-valued slots of the tag are the old fluctuations A_j, j < h, of the operations inside T_h(Z_h∩X) of (1.71), at scale-j sites in old layers of grade ≤ h; the R-step fluctuation B lives on Λ∩Ω″^{∼2}_{h+1} ("The field B is localized in Λ", p.372), i.e. on exposed blocks, at the native mesh.

**The frame.** By (1.54) and p.373, the representative U_k⁰ of its orbit is the Ax(𝔅⁰, U_{1,2})-representative, and on a block it is determined by H″_k and U_{1,2} restricted to that block. On a hidden block U_{1,2} = U₂ is a function of the hidden data alone (1.53). So **the frame of the old configuration on a hidden block is a function of frozen data only**, whatever the current background is.

**Consequence for DN-1 additional assumption.** The source never fixes the joint law of (current fluctuation, hidden A_j): it integrates A_j, and it changes the background's representative freely with compensating transformations (LFR I p.197). Under ★ the conditional law of z given A_j is defined only once A_j is a definite function of the pre-step fields. The source's own formulas (1.54), (1.61)–(1.71) carry the hidden variables in the frame of U_k⁰ relative to U_{1,2}; this frame is frozen-measurable on hidden blocks; and it is the frame in which the record's dependence on the current background is the source's analytic function (1.65). Therefore read DN-1 with the clause

> **(GF)** The hidden internal variables of a second-class component are the variables of the source's formula (1.71), with the old configuration (1.61) taken in the frame (1.54) (axial relative to U_{1,2}), U₂ in any fixed frozen-measurable frame.

This is a clarification, not a choice: any other frozen-measurable frame gives the same law (§2.4) and the same function G(z) after re-coordinatisation; a frame tied to the current background at the hidden sites is not frozen-measurable. [Deep retained tags and boundary rotations]'s Lemma 2.1 silently assumed the latter for all slots.

### 2.6 Lemma F (the hidden blocks do not see the current frame) (deduction; given (1.54) and the uniqueness in [R] (1.19))

Fix a real current coordinate z. Let 𝒲(z) be the configuration (1.61) built on the actual pair Φ(z), and 𝒲_L(z) the same construction built on Φ_L(z) (real for real z). Then there is a real gauge transformation 𝔤(z) on Y₁ with

 𝒲(z) = 𝒲_L(z)^{𝔤(z)}, 𝔤(z) = 1 on every hidden block, 𝔤(z) = u(z)^{−1} on the part of Y₁ where the chain is built on Φ (the blocks whose frame is relative to U₁, U_{k,Λ}, Φ), and on an exposed block anchored to U₂ (a block of Ω″^{∼2}_{h+1}∖Ω″^{∼}_{h+1} with data from Φ) 𝔤(z) = (block-constant extension of u(z)^{−1} at the block's determining sites)·𝔟(z) with 𝔟(z) ∈ (1.14).

*Proof.* (i) *Orbits.* The data of 𝒲(z) are those of 𝒲_L(z) rotated by u(z)^{−1} at the determining sites of the exposed blocks (averaging covariance, which for the group average of [I] (0.5)–(0.10) and the definition (0.12) is an algebraic identity: M(U^g)(b) = g(y)M(U)(b)g(y′)^{−1} at block centres), and unchanged on hidden blocks. Let ĝ be the gauge transformation equal to u(z)^{−1}(y) on each exposed block B(y) and to 1 on hidden blocks. Then M_{𝔅⁰}(𝒲_L^{ĝ}) = M_{𝔅⁰}(𝒲_L)^{ĝ|det} is the data of 𝒲(z), and A(𝒲_L^{ĝ}) = A(𝒲_L). By [III] (2.12) ("the minimal orbit … of the functional U → A(U) on U : U regular and M_B(U) = V") and uniqueness of the minimal orbit ([V] Theorem 1), 𝒲(z) and 𝒲_L(z)^{ĝ} lie on one orbit: 𝒲(z) = 𝒲_L(z)^{ĝ𝔟} with 𝔟 = 1 at every determining site of 𝔅⁰ (the subgroup (1.14)).
(ii) *Hidden blocks.* On a hidden block B both 𝒲(z)|_B and 𝒲_L(z)|_B are the Ax(U₂|_B)-representatives (the frame (1.54) is block-local, p.373, and U_{1,2} = U₂ on B). Since ĝ = 1 on B, 𝒲(z)|_B = (𝒲_L(z)|_B)^{𝔟|_B} with 𝔟|_B ∈ (1.14). Two configurations on one (1.14)-orbit of the block that both satisfy (1.19) relative to the same U₂|_B coincide ([R] p.79: "The conditions (1.19) determine uniquely an element in each orbit given by the subgroup (1.14)"). So 𝔟|_B = 1.
(iii) *Blocks whose reference is built on Φ.* The chain (1.58)→(1.57)→(1.54) is covariant under a simultaneous gauge transformation of Φ and of all derived data: the response operators satisfy H_{Φ^g}(Ad_g X) = Ad_g H_Φ(X) (algebraic), the averages are covariant, and the conditions (1.19) are covariant under a simultaneous transformation of U and U₀ ([V] p.280, "the second point of view"; in the telescoped form of [CR-ref] §2.1, Ū^n(Γ) = Ū₀^n(Γ), both sides transform by g(x_{n+1})(·)g(x_n)^{−1}). Hence U_{k,Λ}[Φ^g] = U_{k,Λ}[Φ]^g, U₁[Φ^g] = U₁[Φ]^g, and on such blocks 𝒲[Φ^g] = 𝒲[Φ]^g, with g = u(z)^{−1}. Uniqueness within each block identifies this with ĝ𝔟.
(iv) *Exposed blocks anchored to U₂.* There ĝ is the block-constant u(z)^{−1}(y) and U₂ does not move, so 𝔟|_B is the element of (1.14) that restores (1.19) relative to U₂|_B; it is determined by the two configurations. □

**Remark.** Only (ii) is used below for the deep slots. (iii)–(iv) show that the non-hidden part behaves exactly as [Deep retained tags and boundary rotations] assumed (rotation by the T-step converter), and that the whole gauge 𝔤(z) is real for real z and explicit.

**Corollary F (the correct real-slice identity for a tag record).** Let F(Φ;η) = 𝔈(𝒲(Φ;η_V); η_A) be a tag record (the structure (TR) of §3). For real z,

 F(Φ(z);η) = 𝔈(𝒲_L(z); 𝔤(z)^{−1}·η_A) with 𝔤(z)^{−1}·η_A = η_A at every deep slot,

and 𝔤(z)^{−1} = u(z) at the slots on blocks built on Φ, and = 𝔟(z)^{−1}·u(z)(y) (block-constant, with the factor 𝔟 of Lemma F(iv)) at the native-mesh slots of B on U₂-anchored exposed blocks. *Proof.* Lemma F and (Cov) of 𝔈 with the real gauge 𝔤(z)^{−1}. □

So the T-step converter does not act on the deep slots. What does act on them is the dependence of 𝒲_L(z) on z, through the response of the *old* reconstruction to the current data — §5.

### 4.2 Repair of the step for the reconstruction clauses ((2.35)/(2.37) and their LFR I analogues)

[Deep retained tags and boundary rotations] Lemma I, proof, paragraph "Clauses (2.35) and (2.37)", invokes "the orbit covariance of complex reconstructions: the extension 'to all orbits … by the equality (181)' of [V] Prop. 9 ((c\*) via [CR] §1), and single-valued on the union of absolute tubes ([CR] Lemma 2.1, conditional)". ### 5.3 The alternative without (H-dec) (for the record)

Dropping the decay factor in (2) (P9♮ alone), the requirement of §6 becomes √(1+k−n(x))·K₉ e^{−μD_x/2} ≤ c for the deepest slot, i.e. with the AF ratio written as the coupling ratio g_k/g_n = (1+β₀g_k²(k−n))^{1/2}: e^{μD_k/2} ≥ C K₉ g_k/g_n.
