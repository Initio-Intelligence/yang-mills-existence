# Current-rooted derivatives of the conditional density

These estimates concern current-rooted derivatives on a specified conditional law. Use the [retained coordinates and Haar factors](retained_coordinates.md), including clause (2″), throughout. The purely native derivatives, retained-data derivatives, and current-independent outer density are separate. For collared box estimates the [two-layer constraint](local_test_estimates.md) is required. A statement about a single holed record does not control overlapping records at a T step; the collective crossing estimate is open. See [external branches](holed_ancestors.md).

**Conditional coordinates.** Clause (2″) in [Retained coordinates](retained_coordinates.md) specifies all variables integrated, including the completed preparation stages and auxiliary gauge variables. On a relocated bond the log Jacobian is combined with the Haar density ratio.

## 1. The law and the objects

### 1.1 The one-step law (★, amended)

Fix a finite cutoff, an admitted geometry, and the T step k→k+1 (K := k+1). The conditioning σ-field 𝒢★ after the preceding R step is generated by: the current field; the history labels **of the source normal form (2.18)** — the sequences {Ω_j},{Λ_j},{Z_j}, component classes, retained gauge graphs, labels of stopped preparations (amendment L1 of [Retained sigma-fields and conditional expectations] §2.3; primitive labels resummed by the source, e.g. P_{k+1},Q_{k+1} at fixed Ω_{k+1}, are integrated); and every variable the main-line representation has not yet integrated: raw large-field fields V_j↾Γ_j and retained fluctuations a_j of unprocessed components, the variables of stopped preparations not yet integrated, fresh reference fields still present, and the internal variables η_Y of every second-class component ("tag") T′_b(Y) from its birth until a successful R step containing it or the terminal step (LFR II (1.102); DN-1 (1)–(2)). The T-step law is the regular conditional law of the current free coordinates z = (z_i)_{i∈I} (free bonds of Λ_{k+1}, sites x_i, |z_i| ≤ P_k on the real support) given 𝒢★. Write 𝔣 for a generic value of the 𝒢★ data.

By C-01 (Annex W (3.14)) with erratum AX part 1 ([U17] l.4308) and DN-1 (3), the log-density of z given 𝔣 is the exponent (3.11) at frozen data plus the exact log-ratios Θ of every retained operation; **no logarithm of an integral over a retained variable occurs** ([Det] §5.1 item 4; [Retained sigma-fields and conditional expectations] §3.1). The selectors of all retained operations do not depend on z (LFR I p.178 (1.3)–(1.9); LFR II p.379; source [Retained sigma-fields and conditional expectations] §2.1).

## 2. Theorem C-05★ (conditional)

## 5. Derivatives outside the current-rooted bound

The criterion asks for "the same local derivative bounds" as C-04, whose test is "a displayed small weighted-Hessian budget plus local source-derivative budgets". C-04 (Annex AH, DS.13–16) supplies, besides the weighted Hessian row, fixed-factorial *mixed native jets* at the current anchor and *local native jets anchored on a native direction, including one with zero current component*. the conditional boundary estimates (last paragraph) asked that any acceptance of C-05 distinguish these; the conditional boundary estimates repeated it ("The original frozen proposition includes local native boundary controls analogous to C-04. A scalar H(ξ) survives in the outer density while disappearing from all current derivatives. Its native response and derivatives of a moving retained-data map require their own identification and bounds.").

1. **Mixed native jets at a current anchor** (AB17.6): follow from the joint contour in (z, b, J, s) by Cauchy, provided A1/A2 are obtained on a joint domain (Route II gives this; Route I's (3.43) gives joint analyticity in the new background and A). Hypotheses: as A1/A2.
2. **Pure native-anchored jets** (anchor a native point x in an old region, no current component): a different root sum. For a native anchor deep in a grade-n region, the records containing x of births j ≥ n+2 each pay e^{−κω/L} from layer crossing but their number of births is K−n; the sum Σ_j W_j e^{−κω_{j−1}/L} converges uniformly only with ω_{j−1} ≥ 8LR_j and R_j ≥ (log g_j^{−2})^r growing along the trajectory (a superlogarithmic birth tail), or by a different organization. open; not examined in the comparison argument; ★-independent. Interpretation additional assumption: the HC.18/C-08 interface does not need it; M-06's "local source-derivative budgets" may.
3. **Derivatives in retained coordinates** (moving retained-data maps; under ★ this includes the fine-scale hidden coordinates η): no analyticity in η is supplied by the source ((2.42) is analytic in (U,J) at fixed A). What C-05 can supply is the *oscillation* bound (v), which is what the representation convention sector-record norm and H-03's posterior input norms consume. Pointwise η-derivatives are open and, by the representation convention, are M-06's to decide whether they are needed at all.
4. **Marked families** (observable marks transported through the step): H-03/M-04–M-06; not C-05.
5. **The value B̃_k(0;𝔣)** in the outer weight: not a derivative bound; its size grows with the volume of the geometry and is H-02's/H-04's concern ([Boundary estimate d] §1.8).
