# Retained selectors and conditional-weight identities

These estimates concern current-rooted derivatives on a specified conditional law. Use the [retained coordinates and Haar factors](retained_coordinates.md), including clause (2″), throughout. The purely native derivatives, retained-data derivatives, and current-independent outer density are separate. For collared box estimates the [two-layer constraint](local_test_estimates.md) is required. A statement about a single holed record does not control overlapping records at a T step; the collective crossing estimate is open. See [external branches](holed_ancestors.md).

A selector is frozen only for the normal-form law with all completed preparation variables removed; the coordinates and integration clause (2″) are specified in [Retained coordinates](retained_coordinates.md).

## 3. Item 2: the simplification, checked

### 3.1 The T-step law under ★: what the boundary perturbation is conditional

Fix the T step k→k+1 and the ★ data 𝔣 (everything except the current free coordinates z). Under DN-1 (3) and erratum AX part 1 ([U17] l.4308, source: "Here a denotes the still retained older integration variables … Every surviving current dependence of its coefficients belongs in Θ"), the log-density of z given 𝔣 is the C-01 exponent (3.11) at frozen data plus the exact log-ratios Θ of every retained operation. By F1, F2, F6 and N1 the current-dependent part of the boundary sector decomposes as

\[
\widetilde B_k(z;\mathfrak f)=\underbrace{\sum_{\rho}\bigl[F_\rho(z;\mathfrak f)-F_\rho(0;\mathfrak f)\bigr]}_{\text{S1: records}}
+\underbrace{\sum_{v\ \text{live tag}}\Theta_v(z;\eta_v,\mathfrak f)}_{\text{S2}^\star}
+\underbrace{\Theta^{\rm OR}(z;\mathfrak f)}_{\text{S4}}+\underbrace{\Theta^{\rm PF}(z;\mathfrak f)}_{\text{S5}}
+\underbrace{F^{\rm het}+F^{\rm reloc}}_{\text{S6}},
\]

where S1 collects every B-record of the main-line action (inherited B^{(j)}(X), R-step records B′^{(b)}(X) with the second-class components of their birth as holes, boundary-assigned E/R pieces, the relocation action change V^{(k)}), each a function of z at frozen 𝔣, including records that touch or cross non-eligible components, stopped-prefix components and frozen tags; S2★ is the current response of the second-class densities (1.71) at frozen internal variables; S4 and S5 are the responses of the ordinary (2.21) and stopped-prefix (T″) densities; S6 the heterogeneous Wilson coefficient and the current exterior relocation. **No term is the logarithm of an integral over a retained variable** ([Det] §5.1 item 4; deduction by definition of ★). The selectors of all retained operations are z-independent (F2 p.178, F6 p.379: tests on raw/localized data; source).

Two facts fix what "records at frozen η" means. (i) Dependence: records with a tag in their carrier depend on the tag's internal variables (F6: the internal sums act on the last exponential; N2: V(Y) depends on A,B,V″ in Y). (ii) Bounds: (1.68)/(1.99) are stated for the analytic extension in (U,J) with those variables suppressed; (1.92) derives the activity bound from (1.68), (1.73), (1.89) alone (N4), so the printed bounds are uniform in the second-class variables **if** (1.68) is — which the source never says but must mean, since T′_k(Y_i) is applied afterwards and (1.75) needs sup|F|. Hypotheses: source input for the dependence, additional assumption for the uniformity.

### 3.2 The combinatorial half is right; uniformity in η is a question about the radius, not the size

**Lemma U (trivial but decisive).** Let F(·;η) be holomorphic on the stadium 𝒮_r for every η in a set 𝔈, with sup_{η}sup_{𝒮_r}|F(·;η)| ≤ w. Then for every real z₀ in the support, z ∈ z₀+D_{r/2}, and every multi-index α, sup_η|∂^αF(z;η)| ≤ α!(2/r)^{|α|}w. *Proof.* Cauchy on the polydisc of radius r/2 about z, which lies in 𝒮_r. □

So (Loc★) uniform in η follows from a sup bound uniform in η **and** a z-analyticity radius r uniform in η. The size of η (large-field raw fields, bounded hidden fields) never enters: the raw field V_b↾Y is regular in the weaker sense required of large-field layers (LFR II p.378: "|V′_k−1| < O(1)B₅M⁶ε_k"; [III] p.256: "|∂V_{j−1}−1| < O(L²)ε_{j−1}"), and the hidden variables are restricted by their own characteristic functions inside (1.71). With η frozen, a record touching n tags is one holomorphic function of z; the multi-kernel incidence problem of [Boundary estimate e] §4 (Example N2 needs an integration) does not arise; the rooted counts are over carriers (one record per (j,X), [III] (2.41)), so (M2) is [Boundary estimate e] Prop. 3.1 and (MS) is not needed. **This part of [Det] §7.1 is correct.** What remains is the radius, §3.3 G2.

### 3.4 Can a record see through a frozen tag cheaply?

Let a record have paid exterior F, declared holes H (closed sets), carrier X = ∪F ∪ ⋃H, and sites i, l.

Then for all i, l with ∂_i∂_lF_ρ ≠ 0, |x_i − x_l|_∞ ≤ s_j(δ_j(P)+2) ≤ M(δ_j(P)+2). *Proof.* Both sites lie in cubes of P; by [Hidden components] Lemma A3 (whose tree is not required to stay in P) diam_∞(∪P) ≤ s_j(δ_j(P)+2). □ Hence e^{χ|x_i−x_l|}|∂_i∂_lF_ρ| ≤ (2/r)²w_ρe^{χM(δ_j(P)+2)}: the hidden diameter of any hole is paid by the paid tree, with no collar. **Tail form.** With (Loc★) in the capped metric to the full carrier, a record with one-cube exterior next to a tag has |∂_i∂_lF| ≍ (w/r²)e^{−ν max(d(x_i,Q),d(x_l,Q))} for sites on opposite sides of Q, and e^{χ|x_i−x_l|} ≥ e^{χ diam Q}: the hole transmits, and the collar (Col_j): d^{(j)}(x_i,Q) ≥ θ diam Q/s_j is what pays it ([Boundary estimate b] Remark 3 to C′; [Boundary estimate c] Prop. So: **no cheap see-through anywhere, conditional on (FT★) and, in tail form, on (Col_j).**

## 4. Lemmas proved in this note

Conventions as in [Hidden components] §4.1 and [Boundary estimate b] §2.1: grids G_n of cubes of side s_n = Mh_n, h_n = L^{n−K}; capped metrics d^{(b)}; visiting size δ_b; sites x_i of grade K with density n_I; D_P or the stadium 𝒮_r.

### 4.1 Tag geometry used below

**(T-box)** Every live tag Q (second-class component of some R step, or a stopped-prefix component) is a closed box whose sides all lie in [20MR_bh_b, 100MR_bh_b] for its birth b (LFR I p.177, source: "it is contained in a cube of the size 100MR_k", "the size must be greater than 20MR_k", "rectangular parallelepipeds"). In particular Δ_Q := diam_∞Q ≤ 5·(min side).

**(T-sep)** Distinct live tags satisfy dist_∞(Q,Q′) ≥ c_sep max(Δ_Q,Δ_{Q′}), c_sep = c_L/100, c_L := inf_{n≥1} L^n/((L+1)n^{e₀}) > 0, e₀ the exponent in [III] (2.9).
*Derivation (additional assumption; from (c) facts).* Let b ≤ b′ be the births. At step b′, Q′ is a whole component of the eligible union, Q lies in a component of Z_{b′} (monotonicity of Z, [III] (2.1)), and Q ⊄ Q′ (else Q is consumed, F2). Distinct components of Z_{b′} are unions of MR_{b′}-cubes of one partition and are joined when they intersect or touch ([III] p.256; LFR II p.386 per [Hidden components] S2), so they are at sup-distance ≥ MR_{b′}h_{b′} = R_{b′}s_{b′} ≥ Δ_{Q′}/100. For the older tag, R_{b′}s_{b′} = R_{b′}L^{b′−b}s_b and R_b ≤ (L+1)(b′−b)^{e₀}R_{b′} ([III] (2.9), p.256), so R_{b′}s_{b′} ≥ c_L R_b s_b ≥ c_LΔ_Q/100. □

### 4.2 Lemma H (hole normalization across grids)

**Input format (FT★) at grid G_j.** A record ρ of birth j consists of a finite set X of G_j-cubes (its carrier, as in [III] (2.41)), a finite set H of live tags with Q ⊂ ∪X for each Q ∈ H, a nonempty set P ⊂ X (paid cubes) and a weight w, such that
 (a) every c ∈ X∖P has int(c) ∩ Q ≠ ∅ for some Q ∈ H;
 (b) every Q ∈ H has a common point with some cube of P;
 (c) w ≤ W e^{−κ δ_j(P)};
 (d) F_ρ obeys (Loc★) with carrier ∪X; at most m₀ records per (j,X).

Put D₀ := ⌈5/c_sep⌉, call Q **big in grid j** if Δ_Q ≥ D₀s_j, and for big Q let Q⁺ := {y : dist_∞(y,Q) ≤ s_j}. Put H^big := {Q ∈ H big}, F := P ∪ {c ∈ X∖P : int(c) meets a small tag of H}.

**Lemma H.** Assume (T-box), (T-sep) and D₀ ≥ 10. Then:
1. ∪X ⊂ ∪F ∪ ⋃_{Q∈H^big}Q⁺, so (Loc★) holds with the carrier ∪F ∪ ⋃Q⁺.
2. δ_j(F) ≤ C_♯(δ_j(P)+1), C_♯ := 1 + 2^{d+1}[(2D₀+5)^d + √d]; hence w ≤ We^{κ}e^{−(κ/C_♯)δ_j(F)}.
3. For distinct big tags, dist_∞(Q⁺,Q′⁺) ≥ 3s_j; so no G_j-cube touches two of them ((H-sep) in the form used by [Boundary estimate b] B2′, C′), and every Q⁺, Q ∈ H^big, meets a cube of F ((Att)).
4. If d^{(j)}(x_i,Q) ≥ θΔ_Q/s_j for all sites ((Col_j), [Boundary estimate d] Lemma NE.3), then d^{(j)}(x_i,Q⁺) ≥ θ′ diam_∞(Q⁺)/s_j with θ′ := (θD₀−√d)/(D₀+2) (> θ/2 when θD₀ ≥ 2√d+2θ).
5. X = F ∪ {c : int(c) ∩ Q ≠ ∅ for some Q ∈ H^big}; so at most m₀ records share (j,F,H^big).

*Proof.* (1) A cube c ∈ X∖F has int(c)∩Q ≠ ∅ only for big Q ∈ H (by deduction and the definition of F); a cube of side s_j meeting Q lies in Q⁺. (2) Let c ∈ F∖P; its interior meets a small Q ∈ H, and Q touches a cube c′ ∈ P by conditional; since Δ_Q < D₀s_j, c is within index distance D₀+1 of c′. Take a tree T meeting every cube of P with |T| ≤ s_j(δ_j(P)+ε). For each c′ ∈ P adjoin a polygonal path through the centres of all cubes within index distance D₀+2 of c′ (at most (2D₀+5)^d cubes, length ≤ (2D₀+5)^d s_j) and a segment of length ≤ √d s_j from a point of T∩c′ to that path. The result is a tree meeting every cube of F of length ≤ |T| + |P|((2D₀+5)^d+√d)s_j, and |P| ≤ 2^d(2δ_j(P)+1) by [Hidden components] Lemma A3. Let ε↓0. (3) By (T-sep), dist_∞(Q,Q′) ≥ c_sep D₀s_j ≥ 5s_j, so the enlargements are ≥ 3s_j apart; a cube of side s_j touching both would force distance ≤ s_j. (Att): Q touches c′ ∈ P ⊂ F, hence Q⁺ ∩ c′ ≠ ∅. (4) A point of Q⁺ is within Euclidean distance √d s_j of Q and the capped unit is ≥ s_j, so d^{(j)}(x,Q⁺) ≥ d^{(j)}(x,Q) − √d ≥ θΔ_Q/s_j − √d; and diam_∞(Q⁺)/s_j = Δ_Q/s_j + 2 ≤ (1+2/D₀)Δ_Q/s_j; the stated θ′ follows from Δ_Q/s_j ≥ D₀. (5) "⊂": a cube of X∖F has interior meeting a big Q ∈ H by (1)'s argument. "⊃": if int(c) ∩ Q ≠ ∅ with Q ⊂ ∪X, a point of int(c)∩Q lies in a cube of X, and the only cube containing a point of int(c) is c. □

**Remarks.** (i) Big holes for which Δ_Q/s_j is large are exactly the ones for which (Col_j) matters; small tags are absorbed at a constant loss. (ii) The constant C_♯ is astronomically large (D₀ = 750 for c_sep = 1/150 and d = 4 gives C_♯ ≈ 2^5·1505^4 ≈ 1.6·10^{14}); the resulting threshold κ ≥ C_♯(ν/2+1+κ′_cnt) is legitimate only because the source leaves κ free ([III] p.260 per [Hidden components] S12, source), and nothing else in the construction should be made to depend on it. The loss comes from charging every cube near a paid cube, not from the tags; a better argument would use that a descendant pays the *cover* of the ancestor's paid set, so that unpaid cubes near small tags are at index distance ≤ 1 from paid ones, giving (2D₀+5)^d → 3^d. This remains an assumption version because it needs the T-step relocalization rule in a precise form that is additional assumption. (iii) With Lemma H in hand, [Boundary estimate b] Theorem C′ + Prop. B2′ apply in every grid j, with the hole family {Q⁺ : Q live, big in grid j}; the holes are closed sets rather than cube sets, which C′'s Lemma C3 and B2′'s counting allow verbatim (they use a hole only through its diameter, (Att), (H-sep) and (Col)).

### 4.4 Corollary T (tag influence load) conditional

Let records of all births j ≥ b whose carrier meets a live tag Q_v (birth b, Δ_v) satisfy (Loc★) with holes in the normalized format of Lemma H (so (Col_j) for Q_v and (H-sep_j)), with weights w_ρ ≤ W_je^{−κ_*δ_j(F_ρ)}, κ_* − ν − 1 ≥ κ′_cnt(d), χM ≤ ν/4, and let (Geo) hold at c = ν/(8Θ′). Put
Λ_v := Σ_{ρ: X_ρ∩Q_v≠∅} Σ_l (P_k/r) sup_𝔣|∂_lF_ρ| e^{χ r_l}, r_l := dist_∞(x_l,Q_v) (+ the same sum for Θ_v with weight s_{*,b}). Then Λ_v ≤ C_Λ(s_{*,b} + e^{C₀}sup_jW_j) with C_Λ = C_Λ(additional assumption; M,ν,θ,n_I,m₀,γ), uniformly in Δ_v, b, the age K−b and the number and positions of other tags; and the oscillation in η_v of the z-dependent part of the T-step log-density over the real support is ≤ 2Λ_v.
*Proof.* This is [Boundary estimate d] Theorem R6 Step 2 with two changes: a record meeting Q_v is rooted at one of the ≤ (Δ_v/s_j+3)^d cubes of G_j meeting Q_v and counted by [Boundary estimate b] B2′ there (no kernel and no single-hole restriction are used in that step); and the geometric inequality d^{(j)}(x_l,Q_v) ≤ Θ′d^{(j)}(x_l,X_ρ) + δ_j(F_ρ) + 2√d holds for records with several holes by [Boundary estimate b] Lemma C3(2)'s argument (if the nearest point of X_ρ to x_l lies in another hole Q′, pass from it to a touching paid cube, length ≤ √d D_{Q′} ≤ (√d/θ)d^{(j)}(x_l,Q′) by (Col_j)). The rest of R6 Step 2 is unchanged. The oscillation statement is the integration along the segment in [Boundary estimate c] Prop. R3 Step 3. □
This is the quantity M-06 needs for the "first-order responses of regular marks to frozen tag variables" ([Det] §7.1): the influence of a whole tag state η_v on the current law is bounded by Λ_v, with no component-incidence load and no dependence on the number of records attached to the tag beyond the rooted norm.
