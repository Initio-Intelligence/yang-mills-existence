## 5. C06 on the complete conditional potential

The exact law has the form

\[
 d\nu_v(z)=Z_v^{-1}{\bf1}_{D_v}(z)
 \exp\{-\tfrac12 z^TKz+b^Tz+W(z;v)\}\,dz,
 \tag{CS.8}
\]

with the actual principal constrained precision \(K\), exterior force \(b=-K_{IO}y\), and disjoint \(W=W_{ER}+W_B+W_{\rm loc}\). Older fields are retained parameters, not appended to the current vector \(y\). The current exterior force is allowed to be large near the boundary.

The primary principal-operator estimate gives \(K\ge\lambda_0 I\) and exponentially decaying entries. C03/C04, CS.6, and the actual constrained classical/Haar/coarea estimates give a small complete Hessian perturbation. With the parameter choices in the C06 companion,

\[
 H(z):=D_z^2[-\log\nu_v(z)]
       =K-W''(z)\ge\tfrac34\lambda_0 I
 \tag{CS.9}
\]

on the interior of the full literal support. This expression refers to the smooth potential, not derivatives of its indicator.

For a 1-Lipschitz physical function \(f\), conjugate by \(D_f=\operatorname{diag}(e^{\gamma f_i})\). The off-diagonal precision change has norm at most \(\gamma A_K\), while both weighted Schur sums of the conjugated perturbation are small. One fixed \(\gamma\) gives accretivity at least \(\lambda_0/2\). Choosing \(f_i=\rho(i,j)\) proves

\[
 \|[H(z)^{-1}]_{ij}\|\le(2/\lambda_0)e^{-\gamma\rho(i,j)}.

\]

The complete native derivative clauses are retained on the common strong domain, including current-independent native scalars. ## 6. C08: interacting covariance, means and moments

C07 identifies \(D_v\) as the actual product of symmetric convex free-bond balls after the complete source operation. On smooth inner approximations of these balls, the scalar Neumann form has a differentiated vector form whose face curvature term is nonnegative. Blockwise constant spatial conjugations preserve the tangency conditions. Using CS.9 and the same weighted perturbation estimate gives the scalar inequality

\[
 |\operatorname{Cov}_{\nu_v}(F,G)|
 \le\sum_{i,j}\mathsf G_{ij}
       \|\nabla_iF\|_{L^2(\nu_v)}
       \|\nabla_jG\|_{L^2(\nu_v)},
 \qquad
 \mathsf G_{ij}=(2/\lambda_0)e^{-\gamma\rho(i,j)}.
 \tag{CS.11}
\]

Pass this scalar inequality to the literal norm balls by convergence of the densities and Sobolev approximation. No identity equates interacting covariance with the matrix \(H(z)^{-1}\). No convergence assertion for an unspecified arbitrary-vector boundary realization is needed.

The fixed complete score and Hessian majorant satisfy

\[
 d_i=|D_iW(0)|\le C/P_k,
 \qquad \sup_i\sum_j e^{a\rho(i,j)}A_{ij}\le C/P_k^2,
 \qquad A_{ij}\ge\sup_z\|D_iD_jW(z)\|.
 \tag{CS.12}
\]

The actual exterior has \(|y|\le CP_k\). The precision row gives \(|b_i|\le CP_ke^{-\delta d_O(i)}\). In the source law with interpolated force and interaction, the even zero-force Gaussian on the same support has mean zero. Differentiating normalized means retains the covariance with the complete score. If \(M_i\) is the supremum of the means over this interpolation, CS.11 gives

\[
 M\le\mathsf G(|b|+d)+\mathsf G A(M+\sigma\mathbf1),
 \qquad \sigma=(d_{\mathfrak g}/\lambda)^{1/2}.
 \tag{CS.13}
\]

Choose the common ceiling so the weighted norm of \(\mathsf G A\) is at most \(1/2\). The positive numerical resolvent \((I-\mathsf GA)^{-1}\mathsf G\) and the distance inequality then imply

\[
 |\nu_v z_i|\le C(P_k^{-1}+P_k e^{-c d_O(i)}),
 \qquad
 \|z_i-\nu_vz_i\|_{L^p(\nu_v)}\le C\sqrt p.
 \tag{CS.14}
\]

The second estimate follows from the same unweighted covariance bound under every real linear tilt. The constants are uniform in \(p\) except for the displayed \(\sqrt p\), and uniform in the actual fiber. The full nonlinear constrained chart transfers these estimates to the stated current field and finite covariant-gradient stencils, with the reference field and physical mesh powers kept explicit.

For the prescribed native scalar/tensor fields \(O_{\alpha,x}\), put \(Q_x=1+B_x+M_x\), retaining the actual reference-curvature and conditional-mean envelopes. The companion proves

\[
 \|O_{\alpha,x}-\nu_vO_{\alpha,x}\|_p
 \le Ch_k^{-4}(Q_x\sqrt p+p),
 \tag{CS.15}
\]

and, for \(m\ge2\),

\[
 |\kappa_{\nu_v}(O_{\alpha_1,x_1},\ldots,O_{\alpha_m,x_m})|
 \le C^{m+1}(m!)^2h_k^{-4m}
       \prod_iQ_{x_i}
       e^{-\theta\tau(x_1,\ldots,x_m)/(m-1)}.
 \tag{CS.16}
\]

A longest-edge cut of a minimum spanning tree reduces the cumulant to a scalar covariance of products. The partition coefficient is bounded by a fixed exponential times \((m-1)!\), and the native \(L^{2m}\) norms supply one further factorial. Summing the divided-tree kernel over the other anchors in four dimensions costs at most \(C^m(m!)^5\). The anchored cumulant bound therefore has fixed factorial power seven. ## 7. Additional results and exact limits of their use

The C08 proof also supplies finite-support quadratic regulator moments for the actual centered conditional current. For any real symmetric positive semidefinite matrix \(T\) and \(0\le t<\lambda/(2\|T\|)\), joint sub-Gaussian linear tilts and Gaussian auxiliary integration give

\[
 \nu_v\exp\{t(z-m)^TT(z-m)\}
 \le\det(I-2tT/\lambda)^{-1/2},
 \qquad m=\nu_vz.
 \tag{CS.17}
\]

The matrix may be restricted to any finite set of actual free-coordinate components. Its trace/volume and any nonzero center cost remain explicit. This is not a volume-free bound for every strong history regulator.

For the exterior part y of the same newly introduced constrained-current field, the full current chart gives a mixed inner/exterior derivative row. The normalized response is

\[
 D_{y_a}\nu_yF
 =\nu_yD_{y_a}F+
       \operatorname{Cov}_{\nu_y}(F,S_a),
 \qquad S_a=D_{y_a}\log p_y.
 \tag{CS.18}
\]

The centered score is bounded in \(L^p\) by \(C\sqrt p\), and its kernel response is exponentially localized, with the \(O(1)\) precision contribution retained. The statement includes the source's real first-derivative relocation at fixed geometry. It does not differentiate arbitrary older raw arguments, varying selector surfaces, or a changing history chart.
