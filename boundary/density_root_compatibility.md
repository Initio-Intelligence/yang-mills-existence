Complete boundary estimates and their C06/C08 applications in this chapter are conditional on CR and the other displayed source, AF, domain and support assumptions. Exact density identities and abstract inequalities retain their individually stated hypotheses.

The exact partial-gauge transport and the surviving fixed-exterior derivative are given in the chapter [Gauge covariance of partial integrals and exterior scores](../continuum/partial_gauge_transport.md), equations PG.2–PG.6 and the subsequent anchored-score criterion.

## 1. The correct input to the stopped-law comparison

The needed incoming estimate is an anchored response catalog

\[
 \sup_{x,a,a_*}\sum_G\sup_\zeta
   |F_G(\Phi_\zeta(x,a),{\rm raw}(x,a))
        -F_G(\Phi_\zeta(x,a_*),{\rm raw}(x,a_*))|
 \le d_r A_{r-1}.
 \tag{DR.1}
\]

The raw supremum is outside the sum. A sum of full values of every atom
with any nonzero native dependence would be too strong: arbitrarily remote

genuine forcing leaf and the resulting summable row.

grade-j raw slot has a creation/geometric grade above j and can use the
bounded two-value estimate. An older geometric grade m≤j is native-only
and needs the actual native-map response. It cannot use a raw Cauchy
derivative.

## 4. A positive local cumulant estimate with all old carriers retained

The remote score estimate RL.17 does not cover a local nonlinear
cumulant inside P. Such a cumulant has no new exterior crossing. Its
smallness instead comes from two incoming relative factors.

Let \(\mu_\zeta\) be the actual ordinary normalized reference on P, with
one common positive reference \(\nu\) and

\[
 |d\mu_\zeta|\le M\,d\nu,\qquad
 \sum_G w_G\sup_\zeta|D_G(\zeta,a)|\le K
 \quad\text{for every fixed raw }a.
 \tag{DR.4}
\]

Here D_G is the complete anchored response in (DR.1). The weights must
be chosen so that the output union-graph weight is bounded by the product
of the input weights, times the separately displayed cost of adding P.

For a tuple \(G_1,\ldots,G_n\), retain the *whole* cumulant coefficient

\[
 \kappa_{\mu_\zeta}(D_{G_1},\ldots,D_{G_n})
 =
 \sum_{\pi\in\mathcal P_n}
   (-1)^{|\pi|-1}(|\pi|-1)!
   \prod_{B\in\pi}
    \mu_\zeta\!\left(\prod_{i\in B}D_{G_i}\right).
 \tag{DR.5}
\]

It remains labeled by the multiset of incoming atoms and retains the
support

\[
 D(P)\cup X_{G_1}\cup\cdots\cup X_{G_n}.
 \tag{DR.6}
\]

One must not replace this by an unlabeled scalar allegedly supported
only in P. The incoming carriers may extend outside P and matter to
both the rooted norm and later self extraction.

Taking positive absolute bounds **after** retaining this tuple gives

\[
 \frac1{n!}\sum_{G_1,\ldots,G_n}
       \prod_iw_{G_i}\,
       \sup_\zeta|\kappa_{\mu_\zeta}(D_{G_1},\ldots,D_{G_n})|
 \le b_n(M)K^n,
 \tag{DR.7}
\]

where

\[
 b_n(M)=\frac1{n!}\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal P_n}
                       (|\pi|-1)!M^{|\pi|}.
 \tag{DR.8}
\]

**Proof.** For each block B of a partition, use the same positive
reference \(\nu\) and put the native supremum inside its integral.
Use an independent integration copy for each partition block. At each
copy the pointwise catalog sum in (DR.4) bounds the sum over the labels
appearing in that block. Every label occurrence appears in one block.
Thus the positive tuple sum is at most \(M^{|\pi|}K^n\). No independent
raw supremum is introduced. Summing the partition coefficients proves
(DR.7). ∎

The exponential generating function is explicit:

\[
 \sum_{n\ge1}b_n(M)t^n
 =\sum_{q\ge1}\frac{M^q}{q}(e^t-1)^q
 =-\log\{1-M(e^t-1)\}.
 \tag{DR.9}
\]

For \(MK\) below a fixed constant, its terms of degree at least two
therefore sum to \(C_MK^2\). This is a positive tuple-catalog estimate,
stronger than a bound on the single scalar
\(\log\mu_\zeta(e^{t\sum_GD_G})\). Scalar common-mark analyticity by
itself would not justify the sum of the individual tuple norms.

The degree-one term, together with the extracted anchor scalar, is the
complete ordinary transport \(\mu_\zeta V_{\rm rel}\). It uses the
original one-time integration marks. The degree-at-least-two sector
is a new local nonlinear contribution.

 \tag{DR.11}

\[

 \tag{DR.13}

Then \(\sigma_r^{-1}\le e^{s_0\ell_r^p}\), and

\[
