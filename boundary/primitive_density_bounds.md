# Primitive ordinary density variations and power counts

**Composed-response hypothesis (CR).** Each complete boundary conclusion below assumes the displayed native-chart and spatial response estimates for the actual iterated reconstruction, with the prescribed earlier innovations and raw variables fixed, uniformly over the admitted histories. A sufficient estimate for the latent contribution is (A.8) in [Conditional-law estimates and the continuum application](../continuum/conditional_to_continuum.md), refined for the anchored exterior derivative in PG.18–PG.20. The two endpoint bounds concern the identical Wilson-subtracted primitive, its actual cutoff and its independently extended current. A direct minimizing-field derivative alone does not establish this hypothesis. The application to every required original Wilson history remains open. Complete boundary estimates and their C06/C08 applications in this chapter are conditional on CR and the other displayed source, AF, domain and support assumptions. Exact density identities and abstract inequalities retain their individually stated hypotheses.

In the projective density formulation, a sufficient comparison is
\[
 \sup_{\zeta,x,a}\left|
 \log\frac{W_\zeta(x,a)W_0(x,a_*)}
               {W_0(x,a)W_\zeta(x,a_*)}
 \right|\le\delta_r<\pi/2,
\]
on the stipulated chart with the required uniform rates and actual fixed support. The composed ordinary response is the unresolved input in its derivation. The exact partial-gauge transport and the surviving fixed-exterior derivative are given in the chapter [Gauge covariance of partial integrals and exterior scores](../continuum/partial_gauge_transport.md), equations PG.2–PG.6 and the subsequent anchored-score criterion.

**Scale convention.** The Ward estimate uses the evaluation age $n-b$. When $b\le n<j$, one also needs decay in the forcing age $j-n$. The latter response for the actual composed field, with earlier innovations fixed, is conditional on CR. The two-age interpolation SR.12–SR.24 is used here in place of the one-age calculation of OR.19.

This analysis concerns the ordinary preintegration estimate SD.25–SD.27, the exact mass cancellation SD.6a–SD.6e, and their interface with the incoming relative tail. It does not use a prior C05 completion label.

## 1. Determination

The ordinary projective estimate

\[
 \sup_{\zeta\in\mathcal D^{++}}\sup_{x,a,a_*}
 \left|H_{\rm scalar,ord}(\zeta;x,a)
          -H_{\rm scalar,ord}(\zeta;x,a_*)\right|
 \le C(Q)\ell_r^{D_0}
 \tag{OR.1}
\]

follows under CR at the stated source/C01 scope, with \(D_0\) independent of the selected ordinary profile enlargement exponent \(Q\), provided “ordinary” means the mark-zero source family. Explicit incoming completed-relative insertions, and their explicitly marked scalar descendants outside the canonical self operation, remain in the separate relative catalog. They are not covered by the ordinary full-tree bounds in this analysis. The literal canonical self operation is unchanged: its historical self action is not set to zero. A source-small forcing coefficient already bounded on that unchanged canonical reference may retain historical self data while remaining in the ordinary source family.

The proof below gives the required old-grade sum explicitly. In particular the inverse coupling appearing in the conservative relative estimate RL.7/RL.11 is **not** inserted into (OR.1). For the ordinary difference there is an actual \(g_jP_j\) fluctuation factor which cancels the \(g_b^{-1}\) native derivative cost. The old \(E\) marginal cancellation is performed before root counting. Recent births \(b>j\), older prefix coefficients, and the top overlap band have finite polynomial costs.

The literal identity SD.6a–SD.6e is also valid on the specified source branch. Its use is limited to the actual source joint density with the actual triangular changes of variables and Jacobians. A different normalized partial kernel cannot be introduced and then declared equal to that joint density. Fixed conditioning must concern the variables actually fixed in the intended source object.

These conclusions settle the ordinary part of SD.37. They do not by themselves settle RL.11, the new-root estimate for an archived relative record, or the one-time transport recurrence. Those are separate estimates.

## 3. Geometry and notation for one stopped attempt

The global attempt is at scale \(r\), with lower prefix endpoint \(h=r-N_r\). A true stage \(j\) has \(h\le j<r\). Write \(S_j(P)\) for its actual free-coordinate strip in one eligible component and

\[
 D_j(P)=|S_j(P)|_{\text{own }j\text{-mesh}}
 \le C(1+R_{j+1})^4.
 \tag{OR.2}
\]

Both free \(A_j\) and free \(B_j\) variables are included. This is a proper-grid count from the bounded componentwise eligible ancestry. It is not the fine subdivision of a final current box.

The source preparation length and top-band estimates give, for fixed exponents,

\[
 N_r\le C\ell_r^{p_N},\qquad
 L_0^{cN_0}\le C\ell_r^{p_{\rm top}(c)},\qquad
 \sum_{j=h}^{r-1}D_j(P)\le C\ell_r^{p_N+4p_R}.
 \tag{OR.3}
\]

Here \(c\) is a fixed number of map/stencil operations. The precise exponent is immaterial, but it is fixed before \(Q\). Any finite list of stage/proper-support/recent-birth labels adds only another fixed power of \(N_r\).

For a point in a genuine old stratum \(b\le j\), the prepared scaled distance from stage-\(j\) forcing has the source estimate

\[
 d_{\rm graded}\ge c_M(j-b)-C,\qquad c_M\longrightarrow\infty
 \quad\text{as }M/M_1\longrightarrow\infty.
 \tag{OR.4}
\]

This is the content of LFR I (1.47), rather than a count of current-grid cells. A full old carrier can connect its true old port to a higher attachment. Split into the two cases: either the full old-tree path pays the crossed strata before that attachment, or the source map row pays them after it. Since the ordinary carrier has no free component interiors, no crossing is lost in this split.

Thus, after retaining fixed shares for ordinary tree enumeration and output localization, one can choose a fixed rate

\[
 \Gamma_B>4\log L+\log2+\epsilon
 \tag{OR.5}
\]

for the combined full-tree/actual-map response. The \(\log2\) is sufficient for the conservative dyadic Cauchy loss described on CMP119 p.277. This is the ordinary full-tree case; it makes no assertion about a previously contracted relative carrier.

## 4. Ordinary \(B\): the coupling cancellation before the grade sum

Let \(F_b(X)\) be a whole ordinary coefficient at its proper native grade \(b\). Its source bound is

\[
 \sup_{\mathcal D_b^{++}}|F_b(X)|
 \le B_0e^{-\kappa d_b(X)}.
 \tag{OR.6}
\]

Fix all explicit raw variables of this coefficient. First consider its dependence on a stage-\(j\) integration variable only through the native reconstruction. The real input displacement has physical angular size at most \(Cg_jP_j\). Compose the source maps on the common external \(++\) chart before differentiating the coefficient.

The native directional Cauchy bound divides (OR.6) by \(\alpha_b^{++}\), with at most the conservative factor \(2^{j-b}\) for the source dyadic reserve. The actual map row supplies \(g_jP_j\), its physical mesh factors, and the graded decay. Pair the native measure in the row before summing any old spatial roots.

Put \(a=j-b\). The retained AF profile gives

\[
 \frac{g_jP_j}{\alpha_b^{++}}
 \le C\,\frac{g_j}{g_b}
              \frac{\ell_j^{p_0}}{\ell_b^{q_{\rm ord}+Q}}
 \le C_Q(1+a)^{1/2}.
 \tag{OR.7}
\]

The last bound is deliberately coarse. Once \(q_{\rm ord}>p_0\), it can retain a favorable logarithmic factor as well. No inverse power of \(g_r\) remains. The logarithmic ratio does not produce an age power proportional to \(Q\): \(\ell_b\ge\ell_j-C\), so a fixed \(Q\) only changes a constant after the ceiling is selected.

At a fixed proper \(j\)-root, the old root density is \(CL^{4a}\). Full-tree anchored enumeration is bounded by a fixed constant after a reserved part of \(\kappa\) is spent. Polynomial footprint, finite-moment, and old label factors can be written \((1+a)^{s_B}\). Hence the complete old-\(B\) response rooted at one actual stage-\(j\) cell is at most

\[
 C_QB_0
 \sum_{a\ge0}(1+a)^{s_B+1/2}
 2^a L^{4a}e^{-\Gamma_Ba}
 \le C_Q'B_0.
 \tag{OR.8}
\]

The sum is finite by (OR.5), independently of volume, cutoff depth, and \(Q\) except through fixed constants. This is the missing distinction between the ordinary row and a conservative general relative row with an exposed inverse coupling.

The argument estimates the **whole scalar difference** on \(++\). It takes the needed native derivative within the coefficient's prescribed source reserve, multiplies by the actual finite fluctuation displacement, and integrates that one native path. It does not take an additional external \(++\) derivative or assume a fourth analytic radius. The external \(+\)-to-\(++\) Cauchy step occurs only later, in Section 8.

### 4.1 Direct raw slots and recent coarser grades

If the coefficient directly contains an integrated \(A_j\) rather than only its native map, do not differentiate it in that raw argument. Bound its difference by its two values at the two common real tuples.

The direct slot must have been introduced after grade \(j\): its actual creation label is at least \(j+1\). A later ordinary transformation which introduces such a slot is a new source response, with that new creation label. An old unchanged coefficient is not declared to acquire a direct slot merely because its native argument is reconstructed.

There are at most \(N_r\) such creation scales in the active window. Each actual \(j\)-cell lies in only a fixed number of compatible coarser cells at a given proper grade. The full-tree sum is rooted at that actual coarse cell; no factor \(L^{4(b-j)}\) is introduced. Thus this case costs at most

\[
 C B_0N_r^{s_1}D_j(P)
 \tag{OR.9}
\]

after the finite stage/proper-grade labels are retained. Any actual reconstruction leg before the contact is summed with its normalized native row first and gives the same count. These statements are about genuine slots; filling a protected footprint with invented slots would invalidate them.

Combining (OR.8)–(OR.9), the ordinary \(B\) contribution to the centered prefix scalar is at most

\[
 C_QB_0N_r^{s_2}L_0^{c_BN_0}
          \sum_{j=h}^{r-1}D_j(P).
 \tag{OR.10}
\]

Its logarithmic degree is fixed before \(Q\).

## 5. Older ordinary \(C\) coefficients and the top band

- A direct raw slot is treated by the two-value bound of Section 4.1. Its actual slot-creation label is in the recent window.
- A native-only response is treated by (OR.7)–(OR.8), with \(C_0\) in place of \(B_0\), and with its actual proper grade.
- A current-attempt \(C_k^{(n)}\) is an intermediate source mass. It cancels functionally when the actual attempt is unrolled, as explained in Section 9. It is not a second copy of the incoming action.

For a fixed old proper grade \(b\le j\), retaining the old outer-attempt and integration labels costs a fixed polynomial in \(1+j-b\) and in the preparation lengths. An old attempt whose outer label exceeds \(j\) is among the finitely many recent labels. The remaining attempts have outer labels between the proper grade and \(j\). \[
 \ell_b\le C+\ell_j+\log(1+j-b)
 \tag{OR.11}
\]

puts these losses into another fixed logarithmic factor and another polynomial in \(1+j-b\). The exponential margin in (OR.8) pays them.

Alternatively, use the source's already regrouped ordinary \(B\) family at that proper grade; it includes the older \(C\) sector once. These are two estimates of the same ordinary family, not two contributions to be added. In neither version is a separate spatial-component supremum introduced for every old \(C\).

### 6.1 Births \(b\le j\)

Keep the own vacuum and Wilson subtraction inside each primitive fluctuation difference. The source nearby Ward calculation retains a coefficient order \(p\) with its actual field degree \(d\ge p\):

\[
 C E_0(\alpha_b^{++})^{-p}(Q_j^{++})^d,
 \qquad 2\le p\le5,\qquad d\ge p.
 \tag{OR.14}

The fifth-order integral remainder has \(p=d=5\). The Wilson projection has coefficient order two and its corresponding field powers. Replacing all inverse radii by fifth powers and then retaining only two field powers would destroy the estimate.

**One-age estimate: restricted validity.** The following sentence and sum omit the distinction between evaluation and integration scales: after the own marginal quadratic cancellation, the near branch was claimed to have \(L^{-(5-\vartheta)(j-b)}\), \(0<\vartheta<1\).  \tag{OR.15}

### 6.2 Births \(j<b\le r\)

Only the finite preparation window is involved. Keep each recent \(b\)-primitive whole with its own Wilson subtraction. Its coefficient satisfies

\[
 |\beta_b|\le CE_0(\alpha_b^{++})^{-2}.
 \tag{OR.17}
\]

On a proper \(b\)-cell the admitted curvature is at most
\(C\alpha_b^{++}(\eta/h_b)^2\). The Wilson plaquette action has a quadratic zero. The number of plaquettes is \(O((h_b/\eta)^4)\), so

\[
 |A(\phi_b,U;\hbox{one }b\hbox{-cell})|
 \le C(\alpha_b^{++})^2,\qquad
 |\beta_bA(\phi_b,U;\hbox{one }b\hbox{-cell})|\le CE_0.
 \tag{OR.18}
\]

The primitive and Wilson curvature radii must be the same chosen ordinary profile; this is ensured by the source-native admission, not by assuming \(\beta_b=O(1)\).

The response of this whole primitive to a stage-\(j\) strip has a true forcing endpoint. Pair the actual map row with its native measure before counting its positions. A proper \(j\)-cell belongs to a bounded number of \(b\)-cells for each \(b>j\), while remote \(b\)-cells are summed by that row. Thus the cost is \(CD_j(P)\) per recent birth, up to fixed prefix-map factors. It is not \(D_j(P)L^{4(b-j)}\).

## 7. Remaining ordinary factors and the role of raw masses

The ordinary \(R\) family has its own positive coupling power and vacuum subtraction. Its independently verified source coefficient estimates, applied on the same selected ordinary profile, bound it by a constant times the polynomial prefix count. Fixed inverse native-radius costs are absorbed by its stipulated stronger coupling power. The sharper residual small power is useful but unnecessary for (OR.1).

The actual precision, Schur, coarea, Haar and constrained classical factors have fixed absolute coefficient charts. Their complete complex projective differences on the working chart obey

\[
 Cg_r\ell_r^{D_1}
   +Cg_r^{-u}\ell_r^{D_3}e^{-cR_r}.
 \tag{OR.20}
\]

The second term is allowed here because it has an actual growing collar. A bare \(g_r^{-u}\ell_r^{-Q}\) would not be allowed in the ordinary Cauchy phase estimate.

Pure Gaussian terms retain the actual \(P_j^2\) field factors and native trace dimensions. Schur differentiation retains the principal inverse response. None of these bounds identifies that inverse with interacting covariance.

A raw-only canonical mass is an arbitrary positive factor in the reference measure if its raw argument is fixed. It is not deleted from the integration. LFR II p.392 states that the completed second-class mass depends on the new retained raw field on its component and does not participate in subsequent ordinary transformations. The simple eligible segment in LFR I (1.2) has the source one-step form with no new large-field factors in its last \(N_r\) operations, while the deep \(T_h\) operation is unchanged. This is the relevant source-compatible representation for the current attempt.

If an alternative representation places a renewal or its new raw multiplier inside those active labels, it must be checked against that simple-segment identity. It cannot be allowed merely by calling the old mass “archived.” Similarly, if the source coordinate map changes its raw argument, its response has not been shown to be ordinary or zero by the argument above.

Equations (OR.10), (OR.13), and (OR.19), together with the ordinary \(R\) bound and the proper counts (OR.2)–(OR.3), prove (OR.1) with a finite exponent \(D_0\) selected before \(Q\).

\[
 D_{\rm scalar}(\zeta;x,a,a_*)

       -H_{\rm scalar,ord}(\zeta;x,a_*).
 \tag{OR.21}
\]

The actual source successive-insertion theorem admits it on the external \(++\) strong ball with a fixed affine margin. It is not enough that an unrelated modified output point satisfies only some first-order field bounds. The retained field/gradient/current map interface is used here.

\[
 |D_{\rm scalar}(\zeta;x,a,a_*)-
      D_{\rm scalar}(0;x,a,a_*)|
 \le C_Q\ell_r^{D_0+D_{\rm map}-Q}.
 \tag{OR.22}

\[
 \ell_j/\ell_r=1+O(1/\ell_r),\qquad h\le j\le r.
 \tag{OR.23}

Thus choosing \(Q\) once does not introduce a new running power

## 9. Exact source masses and stopping

LFR I (1.62) identifies the next \(C\) as the logarithm of the literal integral in (1.60), plus its Wilson coefficient correction. With \(t_n=(g_r^{(n)}(\cdot))^{-2}\),

\[
 C^{(n+1)}=\log I_{n+1}
           +A(t_{n+1}-t_n,U^{(n+1)}),
\]
\[
 -A(t_{n+1},U^{(n+1)})+C^{(n+1)}
 =-A(t_n,U^{(n+1)})+\log I_{n+1}.
 \tag{OR.25}
\]

The last equality is exact because the Wilson action is linear in its coefficient. The extracted \(E+R+B+B^{(n)}\) action at the same reference field plus its complete difference inside (1.60) restores the old action at the pulled-back field. The actual Gaussian, \(P^{(j)}\), normalization, coarea and relocation factors complete the source change of variables.

Accordingly each source step can be written

\[
 W_{j+1}(y)=\int J_j(a_j,y)
                  W_j(\Phi_j(a_j,y))\,da_j.
 \tag{OR.26}
\]

Its normalized conditional kernel is the displayed numerator divided by that same \(W_{j+1}(y)\). On multiplying the actual nested kernels and the final density, every \(W_j\) cancels at the identical intermediate source field. The remaining joint density is the pullback of the incoming density times the product of the actual Jacobians. This proves SD.4 in common coordinates; it does not assert cancellation for arbitrary independently chosen kernels.

The successful branch removes the earlier background selectors by the source identity (1.29). The next selector is independent of the integration being performed, LFR I after (1.25). At a failed relocation, (1.59) gives the fixed free-coordinate support while retaining the new action terms. A stopped branch therefore retains its actual fixed selector support. No selector is differentiated, and a factor depending on an internal summed label is not called an external scalar.

There are two cautions in applying (OR.26):

1. Conditioning after reconstruction is valid only for the actual requested fixed raw tuple. Fixing a newly reintroduced hidden variable would define a different conditional object.
2. The vacuum and Wilson corrections have to be restored as in (OR.25). Cancelling only \(\log I_{n+1}\), while keeping a mismatched Wilson coefficient or dropping its normalizer, would invalidate the density identity.

SD.6a–SD.6e respects both requirements when interpreted on the specified source branch. The relative action in that unrolled density is the incoming relative action evaluated once. Intermediate current-attempt relative \(C\) terms are not a second independent incoming bank.

\[
 |\sigma_\zeta|\le\delta_{\rm ord,r}+4K_r,

\[

The AF-tail recursion can therefore close without assuming C06, subject to the actual new-root and one-time transport proofs.
