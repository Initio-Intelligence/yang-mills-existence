# Remaining coordinate radius after localization

The bounds involving an actual Wilson boundary derivative require the same chart, conditional law and configuration of fixed earlier innovations as the derivative being estimated. In particular, the all-history application requires the [anchored composed-response estimate](../../continuum/partial_gauge_transport.md); the [two-age estimate](../ordinary_oscillation.md) concerns the identical subtracted quantity at both endpoints. Exact finite-dimensional identities do not depend on these two application bounds.

This note analyses the near-one partial-kernel factor, the source payment for its creation marks, and the zero reference in the higher-slot split.

**Convention.** Section 7 below proves the stronger original-block ownership lemma. By that lemma an arbitrary inherited resummed scalar need not pay one mark for every finer historical coordinate. The coordinate construction of Sections 1–4 remains valid for explicitly introduced current source coordinates, but is not needed for old atoms.

## 1. Two operator constants are different

For the actual ordinary-tilted partial prefix, the phase result gives

\[
 M_{\rm pref}=\sec\eta_*,\qquad
 \eta_*=\sup_{r,\text{admitted branch}}\eta_r\longrightarrow0
\tag{UC1}
\]

as the one common coupling ceiling decreases. This is the constant used for a reserve attached to an individual old free adjoint coordinate.

The source canonical completed operation has a different bound, for example the printed \(e^3\) per used fixed component. Denote it by \(M_{\rm comp}\). Its cost is paid per genuine component incidence by the current guard and the quotient-tour count.

One must not replace \(M_{\rm pref}\) by \(\max(M_{\rm pref},M_{\rm comp})\) in a mark paid per real coordinate. Such a replacement would lose the small \(\log M_{\rm pref}\) needed below.

## 2. Exact contraction in the augmented norm

At the moment a record first acquires dependence on a newly introduced real free adjoint coordinate, attach an unspent mark for that coordinate occurrence. A finite multiset is permitted. Let \(m(F)\) be the number of its still unspent marks, and use the augmented weight

\[
 \|F\|_{\rm aug}=M_{\rm pref}^{m(F)}\|F\|_{\rm spatial}.
\tag{UC2}
\]

Consider \(q\) disjoint partial component operations that act genuinely on the displayed real coordinates of this record. Each such operation integrates at least one marked coordinate. The integration coordinates are disjoint between the components. Remove all marks attached to the coordinates actually eliminated. Thus

\[
 m_{\rm out}+q\le m_{\rm in}.
\tag{UC3}
\]

The norm of the product of the partial operations is at most \(M_{\rm pref}^q\), and

\[
 M_{\rm pref}^{m_{\rm out}}
       M_{\rm pref}^{q}\|F\|_{\rm spatial}
 \le M_{\rm pref}^{m_{\rm in}}\|F\|_{\rm spatial}.
\tag{UC4}
\]

This is an exact one-time contraction. The same underlying real coordinate is never integrated twice; a newly introduced field of the same grade or name has its actual new creation identity. A product of input records retains the multiset of their occurrences. Removing the eliminated occurrences only improves (UC3). The argument does not require a geometric ownership lemma for a large coarse support block.

If a scalar is independent of the integration variables, \(\Pi1=1\) makes its carry exact and no mark is consumed. New dependence on a higher external coordinate through an ordinary tilt is a separate marked \(V_{\rm hi}\) response; it is not called a coordinate-free carry.

At a completed component operation, remove the marks for all coordinates actually consumed there, but pay \(M_{\rm comp}\) by its own component-incidence guard. There is no need to infer that fixed \(e^3\) cost from near-one coordinate marks.

## 3. Paying for the marks at their actual introduction

Fix the source meshes, \(L,M\), and the finite Lie-algebra dimension first. A native source block at the grade where a new \(A_j\) is introduced contains at most a fixed number \(C_{M,L,G}\) of independent real free-bond coordinates. The source's new localization/Gaussian-support volume therefore pays the mark weight if

\[
 C_{M,L,G}\log M_{\rm pref}
       <c_{\rm vol}\kappa_1,
\tag{UC5}
\]

where \(c_{\rm vol}>0\) is a fixed available fraction of its new-volume reserve. This is possible by (UC1), after \(M,L\) have been fixed. No radius or source parameter depends on derivative order or depth.

There is a second source case. A new current variable can enter a map on the exposed part of an old paid carrier \(X\cap\Lambda\), even when the new external localizer is empty. Those coordinates are not charged to nonexistent new volume. The actual Gaussian insertion-support bound is

\[
 \#\{\text{new coordinates}\}
       \le C_{M,L,G}\,[n_{\rm new}+n_{\rm exp}],
\tag{UC6}
\]

with \(n_{\rm exp}\) controlled by the old paid connector, as in the corrected ordinary Gaussian calculation in Section 17.3 of the quotient note. The ordinary step coarsens that connector by \(L\). The exact coarsening reserve S6a pays the additional factor
\(\exp[C_{M,L,G}\log M_{\rm pref}\,n_{\rm exp}]\) after decreasing the ceiling so that the coefficient is smaller than a fixed share of the available \((L-1)\lambda\) reserve. This is a new coarsening gain at that step; it is not a fixed loss repeatedly charged to unchanged old counts.

For a new \(B_j\) retained by a failed preparatory branch, a different fact applies. It lies inside the whole original failed component, and its grade is below the global failure index \(r\). A later containing eligible attempt has lower endpoint \(h'\ge r\), so this coordinate cannot be integrated by a later preparatory prefix. It may eventually be consumed by a whole completed component, whose fixed operator cost has already been assigned to \(M_{\rm comp}\). Such a protected failed coordinate needs no future partial-integration reserve. An immediately integrated successful \(B_j\) also needs no future mark.

Thus the mark payment uses actual new field supports and the already proved exposed-connector coarsening term. It does not assume that every new adjoint dependence has a nonempty new localizer.

## 4. Catalog convention

Marks are attached when an actual source record is created. They remain part of that record's data. Do not subsequently replace a sum of records with different mark sets by one scalar carrying their union without keeping its weighted sum norm: that union can have a larger mark factor than the original sum.

Equivalently, retain the mark multiset as part of the activity label, or keep the corresponding augmented positive norm when some labels are suppressed in notation. Each new map/Gaussian-support record determines its coordinate marks. There is no independent enumeration of arbitrary mark subsets and no new entropy factor.

The original ordinary full-tree bank can be constructed with this same near-one weight: its initial data have no old adjoint marks, new-coordinate costs use (UC5)–(UC6), and old marks are merely transported or consumed. This uses the positive source walk/cluster construction with a fixed smaller available volume/coarsening reserve. It does not claim that an arbitrary already-given bounded function of all historical coordinates automatically satisfies (UC2).

The old resummed scalar remains one atom for future analytic insertion. Retaining the mark label from its birth does not reopen a signed sum over its internal histories.

## 5. Zero is an admitted reference for ordinary scalar coefficients

The source itself uses this operation. CMP119 (3.28)–(3.29), pp. 271–272, multiplies the retained exterior fluctuation \(A_k\) by a real parameter and includes its value at \(A_k=0\). The discussion on p. 276 says that the dependence on this scaled exterior field has already been included in the estimates, and pp. 278–279 derives the final boundary coefficient bounds. LFR I (1.60) likewise subtracts the action with \(A_j=0\) before the partial integration.

These are evaluations of the **scalar coefficient**, not of its actual outer selector. The ordinary estimates use the upper fluctuation ball \(|A_j|\le CP_j\); a lower-annulus selector belonging to a particular external branch remains in the external operation. No indicator is analytically continued or changed when the scalar coefficient is evaluated at zero.

Consequently the higher-slot split may be chosen as

\[
 V_{\rm lo}=V_{\rm ord}\big|_{A_l=0,\ l\ge b},
 \qquad
 V_{\rm hi}=V_{\rm ord}-V_{\rm lo},
\tag{UC7}
\]

after removing the terms independent of the currently integrated coordinates. This is an exact finite difference and requires no derivative in an old real \(A_l\). The source bound applies to both values.

Zero is fixed by every adjoint gauge transformation. Thus (UC7) preserves the scalar covariance of the ordinary coefficient family and leaves no artificial nonzero old gauge nodes after the higher coordinates are eventually integrated. A generic nonzero reference would have to be retained as an additional covariant old parameter, so zero is preferable here.

The normalized interpolation
\(V_{\rm lo}+tV_{\rm hi}\), \(0\le t\le1\), retains the exact covariance derivative of the denominator. Its contribution is the already recorded
\[
 \frac{d}{dt}\Pi_tF
     =\Pi_t(FV_{\rm hi})-\Pi_tF\,\Pi_tV_{\rm hi}.
\tag{UC8}
\]

## 6. Forest and tail-exponent check

The factors in a primitive polymer bound should be written

\[
 M_{\rm comp}^{|I_{\rm comp}|}
       M_{\rm pref}^{|I_{\rm pref}|}.
\tag{UC9}
\]

The second factor is absorbed by (UC4). The first remains in the actual component-incidence reserve. The forest induction then uses the residual small primitive load, not a fresh per-coordinate \(e^3\) factor.

The linear coefficient of
\(\log\Pi_{\rm pref}\exp(\sum z_eF_e)\) is exactly \(\Pi_{\rm pref}F_e\). It transports the same creation budget in the augmented norm and must not be assigned a new cube-root loss. For the degree-at-least-two recent-input part, the Banach-valued Cauchy estimate in Q42 gives
\[
 O(K_{\rm recent}^2/s_r).
\tag{UC10}
\]

With the proposed fixed tail exponent \(\theta=1/18=4/72\) and
\(s_r=\operatorname{poly}(\ell_r)\epsilon_r^{1/72}\), its exponential power is
\[
 2\theta-\frac1{72}=\frac7{72}>\theta.
\tag{UC11}
\]

All fixed polynomial stage/root costs fit the strict remaining exponent. The two completed-operation extractions act only on a newly supplied current collar. This prevents repeated weakening of a previously created tail.

These conclusions verify the new mark and degree bookkeeping at the actual source-family scope. They still require the ordinary-tilted phase estimate (UC1) on the common contour and the positive new-record load Q33; neither follows from the mark algebra alone.

## 7. One mark per original source block is sufficient

Let an old input atom have an original active source block of grade \(b\), present before the ordinary step of an attempt at grade \(r\). Thus \(b\le r-1\). A directly occurring old adjoint slot has grade \(j<b\). Suppose the preparatory prefix in an attempted component \(P\) integrates such a slot. Its endpoint lies in
\[
 Z_{j+1}\subset Z_b\subset Z_{r-1}.
 \tag{UC12}
\]
The current ordinary compatible cover and its four \(MR_r\) layers put this endpoint at distance at least \(cMR_rh_r\) from \(P^c\). The diameter of the entire original source block, with fixed endpoint stencils, is at most \(CMh_b\le CMh_r/L\). At the common large lower cutoff on \(R_r\), the whole block is therefore inside \(P\). Merely knowing that a coarse block intersects \(P\) would not suffice; (UC12) is the essential deep-endpoint fact.

Different simultaneous attempted components cannot use different parts of this same block. After its first partial use there are exactly two cases.

1. **Retained failure.** The whole original \(P\) remains in the large-field history, including its outer failure index \(r\). Any later containing eligible prefix starts at \(h'\ge r\). Every old slot of the block has grade below \(r\), so it is then preserved rather than partially integrated. Newly acquired local raw support in \(D(P)\) has the same protection; remote new raw dependence is a separate guarded density-response record.
2. **Complete R integration.** The remaining source operations integrate all the old adjoint slots in \(P\), not just those used by the successful preliminary prefix. CMP119 (2.20)–(2.22) gives the complete ordered product. LFR I (1.71)–(1.72) leaves the residual \(T_h\) together with the complementary prepared integrations, and (1.100) retains them. LFR II p.378 defines \(T'_r(P)\) by composing precisely these remaining integrations. Finally (1.103)–(1.104), pp.391–392, states that no integral operation remains in a completed retained component: its multiplier has only the new raw \(V_r\) arguments. Thus no old \(A_j\) in the owned block survives to require a second partial mark.

One mark for an original active block occurrence therefore pays one factor \(M_{\rm pref}\), regardless of how many finer historical coordinates that block contains. If \(q\) partial operations genuinely use a record, their distinct owned block occurrences give
\[
 u_{\rm out}+q\le u_{\rm in},
 \qquad
 M_{\rm pref}^{u_{\rm out}}M_{\rm pref}^{q}
 \le M_{\rm pref}^{u_{\rm in}}.
 \tag{UC13}
\]
The relevant multiset is part of the creation record. Addition of records does not replace their different multisets by a free union. Products can retain occurrences.

The mark count for an ordinary source atom is at most its original active block count. The full-tree exponent and source count reserve therefore pay \(u\log M_{\rm pref}\) once. For relative atoms use their inherited original-block marks and their genuine newly created map support. A fine point in a completed core or a purely decorative halo does not create an extra mark. This argument needs no \(L^{4(b-j)}\) budget.

The statement is about old input records. A current-born \(A_{r-1}\), or a field introduced and used inside the same attempted prefix, stays in its explicit new source/map record until the whole prefix is finished. Such a field is covered by its actual creation support or by the whole-prefix operation, not by an artificial application of (UC12).

The original-block mark pays the normalized **operator** factor. New protected-core tags, physical decoration diameters and migration of a root into \(D(P)\) still require the geometric payment. An outside-terminal record releases its paid interior guard segment when \(P\) is made a free core. A wholly contained record has one unguarded event, whose fixed window polynomial is reserved once at its original creation. At a fixed new root the original source atoms are summed on their actual integrated strips; the bound is a single \(\sum_j D_j\) window polynomial. It is not iterated as a new polynomial loss each time the record moves.

Successful components which disappear are handled separately. Their exact \(T'_r(P)1\) mass pays the restored interior and produces a genuinely new creation record. A retained-failure connector release is not reused across a successful removal. The constant \(M_{\rm comp}\) remains separate from \(M_{\rm pref}\) throughout.
