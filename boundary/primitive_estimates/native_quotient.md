# Norm of a quotient with its actual normalizer

The bounds involving an actual Wilson boundary derivative require the same chart, conditional law and configuration of fixed earlier innovations as the derivative being estimated. In particular, the all-history application requires the [anchored composed-response estimate](../../continuum/partial_gauge_transport.md); the [two-age estimate](../ordinary_oscillation.md) concerns the identical subtracted quantity at both endpoints. Exact finite-dimensional identities do not depend on these two application bounds.

This note analyses the quotient and summable-reserve route. It separates identities of the actual real source family from identities of a chosen native extension. In particular it does not cancel two different off-physical extensions because they agree at physical fields.

**Choice of argument:** Section 8 is used in place of the enlargement of the exterior native branch considered in Sections 2 and 4. The proof keeps the literal source interior/self extension. A current collar pays its possible projection difference; no extension reset is made before a subsequent canonical self extraction.

## 1. The reserve calculation itself is sound

Equations S1–S5 of **Summable reserves in the native norm** give the needed summability from the explicit retained CMP109 (0.31) two-sided envelope. The proof does not need a positive one-step decrement. The algebra S7–S10 then pays a factor
\[
 \exp\{D\epsilon_k[n(G)+q(G)+1]\}
\]
by summable reductions of the auxiliary count weights and a uniformly bounded product of scalar factors. This is a valid sufficient norm mechanism once the literal attachment estimate S6 is proved. It does not establish S6 by itself.

The connector reserve S12 also has the correct direction: the old physical graph is unchanged, so its length in the new units is \(L^{-(k-b)}\ell_b\), with coefficient one. A fresh factor \(\exp\{d_k\ell_b\}\), with \(d_k\) uniformly small, can be paid without requiring \(\sum_k d_k<\infty\). This is appropriate for ordinary local attachments along the already paid part of a long old graph. Unpaid component or footprint counts still require the summable \(\epsilon_k\) part.

## 2. The four exterior identities really apply outside the deep set

LFR II p.370 explicitly states that the old and new determining sets, and their data, coincide on \(\Lambda^c\). Formula (1.50) lists the altered data inside \(\Lambda\). The later choice to apply (1.51), (1.54), (1.57), and (1.58) first to \(X\subset Z^c\) is a convenient partition of the action; it is not the pointwise domain on which the field identities are valid.

More precisely:

* (1.51) is the exact response to \(B\), supported in \(\Lambda\).
* \(U_{1,2}=U_1\) outside \((\Omega''_{h+1})^c\), by (1.53), and that exceptional region is inside the deep set.
* In (1.54), the determining-data forcing is in the deep region. Its apparently nonlocalized current forcing must first be rewritten by (1.55).
* The forcing in (1.57) is in the deep old-data region.
* The forcing in (1.58) is \(V'_k\), supported in \(\Lambda\).

Thus a full old dependency collar \(X^+\) disjoint from the chosen deep enlargement \(K_H\) is on the exterior side of all four identities, even if \(X^+\) meets the outer part of \(H\). The old action's current-born forcing and changes of the current \(\Lambda_k\) on all of \(H\) must still be assigned to the separate current-born sector. They are not old deep-history changes.

### 2.1 The criticality step in (1.55)

At p.370, (1.53) defines
\[
 U_1=U_{\mathfrak B_1}(\text{the displayed mixed determining data}).
\]
The \(J_1\) used in the subsequent criticality argument is the action current of this constructed minimum. It is not the independent external current coordinate \(J\). For every admitted determining datum \(D\), the constrained Euler–Lagrange identity reads
\[
 \langle v,J(U_{\mathfrak B_1}(D))\rangle=0
 \quad\text{if }Q_{\mathfrak B_1}v=0.
 \tag{A1}
\]
This identity holds throughout the admitted complex determining-data chart, by the analytic minimizing equations (or their holomorphic continuation from the real determining data). It therefore remains true after a holomorphic substitution \(D=D(U,J,s)\). No criticality of an arbitrary external background pair \((U,J)\) is asserted.

The identity \(G''Q_{\mathfrak B''}^*=0\) is an algebraic constrained-inverse identity. On the region where the determining conditions coincide, (A1) therefore gives the printed repair
\[
 G''J_{1,2}
 =G''(1-\theta)Q^*\theta_1H^*J_1
       +G''\theta J_{1,2}.
 \tag{A2}
\]
Both terms on the right have forcing at the deep boundary or in the deep region. Apply localization to this supported expression **after** the identity has been established. A decoupled operator \(G''(s)\) need not satisfy the original exact constraint identity, and no such assertion is used.

There is an important distinction for the native extension. The argument above verifies (A2) on the composed literal-minimum family. Alternatively, the source exterior construction can first perform (A2) in the exact real expressions and then extend its supported right-hand side, as its localization proof does. What is not valid is to substitute an arbitrary independent current for \(J_1\) in the unrepaired left side and infer (A2) from equality only on the physical-current graph.

## 4. The zero-reference identity must use one construction

There are two different representations in play.

The **unlocalized exterior** final identity (1.58) has the multiplicative form
\[
 U_{k,\Lambda}
   =\bigl(\exp[i\eta H_k(\log V'_k)]\,U_k\bigr)^{u_k^{-1}} .
 \tag{A5}
\]
The source relative map and gauge have value \(0\) and \(1\), respectively, at zero relative input. Its final reference is therefore literally the displayed \(U_k\). In the source exterior native construction the reference pair is the native pair \((U,J)\); the formal current update likewise has zero increment at zero field increment.

By contrast, a **localized interior** representation such as (1.63) contains a literal minimizing projection \(U(\mathfrak B_k(Y_3),M(U))\). For arbitrary off-physical native \(U\), it is not generally equal to \(U\). Thus one must not telescope with the interior projection and then cancel its reference value against an independently extended \(F(U,J)\).

The valid source order is:

1. At the exact real level, apply the exterior four-map identities and telescope to the displayed new background \(U_k\).
2. Pair common old/new reference coefficient records at this same reference. Equal local records cancel before absolute values or separate native extension.
3. Keep the actual new action with its chosen source extension \(F_{\rm new}(U,J)\).
4. Extend only the remaining supported differences by the source exterior construction of pp.372–373, on the contour in Section 3.

One can make the selected family explicit. Put \(\Phi_0(U,J)=(U,J)\), and form \(\Phi_1,\ldots,\Phi_4\) successively by the four exterior relative-field and gauge formulas, with the current forcing in the second formula already in the supported form (A2). For a relative field increment \(A\) at a pair \((V,J_V)\), use the usual independent-current update
\[
 \mathcal J(V,J_V;A)
   =J_V+
       J_{\rm phys}(e^{i\eta A}V)-J_{\rm phys}(V),
 \tag{A5a}
\]
followed by the displayed gauge conjugation. This is the triangular physical-current-plus-independent-deviation convention: it equals the physical transformed current on the physical graph and is exactly \(J_V\) at \(A=0\). The defining local difference in (A5a) is holomorphic on the source chart and is controlled by the field, gradient and covariant-current output bounds already retained there.

The selected extension of the paired old/new correction is
\[
 \sum_{\ell=1}^{4}
   \bigl[F_{\rm old}(\Phi_\ell)-F_{\rm old}(\Phi_{\ell-1})\bigr]
       +F_{\rm old}(\Phi_0)-F_{\rm new}(\Phi_0).
 \tag{A5b}
\]
On every admitted real current family it is the exact source correction. For a record whose full defining data miss \(K_H\), \(F_{\rm old}\) and \(F_{\rm new}\) are the identical local analytic function, so the last difference vanishes as an identity, not merely on a physical graph. Otherwise it is a changed-record contribution with its actual deep marker. In all cases the zero reference in (A5b) is the unchanged pair \((U,J)\).

The supported formula (A2) is used as part of the definition before the independent-current extension. It is not recombined afterward into an unrepaired \(G''J_{1,2}\) with an arbitrary auxiliary current. This is precisely what lets the source supported exterior construction retain both its deep forcing and its explicit zero reference. Its contour admission is the reduced-decoupling replay in Section 3.

This does not require equality of the exterior and interior off-physical extensions. It uses the actual source exterior extension on the larger geometric range where its proof applies. It introduces no extra term \(F\circ\Phi_{\rm ref}-F\), and no repeated unsuppressed projection correction.

If instead one elects to carry the literal interior composed reference, the unchanged activity must be carried as that same composition, and the corresponding chart inclusion must give norm factor one. It is not permissible to switch between the two reference conventions later without retaining and estimating their difference. The exterior convention above avoids that switch.

The arbitrary fixed hidden evaluation \(E_H\) in Q12 is a different operation. Apply it **after** the exact source telescope and local-record pairing. It need not equal the source zero-forcing reference. Evaluating a supported response at its fixed hidden point leaves its deep marker and its bound intact; it is not discarded as an unchanged reference term.

## 5. Old/new equality includes the entire normalized kernel

A prior scalar record must retain the full dependency footprint of every normalized kernel used to define it: numerator, self action, selectors, internal history catalog and denominator. If that entire footprint misses \(K_H\), the old and new local defining data coincide and so do the complete operators and masses. Their normalized scalar coefficients then agree at the common reference.

Q16 has the right geometric mechanism. A birth-\(m\) footprint has only one halo layer and fixed local stencils beyond its actual core. Their total thickness is at most \(2MR_mh_m\) after the lower cutoff on \(R_m\). The next ordinary step surrounds the compatible cover of the whole old large-field region by four \(MR_{m+1}h_{m+1}\) layers. The source rounding gives \(R_mh_m\le R_{m+1}h_{m+1}\). Thus the complete old footprint lies in the same next external component as its core. Ordinary growth preserves it, and only a whole-component completion can consume it.

An exterior old core therefore cannot have its kernel halo reach the deep region of an attempted component. A resident old core has birth at most \(h=k-N\). The preserved nested inner region \(Z_h\subset Z''_{h+1}\subset\cdots\), together with the ordinary eight-layer guard minus the five-layer preparation enlargement and at most two halo layers, leaves a positive guard around the whole resident footprint. This is the source distinction between a genuine deep dependency and a fake decorative incidence.

No numerator-only comparison is used. If the footprint changes, the mass response belongs to the same changed dependency record. If it does not, both numerator and denominator are exactly identical.

## 6. Paying missing joins without spending an old rate again

Suppose a new source-localizer component \(W\) meets a preceding dependency footprint at \(x\), contains a deep forcing component, and must be joined to the already recorded graph. If \(x\) is in an active old cell, the join costs at most a fixed cell diameter. If \(x\) is in an old free core, it is free in the old quotient. The remaining case is \(x\in D(H_m)\setminus H_m\).

Join \(x\) to the old core by a path of length at most \(C MR_mh_m\), then use the old zero-cost core. If \(H_m\) is resident in an attempted current component, \(m\le h=k-N\), so its physical halo is at most one fixed current cell for the source choice of \(N\). That bounded join is paid by the nonempty new domain.

If \(H_m\) is exterior, Q16 puts its entire footprint outside the attempted component containing the deep forcing endpoint of \(W\). Thus \(W\) crosses the current guard and contains at least \(cR_k\) current \(M\)-cells. The AF physical-scale comparison gives \(MR_mh_m\le CMR_k\). Therefore
\[
 \text{missing join length in current \(M\)-units}
       \le C |W|/M^4.
 \tag{A6}
\]
A sufficiently small fixed output connector rate pays this from the new domain's \(\kappa_1\) volume reserve. No constant greater than one multiplies an old connector length. This is the needed quantitative justification for the joining clause in Q9.

## 8. Final literal-branch analysis: the interior projection difference is guarded

The field identities on \(\Lambda^c\) are exact at physical source configurations. They do not identify two different off-physical localizations of an inherited scalar. In particular replacing its interior projection extension by an exterior zero-reference extension could change the next localized self/nonself split, even if their unlocalized physical values agree. The final argument avoids that replacement.

Keep the source's actual interior branch and its literal projection
\(U(\mathfrak B,M(U))\). An all-empty projection response can be bounded by \(2\|F\|\), rather than being asserted to vanish. For every actual old-input nonself record one of the following source alternatives pays the needed current collar.

**Resident owner.** If its old completed owner lies in the current attempted component \(P\), eligibility places the whole old owner footprint in the preserved deep region. If an old dependency terminal leaves the source self domain \(P^\sim\), the old paid connector crosses the current outer shell. If every old dependency is inside that self domain and only a genuinely new localization component \(W\) makes the output nonself, that component joins the deep forcing to the outside and retains its own volume/response cost. If no old terminal or new piece leaves the self domain, the record belongs to the literal self action and is not estimated as a new nonself activity.

**Exterior owner.** Let the true old owner be in another current large-field component \(Q\), and suppose the actual paid old dependency reaches \(P\). The core lies in \(Z_{r-1}\). The current compatible cover plus its four \(MR_r\) layers around this entire old region lies in its current component \(Q\), which is disjoint from \(P\). Every path from the owner's actual footprint to a contact in \(P\) crosses this shell. Old free cores are subsets of \(Z_{r-1}\), so none is a zero-cost passage through the shell. The owner halo takes at most two proper-scale layers; the fixed AF comparison and finite stencils leave a strictly positive current-width share, as in (CE18).

Consequently the paid old length in its actual transport units obeys
\[
 L_{\rm paid}^{(b)}\ge cR_rL^{r-b}.
 \tag{A7}
\]
The input grade \(b\) in (A7) is the actual transport/root grade, not an earlier tail-budget label of a migrated family. This cost pays the complete \(2\|F\|\) projection bound, the finer-root density and every fixed current root-completion polynomial. If the apparent contact is only an old canonical kernel decoration, Section 5 shows that the whole decoration remains in \(Q\); it cannot reach \(P\). A fixed active-cell/stencil enlargement subtracts only a fixed mesh allowance from (A7).

These alternatives do not require an outer \(K_1\) difference in the all-empty interior branch. Nonempty branches retain the already verified \(K_1\) and source-localizer exponents as well. The four-map supported zero reference is used only on the source's original exterior branch. There the exact source telescope and its current \(J_1\) convention are unchanged.

Thus the canonical physical and native self catalog, its masses and its operator bound remain the literal ones selected by the source. No compensating scalar is discarded, no off-physical projection is identified with the identity, and no future source operation is fed an unrecorded replacement extension. The old all-empty interior term is controlled by a real physical collar which is absent only in the genuine self class.
