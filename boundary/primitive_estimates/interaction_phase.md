# Correction and scope

The ordinary \(E\) in the actual retained prefix has the running native domains of CMP119 (2.27)–(2.28). It must not be called an activity on a fixed absolute native radius merely because CMP109 (1.18) gives a coefficient bound in its earlier notation.

The required phase estimate uses the **complete renormalized \(E\) fluctuation differences** on a once-selected larger ordinary profile. Their native domain, own Wilson subtraction and old-grade summation are retained. The counting argument below shows that the logarithmic degree paid by the last profile enlargement is independent of the chosen enlargement exponent. This corrects the cited source of the phase reserve; it does not alter the source density, canonical masses or declared AF/C01 history scope.

The actual simultaneous native-domain statement is supplied by the retained E14.49–E14.51 and source CMP119 pp.275–277, transferred through the genuine PF external strong core. The companion source analysis checks that these statements may be run with the once-selected \(++\) ordinary profile. No bound for an arbitrary completed relative family on a fourth chart is used.

## 1. Where the E terms occur in the joint prefix density

Let \(P\) be one attempted component, \(r\) the outer scale and \(h=r-N_r\). At the \(n+1\)st successful preparation integration, LFR I, pp.188–189, (1.60), writes the transformed action evaluated at the new background outside the fluctuation integral. It is explicitly described as the analogue of CMP119 (3.15)/(3.25), with the extra real \(A_j\) integration.

For the \(E\) part, this gives the exact identity
\[
 E_k(U^{[n]}(\xi,a))=
 E_k(U^{[n+1]}(\xi,a_{>n}))
 +\Delta_nE_k(\xi,a_{\ge n}),
 \tag{EP.1}
\]
where
\[
 \Delta_nE_k
 =E_k(U^{[n]}(\xi,a))-E_k(U^{[n+1]}(\xi,a_{>n})).
 \tag{EP.2}
\]
Here \(a\) collects the actual integrated real free \(A_j,B_j\) variables, with their exact constraint/relocation maps. The notation records order but introduces no independent variables or new action. \(E_k\) is the fully vacuum/Wilson-subtracted source \(E\) bank.

A prefix changes no homogeneous \(E\) birth: LFR I p.189 says that its new integrations contribute to the boundary sector and denotes their accumulated output by the \(C_r^{(i)}\) bank. Thus (EP.1) telescopes through the retained prefix. For a prefix ending after \(s\) stages,
\[
 E_k(U^{[0]}(\xi,a))=
 E_k(U^{[s]}(\xi))
 +\sum_{n=0}^{s-1}\Delta_nE_k(\xi,a).
 \tag{EP.3}
\]
All variables not integrated by that prefix are fixed in this formula. The last background in (EP.3) is independent of the coordinates integrated by the prefix. Its scalar may therefore be factored out of the **joint** normalized prefix functional. It remains in the outer action; it has not been discarded from the exact density.

Consequently the oscillating phase of the normalized ordinary prefix law only needs the sum of the differences (EP.2), together with the other already listed Gaussian/classical/B/C terms. It does not require an extensive bound for the unsubtracted \(E\) action over every older native cell.

## 2. Keep the own Wilson subtraction inside each fluctuation difference

Write the source bank schematically as
\[
 E_k=\sum_{b\le k}\{H_b-H_b(1)-\beta_b A_b\},
 \qquad \beta_b=\mathcal P_bH_b,
 \tag{EP.4}
\]
with its literal source localization and cutoff functions, CMP119 (2.25)–(2.27). Equation (EP.2) uses the difference of this whole expression. The vacuum constant cancels. The quadratic curvature part of \(H_b\) cancels against its **own** \(\beta_b A_b\) before the old-grade root sum.

CMP116 Lemma 1, (1.33)–(1.36), constructs precisely the localized renormalized fluctuation difference; CMP119 pp.275–277 carries its Ward operations and localization to the proper-scale geometry. The earlier estimates (1.24)–(1.32) sum old carriers and old grades before the final current-scale family is bounded. CMP119 (3.49)–(3.67) displays the matching curvature cancellation in the actual running-domain setting. E14.37–E14.51 supplies the explicit native contour and convergent old-age majorant.

It would be incorrect to estimate every \(H_b\) and \(\beta_b A_b\) separately by a constant per old cell and then sum all old cells. It would also be incorrect to assume \(\beta_b=O(1)\) on a complex synthetic or enlarged native family. Its crude projection bound can be \(C E_0/r_b^2\). The source cancellation and the two physical curvature powers are the reason this inverse radius does not reappear as an uncontrolled phase cost.

The new Wilson-action corrections from a preparatory rewrite remain at their prescribed locations in the explicit classical/coupling-difference sector or the newly created \(C\) bank. They are not inserted a second time into (EP.4).

## 3. The actual rooted bound on the ++ profile

Choose the fixed ordinary profile
\[
 \alpha_b^{++}=g_b\ell_b^{q+Q_1+Q_2},
 \quad
 \alpha_b^+=g_b\ell_b^{q+Q_1},
 \qquad \ell_b=\log g_b^{-2}.
 \tag{EP.5}
\]
All fixed source constants are included. The \(++\) profile is selected before the coupling ceiling and then kept for the whole calculation.

Let \(j\) be the true integration scale of a stage. The inherited bank in a prefix is the whole outer \(E_r\), including births larger than \(j\). LFR I p.182, after (1.24), explicitly says the action changes only by new boundary terms. Keep the proper support grade \(m\) in the mixed localization preceding (1.63), with \(j+1\le m\le r\) (and a harmless fixed endpoint convention). On the genuine external \(++\) core, with the real free current coordinates fixed, write its localized difference as
\[
 \Delta_jE_r=\sum_{m=j+1}^{r}\sum_Y V^E_{j,m}(Y;\xi,a).
 \tag{EP.6}
\]
The source/E14 renormalized-family estimate gives
\[
 |V^E_{j,m}(Y;\xi,a)|
 \le C_{\rm PF}\mathfrak K_E^{++}
                         e^{-\kappa_Ed_m(Y)} .
 \tag{EP.7}
\]
Here \(\mathfrak K_E^{++}\) is a fixed source constant for the chosen ordinary profiles. \(C_{\rm PF}\) allows the actual top-band/composed-prefix factor; it is bounded by a fixed polynomial in \(R_r,N_r\). Terms crossing native strata retain their actual proper-scale roots. The boundary-assigned part remains in its actual ordinary boundary ledger and is estimated by the corresponding source subfamily bound, not by assuming that its support is contained in the regular interior.

There is no claim that the activity is bounded on an arbitrary strong ball inferred from a weaker modified output predicate. The function in (EP.6) is the **full composed source coefficient** on the external strong core, with its actual independent/reconstructed current. This is the same external-core convention used for the ordinary B/C phase bound.

The support grading is important here. An original \(E^{(b)}\) carrier and its matched Wilson cutoff have support in \(\Lambda_b\subset\Omega_b\). Preparatory regularization does not remove that regularity inclusion. The source localization retains the old support, so a resulting carrier's maximal regularity grade cannot be smaller than \(b\). Thus every primitive assigned to the proper layer \(m\) has \(b\le m\), even when \(b>j\). The proper-layer Ward argument is made on its actual native patches, followed by the source mixed-layer resummation; it is not an assertion that the whole carrier has one homogeneous mesh. CMP119 p.276 supplies this actual scale replacement. LFR I p.191, immediately before (1.70), expressly bounds the expressions in the exponential of (1.60) after renormalization and localization, with only the accumulated new-boundary bank requiring its separate discussion. Hence the estimate being used is pre-integration and includes the whole inherited \(E_r\); it is not inferred from the final bound for the integrated \(C\) coefficient alone.

The constants in (EP.7) may depend on \(Q_2\). That dependence is fixed once. They do not introduce a new running power \(\ell_r^{cQ_2}\). The reason can be checked before the old-age sum. If \(b\le m\), the retained AF profile gives
\[
 \frac{g_m}{g_b}\le C(1+m-b)^{1/2},
 \qquad
 \ell_b\ge\ell_m-C,
\]
and hence
\[
 \frac{\alpha_m^+}{\alpha_b^{++}}
 \le C_{Q_2}(1+m-b)^{1/2}\ell_m^{-Q_2}.
 \tag{EP.8}
\]
The same-profile comparison has the same fixed half-power and no \(\ell_m^{-Q_2}\). Taking the common ceiling after the exponents have been chosen makes the fixed logarithmic-ratio constants harmless. Replacing this estimate by
\((1+m-b)^{q+Q_1+Q_2}\) would create a fictitious dependence of the summation degree on \(Q_2\).

The own marginal subtraction is made before the root density is summed. On a proper patch the remaining near-age terms have the fixed polynomial times \(L^{-(1-\vartheta)(m-b)}\) of E14.37–E14.38, with that patch's grade in place of \(m\) before resummation; the far terms have the corresponding fixed \(\sqrt{1+m-b}\,L^{3(m-b)}e^{-cML^{m-b}}\) majorant of E14.34–E14.35. Their sums are finite with a polynomial degree independent of \(Q_2\). This is the actual running-profile substitute for the incorrect fixed-absolute-E-radius wording.

## 4. Count only actual integration roots and actual stages

At grade \(j\) the source eligible rectangular chain gives
\[
 D_j\le C(LMR_{j+1})^4
 \tag{EP.9}
\]
actual free coordinates in the strip, including the fixed bond and Lie-algebra multiplicity. The new perturbing fluctuation in (EP.2) is supported on this strip. Every nonzero localized fluctuation record has an actual strip endpoint (or the corresponding true kernel root already included by the source localization). Root it there and then in its containing \(m\)-cell. Since \(m>j\), each \(j\)-cell meets only a fixed number of \(m\)-cells; no fine subdivision of an \(m\)-atom is made. The rooted full-tree sum in (EP.7) yields, for each actual pair \((j,m)\),
\[
 \sum_{Y\ {\rm used\ at}\ (j,m)}
     \sup_{\xi\in\mathcal D^{++}}
                 |V_{j,m}^E(Y;\xi,a)|
 \le C\,\mathfrak K_E^{++}\,C_{\rm PF}\,D_j .
 \tag{EP.10}
\]
The old-grade/carrier sums inside \(V_j^E\) have already been performed. No additional factor \(L^{4(r-j)}\), count of all older \(E\) cells, or number of old births is inserted.

For \(h\le j<r\), the signed-prefix profile gives \(R_j\le C R_r\) after a fixed constant/ceiling choice. Thus
\[
 \sum_{j=h}^{r-1}D_j
 \le C N_r R_r^4.
 \tag{EP.11}
\]
The actual \((j,m)\) list has at most \(C N_r^2\) entries. Combining this with the bounded strip counts gives at most \(C N_r^2R_r^4\) before the fixed top-band factor. The top overlap band obeys
\(L_0^{C N_0}\le C R_r^{C'}\), with fixed exponents from the map/stencil calculation. These costs depend on the previously selected real-cutoff/preparation exponents, not on \(Q_2\).

In particular there is a fixed \(D_E\), chosen before \(Q_2\), for which
\[
 \sum_{j,m}\sum_Y\sup_{\mathcal D^{++}}|V_{j,m}^E(Y;\xi,a)|
 \le C(Q_2)\mathfrak K_E^{++}\ell_r^{D_E}.
 \tag{EP.12}
\]
This is a bound on the complete scalar kernel's actual E fluctuation terms with the integrated real variables fixed. It is not an estimate of the full volume of a coarse enclosing component on every older fine grid.

## 5. Phase gain and its common-domain version

At real external native input \(\xi_0\), real integrated variables, and real localization parameters, each complete source coefficient in (EP.6) is real. Its external \(+\) input is at relative distance at most
\(C\ell_r^{D_{\rm map}-Q_2}\)
inside its genuine \(++\) affine margin. Apply one-variable Cauchy to the full composed coefficient, not to an isolated primitive evaluated at an unverified modified output point. Equations (EP.10)–(EP.12) give
\[
 \left|
 \Im\sum_j\Delta_jE_k(\xi,a)
 \right|
 \le C(Q_2)\mathfrak K_E^{++}
                  \ell_r^{D_E+D_{\rm map}-Q_2}.
 \tag{EP.13}
\]
Choose \(Q_2>D_E+D_{\rm map}+2\).

The full source localization disks are treated by the existing enlarged excluded collar \(D(P)\). Their difference from the real/unlocalized family has its actual guarded walk/tree record and a fixed inverse-coupling/logarithmic prefactor times \(e^{-cR_r}\). This separate remote estimate remains as in the main phase proof. Enlarging the joint native/localizer domain by one fixed factor for common positive domination only changes constants.

Adding the actual precision/Schur/coarea and constrained-classical estimates and the B/C profile estimates therefore gives the same phase cone used in the C05 proof:
\[
 \eta_r
 \le Cg_r\ell_r^{D_1}
   +C\ell_r^{D_2-Q_2}
   +Cg_r^{-u}\ell_r^{D_3}e^{-cR_r},
 \tag{EP.14}
\]
with \(D_2=D_E+D_{\rm map}\) enlarged by the independently counted B/C degrees. \(D_2\) is fixed before choosing \(Q_2\). Ordinary large real action values remain permissible.

The resulting normalized operator and fixed positive domination continue to include the complete ordinary interaction, all actual old Gaussian coefficients, and the correct moving mass. This correction does not replace that law by a Gaussian law.
