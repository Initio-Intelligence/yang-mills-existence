# Boundary estimates on a stopped fiber

The current score and Hessian bounds require a uniform estimate on the composed response with the earlier innovations fixed. Under that hypothesis the two-age interpolation controls the ordinary density, and the complete estimates yield conditional coercivity and covariance.

The fixed-exterior anchored score is derived in [Gauge covariance of partial integrals and exterior scores](../continuum/partial_gauge_transport.md). Its implication for the boundary response is explained in [Conditional-law estimates and the continuum application](../continuum/conditional_to_continuum.md).
