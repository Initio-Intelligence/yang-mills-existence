## 3. Why the positive map catalog is more than an operator norm

The source walk expansions have absolute summable walk-label weights
and exponential spatial factors. Expand only the new local analytic
nonlinearities in Banach multilinear norms on the actual blocks. Their
coefficient bounds are geometric after one fixed Cauchy/polarization
allowance. Because the source nonlinear equations start quadratically
in small forcing, their absolute Picard majorants have a small
convergent solution after the common field ceiling is fixed.

Marking one input and one output in this positive series leaves a unique
tree path between them. The side branches sum by the same small
majorant. The marked-path resolvent has a fixed finite bound, retaining
a positive share of the walk exponent. This proves a positive
two-endpoint catalog for the new maps. It does not reopen the canceled
internal expansion of an old scalar.

A protected native footprint is one whole Banach block, with its actual
native supremum norm. It is not a list of fine-coordinate Taylor
variables. Exposed paid blocks and genuine new map-support blocks are
the other patches. This distinction is necessary both for (IR.6) and
for the pointed Taylor estimates RL.12–RL.15.

 d_r(x,P)\le C_0\ell_r(G)+C_1R_r+C_2.                     \tag{IR.8}

## 5. Local nonlinear coefficients, repeated inputs and raw tuples

For a genuinely local action \(V_P=\sum_G V_{P,G}\), use the ordinary
local reference \(\Pi_P^0\), dominated by a single positive probability
with constant \(M\le M_*\). Suppose

\[
 \sup_a\sum_G W_G\sup_\xi|V_{P,G}(\xi,a)|\le K_P.
                                                               \tag{IR.11}
\]

At order \(q\), retain the full cumulant with its ordered input
occurrences and its \(1/q!\) factor:

\[
 R_{P;G_1,\ldots,G_q}
 =\frac1{q!}\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal P([q])}
 (-1)^{|\pi|-1}(|\pi|-1)!
 \prod_{B\in\pi}\Pi_P^0\prod_{i\in B}V_{P,G_i}.             \tag{IR.12}
\]

For each partition block use one independent copy of the same positive
reference. At that copy all factors are evaluated at one common raw
tuple. Tonelli and (IR.11) give the positive tuple-catalog bound

\[
 \sum_{G_1,\ldots,G_q}\prod_i W_{G_i}
 \sup_\xi |R_{P;G_1,\ldots,G_q}|
 \le \frac{B_q(M)}{q!}K_P^q,\qquad
 B_q(M)=\sum_{\pi}(|\pi|-1)!M^{|\pi|}.                    \tag{IR.13}
\]

The exact generating function is

\[
 \sum_{q\ge1}\frac{B_q(M)}{q!}z^q
 =-\log[1-M(e^z-1)].                                    \tag{IR.14}
\]

Its order-at-least-two part is \(O_{M_*}(z^2)\) on a fixed small
interval. Consequently the local nonlinear **positive catalog** is
\(O(K_P^2)\). The argument is stronger than a bound on the single
signed scalar \(\log\Pi_P^0 e^{V_P}\).

Repeated input labels cause no exception: the ordered tuple divided by
\(q!\) gives precisely the usual repeated-label factorials, while the
resource marks remain a multiset of occurrences. Distinct partition
blocks use independent integration copies, not independent suprema
over the raw arguments of each atom.

For different \(P\), choose one global admissible raw anchor
\(a^*=(a_P^*)_P\). A local atom has all its actual integrated slots in
one \(P\), including its new map factors. A mixed-component atom remains
remote even if an anchor substitution makes a displayed term
independent of a slot. This convention prevents the illegitimate
exchange
\(\sup_a\sum_G\longrightarrow\sum_G\sup_a\).

The output support is the union of every old carrier and fixed new map
path with \(D(P)\). Long old exterior carriers are retained, rather than
being replaced by a ball. The linear term plus its extracted anchor is
exactly the ordinary transport of the original scalar. It uses the
original one-time integration marks; it is not a new nonlinear creation.

The enlarged count decrement remains summable because

## 7. Failed prefixes and the same-operation successful branch

For a **failed prefix**, each nonzero local cumulant has a genuine

record even if it is no longer an external argument of the coefficient.

For a **successful prefix which proceeds to a same-operation \(R\)**,

operation. The normalizer telescoping in SD.6a–SD.6e is done on the same
raw variables and source maps. Perform the source self/nonself
projection before installing any failed-prefix complete-cumulant atom.

The source classification on LFR II pp.376–377 has the necessary
exhaustion:

- If an inherited owner is exterior, its genuine paid connector exits
  that owner's ordinary buffer before it can reach the current

- If an owner is resident and its old dependency exits the self region,

The relative nonself positive estimate is made on these original
source-resolved records, retaining the selected old or new guard

already canceled whole cumulant and then bounding the projected pieces
by that cumulant's norm. Old protected blocks entering this operation

Purely ordinary forcing has the independent source full-tree bound.

## 8. Consequences for differentiation

The proof above uses scalar holomorphy and literal normalized
integration. It does not replace the interacting law by a Gaussian.
For a fixed-support kernel \(\Pi_z\), with
\(S_i=\partial_i\log p_z\) and
\(T_{ij}=\partial_{ij}\log p_z\), its exact second derivative is

\[
 \partial_{ij}\Pi_zF
 =\Pi_zF_{ij}
 +\operatorname{Cov}(F_i,S_j)
 +\operatorname{Cov}(F_j,S_i)
 +\operatorname{Cov}(F,T_{ij})
 +\kappa_3(F,S_i,S_j).                                   \tag{IR.21}
\]

For the mass,

\[
 \partial_{ij}\log\!\int p_z
 =\Pi_zT_{ij}+\operatorname{Cov}(S_i,S_j).                 \tag{IR.22}
\]

These identities remain the correct direct tests. The moving-normalizer
terms in them are represented exactly by the normalized tilt and
complete cumulant construction above. No inverse Hessian is identified
with an interacting covariance.

Once the full scalar catalog has the common admitted chart and
weighted root bound just established, endpoint Cauchy estimates may be
applied to the complete normalized coefficients. They include all
terms in (IR.21)–(IR.22) automatically. The actual spatial root and
diameter estimates must precede that Cauchy step. A mere real first or
second derivative bound would not establish the unsubtracted native
catalog or its all-order factorial bound.

The remaining work in a final C05 assembly is therefore bookkeeping of
the proved alternatives and its actual contour/rate conventions, not a
replacement of any of the source or normalization interfaces by a
generic covariance assertion.
