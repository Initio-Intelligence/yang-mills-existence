# Physical normalization and stopped sequences

The signed Gaussian calibration controls the stopped homogeneous coupling construction. Nonzero finite-cutoff reflected susceptibilities allow finite normalization. Passage to a common physical limit requires uniform bounds on those susceptibilities and control of the full normalized hierarchy.

The fixed-exterior anchored score is derived in [Gauge covariance of partial integrals and exterior scores](../continuum/partial_gauge_transport.md). Its implication for the boundary response is explained in [Conditional-law estimates and the continuum application](../continuum/conditional_to_continuum.md). The seven reflected measurements are treated in [Reflected susceptibilities and physical normalization](physical_normalization.md).
