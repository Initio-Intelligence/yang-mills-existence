## 1. The exact map and where the numerical coupling is used

\[

 \tag{BP.1}

\[
 Y_b\ge c_-(Y_k+k-b),\qquad
 Y_b-Y_k\le D_+(k-b)+B_+.
 \tag{BP.1a}

The retained two-sided terminal AF envelope also suffices, since it implies \(Y_b\ge(a_{\rm AF}/A)Y_k+a_{\rm AF}(k-b)\) and the corresponding upper comparison with fixed constants. ## 10. The numerical continuation supplied by the two estimates

This section states the continuation in source inverse-coupling units and retains the actual signed Gaussian increments. It does not assume that each Gaussian increment is positive.

 \tag{BP.23}

\[

 \tag{BP.24}
