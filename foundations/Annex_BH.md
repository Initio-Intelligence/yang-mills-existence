### 1. Exact rooted output with its full physical carrier sum

Construct the marked numerator using the same denominator and the same complete density as the source-free operation. After cancellation of unmarked components, prove an actual carrier identity

\[
Q_kF_k=\sum_X A_{k+1,X},
\tag{M04-R1}
\]

where each \(A_{k+1,X}\) is a function of the coordinates in its declared full dependency footprint \(X\), with all constrained-background, E/B/R, Haar, coarea and retained-mean terms assigned once. All current and response records are part of the construction.

The terms must satisfy the value part of the actual M-03 carrier bound, with constants controlled by \(\mathfrak B_k(F_k)\) uniformly in cutoff, volume and the admitted regular sector. Its weight is the existing
\(w_h(X)=\exp[\kappa(|X|+\mathsf t_h(X))]\), including full footprints. A source norm that exempts a fixed nonsmall component's interior does not supply this bound. Nor does a small positive majorant prove that the function it bounds has the majorant's support. NP19.3 proves a payment for a majorant only.

The rooted expansion in CMP119 (3.44)–(3.47) is a concrete source starting point. The needed marked estimate has not yet been proved for this input budget and this norm.

### 2. Exact direct/current records on the full M-03 ambient domain

For every actual M-03 perturbation, retain the exact identity

\[
\delta_\tau(Q_vF_v)
=Q_{\tau v}\!\left(\frac{F_{\tau v}-F_v}{t}\right)
+  \frac{Q_{\tau v}-Q_v}{t}(F_v),
\tag{M04-R2}
\]

with denominator \(t=1\) for discrete history changes and the prescribed normalized steps for continuous variables. Pullbacks and extensions in this formula must be the actual chosen versions. The second term includes changes of the normalized density, support, selectors and reference geometry. It is not discarded because the unmarked normalization is one.

Construct compatible carrier triples \(A_{k+1,X},B_{k+1,X,\tau},J_{k+1,X,\tau}\) with
\(\delta_\tau A_{k+1,X}=B_{k+1,X,\tau}+J_{k+1,X,\tau}\).

This includes both the specified all-\(p\) moments under the actual outer law and the ambient pointwise quotients by the native regulator. The norm counts direct and current records separately.

A common interior native chart proves only its admitted current directions. Raw retained links, archived labels, zero-normalizer extensions and chart boundaries still need their own exact treatment.
