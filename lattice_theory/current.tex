\section{Conditional current estimates}
\label{rt:target}

Work on the periodic four-dimensional \(n^4\) lattice, with fixed \(SU(N)\), \(N\ge2\), and the original positive compact law
\begin{equation}
 \dd\mu_\beta=Z_\beta^{-1}e^{-\beta S}\dd\lambda,\qquad
 S=\sum_p s_p,\qquad s_p=N-\Re\Tr U_p .
 \label{rt:law}
\end{equation}
The original invariant product-group metric and Casimir convention are fixed throughout. For a positive oriented link \(b\), let \(X_b^A\) denote the original color derivatives and set
\[
 F_b^A=X_b^AS,\qquad
 F_{b;\mu}^A=X_b^AS_{\mu\nu_b},\qquad
 D_b=\sum_{\mu\ne\nu_b,A}(F_{b;\mu}^A)^2,\qquad
 P_b=\sum_A(F_b^A)^2,\qquad
 \ell_b=\sum_A(X_b^A)^2S.
\]
All original roots, pointing shifts, neighboring affected roots and Casimir rows are kept. Equivalently, split the six plaquette orientations into the three perfect matchings
\[
 \{12,34\},\qquad \{13,24\},\qquad \{14,23\},
\]
and write \(S=\sum_{i=1}^3S_i\); at a given original link each matching contributes its unique directional force. For deterministic real marks \(f\), define
\begin{equation}
 r_f=\beta D[f]-\ell[f]/3,\quad
 h_f=\beta\{D[f]-P[f]/3\},\quad
 t_f=h_f-r_f=\{\ell[f]-\beta P[f]\}/3 .
 \label{rt:source}
\end{equation}
Write \(M_f=\sum_bf_b^2\) and \(b_f=\norm f_\infty\). Multiplication of an ordinary source by its original physical factor \(c_\beta^{-1}a^{-2}\) multiplies every stated variance by \(c_\beta^{-2}a^{-4}\).

Where an original local force or plaquette moment is invoked, retain the established periodic regime
\begin{equation}
 n\ge3,\qquad \beta\ge1,\qquad
 n\ge c_*\log(2+N\beta).
 \label{rt:momentregime}
\end{equation}
The relevant input from \texttt{block\_and\_transfer/wilson\_transfer/original\_sparse\_bath\_records.tex} (restated in the subsection ``The actual multiplaquette input'' of \texttt{innovation.tex}) is the original marked-plaquette exponential moment, in particular
\[
 \E_\mu e^{t\beta s_p}\le K_t,\qquad
 \E_\mu\beta\sum_i|F_{b,i}|^2\le C
\]
for a fixed admitted \(t>0\). These are full-law averaged estimates. They do not assert the same bounds for every frozen nonflat boundary.

\subsection{The actual retained susceptibility}

For an actual unrestricted coordinate integration \(K\), let \(\nu\) be its induced marginal and put \(\Phi_f=Kr_f\). For a disjoint family of cubes this marginal will be
\begin{equation}
 \dd\nu(b)=Z_\beta^{-1}e^{-\beta S_{\rm out}(b)}
                 \prod_j Z_j(b)\,\dd\lambda_B(b),\qquad
 Z_j(b)=\int e^{-\beta S_j(u,b)}\dd\lambda_{I_j}(u).
 \label{rt:raw}
\end{equation}
Every raw normalizer occurs exactly once. Other actual coordinate observations use their own exact marginal, with no assertion that it is another Wilson measure.

On the retained original product metric, define the canonical weighted operators
\[
 \mathcal L_0=d_\nu^*d,\qquad
 \mathcal L_1=dd_\nu^*+d_\nu^*d,\qquad
 \mathcal A f=d\Phi_f.
\]
At finite volume the positive smooth compact density permits inverses on centered functions and exact one-forms. The intertwining \(\mathcal L_1d=d\mathcal L_0\) gives
\begin{equation}
 \Cov_\nu(\Phi_f,\Phi_g)
   =\inner{\mathcal Af}{\mathcal L_1^{-1}\mathcal Ag}_{L^2(\nu)} .
 \label{rt:resolvent}
\end{equation}
The missing uniform source-space bound remains
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\mathcal A^*\mathcal L_1^{-1}\mathcal A\le CI.}
 \label{rt:missing}
\end{equation}
No theorem below asserts this inequality for the complete ordinary mean. A bound on \(\E|d\Phi_f|^2\), a representation of \(\Phi_f\) as a divergence, or a bound for a different reference law does not imply it without the relevant inverse or covariance estimate.

For an independent random packing offset \(T\), equations~\eqref{rt:raw}--\eqref{rt:resolvent} are applied fiberwise to \(\nu_T\), and the resulting quadratic forms are averaged over \(T\). Since the original source and \(\mu\) are independent of \(T\), \(\E_\mu K_T r_f=\E_\mu r_f\) for every \(T\). The between-offset first mean is therefore retained and is exactly constant.

\subsection{The unchanged heat-pressure formulation}

The section ``A positive heat path for the unchanged ordinary source'' of \texttt{ward\_heat.tex} constructs, for nonnegative marks \(f\), the original positive interpolation
\[
 \Delta_f=\sum_{b,A}f_b(X_b^A)^2,\qquad
 P_t^f=e^{t\Delta_f/(3\beta)},\qquad
 q_i(t)=P_t^f e^{-3\beta S_i},\qquad
 Z_f(t)=\int\bigl(q_1(t)q_2(t)q_3(t)\bigr)^{1/3}\dd\lambda .
\]
Its first logarithmic density derivative at zero is the unchanged \(r_f\). With
\(\psi_f(t)=\log Z_f(t)-\log Z_f(0)\), its exact second derivative is
\begin{equation}
 \psi_f''(0)=\Var_\mu r_f+\E_\mu J_f,\qquad
 \E_\mu|J_f|\le CM_f .
 \label{rt:heat}
\end{equation}
The full acceleration \(J_f\), including its original Casimir and directional partners, is derived in the subsection ``The generated acceleration under the actual Wilson law'' of \texttt{ward\_heat.tex}. Thus the desired upper bound on \(\psi_f''(0)\) is equivalent, up to an extensive correction, to the ordinary variance bound. The positive interpolation, its endpoint and its monotonicity give no upper second-derivative bound. Signed marks can be split into positive and negative parts for a variance conclusion; positivity of the heat evolution is not asserted for a signed generator.

\subsection{The results established in this section}

The new statements address three different laws and must retain that distinction.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Under the actual compact law~\eqref{rt:law}, an explicitly transported, nonlocal Ward observable \(T_f\) satisfies \(\Var T_f\le CM_f\), an inverse-form bound \(CM_f/\beta\), and complete original source-contact bounds. Its actual separator-conditioned mean has variance \(CM_f/R\); the field-dependent transport and raw conditional responses are included.
 \item For an explicitly constructed coordinate observation with retained collars and complete cube packets, the actual averaged internal ordinary variance is \(CM_f\), and the added good/bad-label variance is exponentially small in the stated mesoscopic range. The proof uses a pointwise full-row gradient inequality and full-law averaging, with no flat-boundary centering.
 \item In the separately named Maxwell Gaussian reference, the full separator mean has variance \(C\upsilon^2 d_GM_f/R\), with every cross-block contraction and arbitrary signed marks. This reference result extends uniformly to commuting flat holonomies after paying their common mean. Random-offset averaging does not automatically preserve the corresponding higher-source bound.
\end{enumerate}

Theorem~\ref{tr:separator} and Section~\ref{rt:closing} combine the first two statements into a paid covariance comparison for the actual retained nonlinear excess. Its covariance is the remaining issue.
