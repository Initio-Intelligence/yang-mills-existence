\paragraph{Input and its exact scope.}
The manuscript proves a stronger local moment than the elementary estimate used
in PD12 and in the orthogonal contact calculation through the second actual elimination calculation: Theorem 10.2, PDF pp.~21--23. Its normalization and finite-volume hypotheses are as follows.
Section 10 uses normalized action $a_p=s_p/N$ and coupling $B=N\beta$, so that
$Ba_p=\beta s_p$ is unchanged. Let $d_G=N^2-1$, fix the chessboard cell side $b=4$
and volume $v=b^4$, and use compatible dyadic tori. The partition exponents are
$Q_n=3n^4+1$, $R_n=3n^4-4n^3+1$, and $M=n^4/v$.
The tree gauge lower bound retains $Q_n$ Haar coordinates; the free-box chain integration
upper bound retains $R_n$. Their difference is exactly $4n^3$, not a bulk power.
The resulting finite-volume exponential moment is
\begin{equation}\label{expmom}
 \E e^{t\beta s_p}\le
 c_u^{R_n/M}c_l^{-Q_n/M}(1-t)^{-d_GR_n/(2M)}B^{2d_Gv/n},\quad 0\le t<1.
\end{equation}
Hence on the explicit regime $n\ge c_*\log(2+B)$, $B\ge1$, the right side is a constant
$C_{t,c_*,N}$ independent of $a,n,\beta$. This includes a compatible physical torus
sequence $n\asymp T/a$, $\beta=O(\log(1/a))$, but not arbitrary fixed $n$ as
$\beta\to\infty$. Earlier unrestricted estimates retain their stated scope.

The chessboard application uses a plaquette strictly inside the cell, so disseminated
plaquettes are distinct and their summed action is at most $S_0$. The needed site-reflection
positivity can also be checked directly: condition on the two reflection-plane boundary
link sets, split the other links into the two halves, and assign boundary plaquettes to
the boundary action. No unit plaquette crosses both interiors. The two conditional half
integrals are reflections of one another; their product for a reflected test is a square.
Haar inversion preserves the reflected oriented-link measure. This verifies the actual
Wilson reflection hypothesis, including shared boundary links.
The site-reflection chessboard form and its block conventions are independently recorded
in Biskup--Chayes--Nussinov, Section 6.1, Theorem 6.2; the general function form is
Biskup, Theorem 4.8. The partition comparison giving \eqref{expmom} is the manuscript's
explicit argument, not an appeal to a weak-coupling asymptotic expansion.

\paragraph{Local moments with unchanged normalization.}
Fix $t\in(0,1)$ and expand the nonnegative exponential series. For every integer $m$,
\[
 \E s_p^m\le C_t m!t^{-m}\beta^{-m}.
\]
For every fixed finite plaquette patch $Y$, Holder gives
$\E\exp(t\beta\sum_{p\in Y}s_p/\#Y)\le C_t$; independence is not assumed.
Thus every fixed local patch energy has all fixed moments at scale $\beta^{-1}$.
In particular every directional force obeys
$\|\mathbf F_{b;\mu}\|_p\le C_p\beta^{-1/2}$, by its actual compact energy bound.
For a literal physical H row and its actual ordinary companion define
\[
 g_c^O=\sigma\left(\sum_\mu|\mathbf F_{c;\mu}|^2-\tfrac13|\mathbf F_c|^2\right),
 \qquad
 g_c^r=\sigma\left(\sum_\mu|\mathbf F_{c;\mu}|^2
            -\frac1{3\beta}\sum_A(X_c^A)^2S_0\right).
\]
Both have
\begin{equation}\label{rowmom}
 \|g_c\|_{L^p(\mu)}\le C_p\sigma\beta^{-1}=C_pc_\beta^{-1}a^{-2}
\end{equation}
for all fixed $p$. The Casimir constant is retained and itself has this scale.
Every original transformed row in the pointed completion satisfies the same estimate.
No global connected covariance bound follows by summing \eqref{rowmom} alone.

\paragraph{A reusable actual product-fiber covariance lemma.}
Let a genuine conditional law of variables $(z_e)_{e\in E}$ given $y$ be a product.
Let $A_c=A_c(y,z_{J_c})$ have deterministic free-coordinate supports $J_c\subset E$.
Suppose $\|A_c\|_{2p}\le L_p$ under the complete probability and let the row overlap
graph $c\sim d$ mean $J_c\cap J_d\ne\varnothing$, with each row meeting at most $D$
other rows (include the diagonal when $J_c\ne\varnothing$).
Conditional independence makes every off-graph covariance exactly zero. For a graph entry,
conditional Holder and Jensen give
$\|\Cov(A_c,A_d|y)\|_p\le2\|A_c\|_{2p}\|A_d\|_{2p}\le2L_p^2$.
Consequently, by the graph's row and column degree bounds,
\begin{equation}\label{fiberlemma}
 \left\|\Cov\left(\sum_cf_cA_c,\sum_dh_dA_d\mid y\right)\right\|_p
 \le2D L_p^2\|f\|_{\ell^2}\|h\|_{\ell^2}.
\end{equation}
This is an estimate of the actual inherited conditional covariance, with its original
probability and supports. It requires neither a gap nor a comparison with fine total variation.

\paragraph{Application through every fixed number of actual independent layers.}
Start from the original plaquette interaction graph. At each stage eliminate an independent
set in the CURRENT genuine interaction graph, including all conditional-normalizer
hyperedges from previous stages. Let $R_k$ be the retained coordinates and
$g_{c,k}=\E(g_c|R_k)$. Conditional Jensen preserves \eqref{rowmom} exactly, with no
factor accumulated per stage. The actual conditional law for the next independent set
is a product by the graph definition. Its row supports are the deterministic inherited
carriers, obtained by replacing an eliminated coordinate by its actual neighboring coordinates.
For every fixed depth $k$, these carriers and their overlap counts are uniformly finite.
Applying \eqref{fiberlemma} gives
\begin{equation}\label{fixeddepth}
 \|\Cov(G_k(f),G_k(h)\mid R_{k+1})\|_{L^p(\nu_{k+1})}
 \le C_pD_k a^{-4}\|f\|_{\ell^2}\|h\|_{\ell^2},
 \qquad G_k=\sum_cf_cg_{c,k}.
\end{equation}
There is NO exponential-in-$\beta$ cost and NO logarithmic loss in this compatible
physical-torus regime. In particular this improves the costly second-step bound of the second actual elimination calculation
on that regime, and improves the local ordinary covariance calculation to all fixed $L^p$ conditional covariances.
The prescribed finite pointed averages are expanded before this estimate, so all shifted
weights and their fixed multiplicity remain. The support count in the support and martingale estimate calculation converts
$\|f\|_{\ell^2}^2$ to the literal completed support count.

For clarity, the depth dependence is not hidden. Eliminating an independent set replaces
each action hyperedge touching an eliminated coordinate by a hyperedge within its old
neighbor set. Thus the next graph is contained in the square of the old graph. Measured
in original plaquette-graph distance, interaction radius is at most $2^k$, and a row carrier
has radius at most a fixed multiple of $2^k$. Four-dimensional lattice ball counts then
bound its overlap degree by $D_k\le C2^{4k}$ until wrapping saturates the torus.
This crude bound is adequate at every fixed $k$ but is not a strict stable scale gain.

\paragraph{Improved inherited-mean derivatives.}
For the first layer, \eqref{response} of the inherited effective score calculation together with \eqref{rowmom} gives directly
\[
 \|X_b\overline G(f)\|_2\le C\sigma\beta^{-1/2}W_b(f).
\]
Indeed the direct derivative is a finite sum of directional force times bounded Hessian,
while $\|\Cov(G_b,F_b|y)\|_2\le2\|G_b\|_4\|F_b\|_4
\le C\sigma\beta^{-3/2}W_b(f)$ for the actual local varying sum.
Thus the logarithms in the inherited effective score calculation also disappear on the stated regime.
This does not remove original-mesh powers or supply complex suprema.

\paragraph{The explicit all-depth obstruction to this inference.}
The reverse-martingale identity still reads
\[
 \Var_\mu G=\sum_{j<k}\E\Var(G_j|R_{j+1})+\Var_{\nu_k}G_k.
\]
The fixed-depth bounds control each displayed increment. They do not bound the final
inherited covariance uniformly in depth, and $D_k$ grows until it can reach the torus
volume. Summing the crude estimate to complete elimination can therefore recover a
quadratic-volume bound, not the required linear-volume global bound.
No separated decay follows merely from the product-fiber lemma: if supports have not
met, the conditional covariance vanishes and the full correlation remains in the retained
mean covariance. Actual multiscale grades, anchored kernel moments, common domains,
current lifts and independent scalar terms still need their own estimates.

\paragraph{Selectors and raw measures.}
Every displayed $L^p$ estimate uses the complete positive Wilson probability or its
actual coordinate marginal. A sharp selected branch with free-coordinate dependence is
not a product fiber unless independently verified; its boundary and denominator responses
remain. Bath-measurable restrictions preserve undivided positive integrals, but normalizing
rare branches has its actual cost. Raw connected numerators carry the same original $Z$,
and independent scalar factors are never removed. The source remains XP3 unless the
explicit optional contact extension of the local source transition calculation is separately chosen.
