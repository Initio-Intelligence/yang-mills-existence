\begin{theorem}[Actual conditional-mean gradient]
For a partition $B=(x,z)$ into original coordinate groups and a smooth real carrier $F$,
let $m(x)=\E_K(F|x)$ under the genuine conditional density.
For every $p\ge2$,
\[
 \|\nabla_xm\|_{L^p(\nu_x)}
 \le\sqrt{1+(\Lambda_*/\kappa)^2}\,
          \|\nabla_BF\|_{L^p(K)}.
\]
This bound is uniform in the choice of retained subset and hence does not multiply once
per elimination step. It is an inherited-gradient estimate under the IDENTIFIED native
measure, not a transfer of an averaged original-Wilson estimate to another law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual fixed-domain conditional denominator gives
\[
 \nabla_xm=\E(\nabla_xF|x)-\Cov(F,\nabla_xU|x).
\]
For any unit vector $a$ in the retained coordinates, conditional Poincare gives
\[
 \Var(a\cdot\nabla_xU|x)\le\kappa^{-1}
     \E(|U_{zx}a|^2|x)\le\kappa^{-1}\Lambda_*^2,
\]
while $\Var(F|x)\le\kappa^{-1}\E(|\nabla_zF|^2|x)$.
Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz and the supremum over $a$ therefore give, without a dimension
factor,
\[
 |\nabla_xm|\le[\E(|\nabla_xF|^2|x)]^{1/2}
       +(\Lambda_*/\kappa)[\E(|\nabla_zF|^2|x)]^{1/2}
 \le\sqrt{1+(\Lambda_*/\kappa)^2}\,[\E(|\nabla_BF|^2|x)]^{1/2}.
\]
Conditional Jensen proves the $L^p$ assertion. The covariance term is the actual effective
score/normalizer response and has not been discarded.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Marginal conditional Poincare without replacing the density.}
Let $Y$ be any retained group subset, and split it as $Y=x\cup z$. The conditional law
of $z$ under the ACTUAL marginal $\nu_Y$ given $x$ is the $z$-marginal of the original
native law conditioned only on $x$. That full conditional native law still has Hessian
floor $\kappa$ and the original product domain. Apply its Poincare inequality to a test
$H(z)$ independent of all other internal coordinates. It gives exactly
\[
 \Var_{\nu_Y(\cdot|x)}H\le\kappa^{-1}
       \nu_Y(|\nabla_zH|^2|x).
\]
Thus EVERY marginal conditional fiber in this native coordinate hierarchy has the same
Poincare constant. This proof does not assert that its effective score is the original
bare score; its marginal density is the genuine integral and its differentiated denominator
is retained. No appeal to an unproved general effective-Wilson spectral gap is made.

\begin{theorem}[Summed energy of the actual increment currents]
Let $Y_0\supset\cdots\supset Y_m$ be retained native coordinate subsets and
$F_k=\E_K(F|Y_k)$. In the actual marginal conditional fiber
$z_k=Y_{k-1}\setminus Y_k$ given $Y_k$, construct the zero-mean Neumann solution
for $F_{k-1}-F_k$ using its ACTUAL weighted Laplacian. Let $W_k$ be its internal
configuration-space gradient current, with zero normal flux on its sharp product boundary.
Then
\[
 \sum_{k=1}^m\int|W_k|^2d\nu_{Y_{k-1}}
 \le\kappa^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^mK|F_{k-1}-F_k|^2
 \le\kappa^{-2}K|\nabla_BF|^2.
\]
Every current represents its exact scalar increment with the true marginal score and
normalizer. The bound is independent of elimination depth.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding conditional Poincare bound gives the weak mean-zero Poisson solution and
its energy estimate exactly as in the native stopped covariance current calculation. Integrate the conditional estimate for each
stage and sum. Reverse-martingale orthogonality bounds the sum of increment variances
by $\Var_KF$, which the original native Poincare inequality bounds by the final expression.
The inherited-gradient theorem ensures the actual conditional means have the necessary
Sobolev regularity. Corners are handled in the original weak Neumann formulation.
\end{proof}
