\paragraph{An identified changing-law interface.}
Use precisely the same-target, same-map, same-forest and same-sharp-selector native
weight interpolation in \cite[Section~4]{ND}:
\[
 dK_t=\frac{e^{-tw}}{K_0(e^{-tw})}\,dK_0,\qquad0\le t\le1.
\]
The function $w$ is the actual centered spatial action difference of UW12, with
$|\nabla w|\le\alpha_w+2\delta_w|B|$ and
$\|D^2w\|\le H_w\le\kappa_0/2$ on the unchanged original domain.
Thus $K_t$ has convexity floor $\kappa=\kappa_0-H_w>0$.
All raw classical, coordinate and independent scalar terms remain separate as in \cite[(4.8)]{ND}.
This note estimates an actual inherited conditional covariance, rather than replacing it
by a total-variation distance.

Let $q=q(B)$ be any FIXED measurable observation and let $F,G$ be the SAME actual
carriers throughout the interpolation. Write
\[
 m_{F,t}=\E_t(F|q),\quad R_{F,t}=F-m_{F,t},\quad
 V_t=\E_t\Cov_t(F,G|q),\quad T_t=\Cov_t(m_{F,t},m_{G,t}).
\]
All output laws are the genuine pushforwards $\nu_t=q_*K_t$, not a fixed replacement law.
The finite native cutoff makes $w$ bounded for each cutoff, so conditional ratios can be
differentiated directly; no uniform bound on that crude supremum is needed in the estimates.

\paragraph{Exact denominator and output-law responses.}
With conditional expectations initially taken under $K_0$,
\[
 m_{F,t}(q)=\frac{\E_0(e^{-tw}F|q)}{\E_0(e^{-tw}|q)},\qquad
 \partial_tm_{F,t}=-\Cov_t(F,w|q).
\]
The output density responds with score $-\E_t(w|q)+\E_tw$.
At the pointwise conditional level,
\[
 \partial_t\Cov_t(F,G|q)
 =-\E_t[R_{F,t}R_{G,t}(w-\E_t(w|q))\mid q].
\]
This term alone is NOT the derivative of its output integral; the output-law score remains.
Combining both responses, or differentiating $V_t=\E_t(R_{F,t}R_{G,t})$, yields
\begin{equation}\label{Vresponse}
 V'_t=-\Cov_t(R_{F,t}R_{G,t},w).
\end{equation}
Indeed the derivatives of the conditional means are $q$-measurable and pair to zero
with the other conditionally centered residual. This is an exact cancellation with the
actual conditional denominators, not deletion of their responses.
Also
\[
 \frac d{dt}\Cov_t(F,G)
 =-\E_t[(F-\E_tF)(G-\E_tG)(w-\E_tw)],\qquad
 T_t=\Cov_t(F,G)-V_t.
\]

\paragraph{A fourth-moment consequence of native Poincare.}
For a smooth carrier $F$, put $H=F-\E_tF$ and $A=(K_t|\nabla F|^4)^{1/4}$.
Poincare applied to $H$ and $H^2$ gives
\[
 K_tH^4\le\kappa^{-2}A^4
    +4\kappa^{-1}(K_tH^4)^{1/2}A^2.
\]
Solving this quadratic inequality shows
$\|H\|_4\le C\kappa^{-1/2}A$.
Conditional expectation is an $L^4$ contraction, so
$\|R_{F,t}\|_4\le2\|F-\E_tF\|_4$.
These bounds hold on the original sharp convex domain, with its native density and drift.

\begin{theorem}[Depth-independent same-record covariance response]
Let $A_F(t)=(K_t|\nabla F|^4)^{1/4}$ and similarly $A_G(t)$.
Then, for every fixed observation $q$,
\[
 |V'_t|+|T'_t|
 \le C\kappa^{-1}A_F(t)A_G(t)\sqrt{\Var_{K_t}w}.
\]
In particular, using the ACTUAL bound of \cite[Section~4]{ND}
$\Var_{K_t}w\le B_w=\kappa^{-1}(2\alpha_w^2+8\delta_w^2M_{2,*})$,
\[
 |V_1-V_0|+|T_1-T_0|
 \le C\kappa^{-1}\sqrt{B_w}\int_0^1 A_F(t)A_G(t)\,dt.
\]
The constant is independent of the number of preceding internal observations or eliminations
used to define $q$. The original same-record and convexity hypotheses are essential.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Cauchy--Schwarz to \eqref{Vresponse}, then Holder:
$|V'_t|\le\|R_{F,t}\|_4\|R_{G,t}\|_4\sqrt{\Var_tw}$.
Use the fourth-moment bound. The derivative of the full covariance has the same bound
without the residual factor two; subtracting gives the estimate for $T'_t$.
Integrate the genuine interpolation. No conditional spectral gap for $q$ is required.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Explicit physical H/lower-row scale.}
For the complete native interior sources of \cite[Section~5]{ND}, let $A_f^{\rm src}$ denote
$\|f\|_\infty\gamma^{-2}V_{D,f}$ for H, or
$\|f\|_\infty\gamma^{-2}[V_{D,f}+\beta_s^{-1}a^{-2}V_{Y,f}]$ for its ordinary partner.
The actual gradient bound is $|\nabla F|\le C A_f^{\rm src}h(\epsilon_{\rm bg}+h|B|)$.
The drift/count-explicit radial moment estimate on $K_t$ gives
\[
 K_t|B|^4\le M_{4,*}:=
 (2/\kappa)^2\left(n+\frac{L_*^2}{2\kappa}\right)
              \left(n+2+\frac{L_*^2}{2\kappa}\right).
\]
Consequently the covariance response is bounded by
\[
 C\kappa^{-1}A_f^{\rm src}A_k^{\rm src}h^2
   (\epsilon_{\rm bg}+hM_{4,*}^{1/4})^2\sqrt{B_w}.
\]
Every original saved coupling, fine mesh, lower action row, target count and support volume
is present. The Casimir constant remains in absolute masses but drops from centered products.
The bound applies to stopped conditional covariance of these actual carriers, not an
unidentified reconstructed physical word.

\paragraph{What changes outside this interface.}
If carriers vary, the exact derivative of $V_t$ additionally contains
$\E_t\Cov_t(\partial_tF_t,G_t|q)+\E_t\Cov_t(F_t,\partial_tG_t|q)$.
If the observation changes, its response is additional; if the original cutoff/forest/map
changes, domain, constraint and live-reference terms are additional. None is set to zero
by the displayed covariance cancellation. Full current and boundary jets remain in the
weak transport. Raw log-denominator comparison still contains the original classical term
and first-order expectation from \cite[(4.8)]{ND}, plus independent scalars.
Thus this gives a genuine quantitative changing-WEIGHT/same-record interface for the
native stopped covariance. It does not supply a general changing-record MT36 bound.
