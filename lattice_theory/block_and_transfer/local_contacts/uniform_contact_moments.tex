\title{Uniform same-Wilson moment bounds for the extracted H contact}\author{}\date{}

This note improves the coupling-dependent upper bounds for the EXPECTED extracted
contact in the orthogonal contact calculation and the local source transition calculation. It changes none of their source conventions or open claims.
Use the original full isotropic Wilson probability, AI1--3 and the packed pairs of the orthogonal contact calculation.
All conclusions here are finite-cutoff exact inequalities under that positive probability.
They are not bounds for arbitrary selected complex histories.

\paragraph{The total-force moment identity.}
Fix a bond $b$, a unit color coordinate $A$, and set $F=X_b^AS_0$.
Only the six incident plaquettes contribute. Compactness gives a constant $H_N$ independent
of $a$, volume, and $\beta$ such that $|X_b^AF|\le H_N$.
Haar integration by parts, with no boundary, gives for every integer $m\ge1$
\[
 \beta\E F^{2m}=(2m-1)\E[F^{2m-2}X_b^AF]
 \le(2m-1)H_N\E F^{2m-2}.
\]
Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{wardmom}
 \E F^{2m}\le(2m-1)!!(H_N/\beta)^m.
\end{equation}
For the entire color vector $\mathbf F_b=(X_b^AS_0)_A$, fixed color dimension and
$(\sum_A x_A^2)^m\le d^{m-1}\sum_A|x_A|^{2m}$ imply
\[
 \E|\mathbf F_b|^{2m}\le C_{N,m}\beta^{-m}.
\]
No positive Hessian hypothesis is needed: only its uniform absolute upper bound enters.
These identities estimate the TOTAL Wilson force; applying them to an individual plane
force with the same density would be incorrect.

\paragraph{Why the total force controls this particular contact.}
For the pair $b_-,b_+$ with middle bond $b_0$, condition on all other links, denoted $\mathcal B$.
The exact middle total force is
\[
 \mathbf F_{b_0}=a_-(U_-)+a_+(U_+)+c(\mathcal B).
\]
The original conditional law is a product and $c$ is a bath vector. Thus
\[
 \Cov(\mathbf F_{b_0}\mid\mathcal B)=C_-+C_+.
\]
Both $C_\pm$ are positive semidefinite. Pointwise in the bath,
\begin{align}
 0\le\operatorname{tr}(C_-C_+)
 &\le (\operatorname{tr}C_-)(\operatorname{tr}C_+)\\
 &\le\tfrac14\{\operatorname{tr}(C_-+C_+)\}^2
 \le\tfrac14\{\E(|\mathbf F_{b_0}|^2\mid\mathcal B)\}^2.
\end{align}
Conditional Jensen and \eqref{wardmom} consequently give, for every integer $p\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{covmom}
 \E[\operatorname{tr}(C_-C_+)]^p
 \le4^{-p}\E|\mathbf F_{b_0}|^{4p}
 \le C_{N,p}\beta^{-2p}.
\end{equation}
This proves the desired moment estimate without a bad-bath tail estimate, spectral gap,
or replacement of the conditional covariance by total variation.

\begin{theorem}[Matching contact scale and all fixed moments]
Let $v_{12,j}$ be the local scalar coefficient from the local source transition calculation for the physical H pair:
\[
 v_{12,j}=\frac{16}{9}\sigma^2(f_1)_{b_{0,j}}(f_2)_{b_{0,j}}
               \operatorname{tr}(C_{j,-}C_{j,+}),\qquad \sigma=g^{-2}a^{-2}.
\]
For every fixed $p\ge1$,
\[
 \|v_{12,j}\|_{L^p(\mu)}\le C_{N,p}c_\beta^{-2}a^{-4}
 |(f_1)_{b_{0,j}}(f_2)_{b_{0,j}}|,
\]
and
\[
 \Big\|\sum_jv_{12,j}\Big\|_{L^p(\mu)}
 \le C_{N,p}c_\beta^{-2}a^{-4}
 \sum_j|(f_1)_{b_{0,j}}(f_2)_{b_{0,j}}|.
\]
For a literal test and $\beta\ge\beta_0$, the lower-bound argument of the orthogonal contact calculation and
the upper bound above imply
\[
 ca^{-4}\sum_j|f_{b_{0,j}}|^2
 \le K_{\cal P}^{O}(f,f)\le Ca^{-4}\sum_j|f_{b_{0,j}}|^2.
\]
In particular a fixed plateau containing $\asymp a^{-4}$ blocks has extracted covariance
of order $a^{-8}$, with constants independent of $\beta\ge\beta_0$ and volume.
For the ordinary $r_H$ source multiply the contact form by $9/4$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \eqref{covmom}, then $\sigma^2\beta^{-2}=c_\beta^{-2}a^{-4}$ and Minkowski.
For noninteger fixed $p$, use the next integer and probability-space monotonicity of norms.
The lower bound is blockwise and multiplies $|f_{b_0}|^2$ exactly, so summing gives the
stated two-sided bound. Pointed completions retain their exact finite averages;
the upper estimate uses the corresponding double sum of absolute original test weights.
For their lower estimate the fixed positive plateau argument remains valid.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Residue moments and actual conditional covariance.}
There is also a short independent route to the residue bound:
\[
 R_j^O(f)=\frac23\sigma f_{b_0}Q_j|\mathbf F_{b_0}|^2.
\]
Each conditional expectation is an $L^p$ contraction, hence $\|Q_j\|_{p\to p}\le4$.
The force moment identity yields
$\|R_j^O(f)\|_p\le C_p a^{-2}|f_{b_0}|$ for every fixed $p$.
For a packed sum $R(f)=\sum_jR_j^O(f)$, conditional independence and conditional zero
means give the stronger square-count bound
\begin{equation}\label{resmom}
 \|R(f)\|_{2m}\le C_m a^{-2}\Big(\sum_j|f_{b_{0,j}}|^2\Big)^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
An elementary proof expands the even moment conditionally on the bath: every surviving
index occurs at least twice. For a multiplicity pattern $k_1+\cdots+k_l=2m$, $k_i\ge2$,
conditional Jensen followed by unconditional Holder bounds its expectation by
$\prod_i\|R_{j_i}\|_{2m}^{k_i}$. The finite set of multiplicity patterns is bounded by
$C_m(\sum_j w_j^2)^m$, with $w_j=|f_{b_{0,j}}|$. Signs are bounded in absolute value;
no independence of the bath variables is assumed.

Let $q$ now be ANY genuine measurable observation of the original probability, with
output law $\nu=q_*\mu$. Conditional Holder and Jensen, followed by \eqref{resmom}, give
\begin{equation}\label{outcov}
 \|\Cov(R(f),R(h)\mid q)\|_{L^p(\nu)}
 \le C_p a^{-4}\|f\|_{\ell^2(\mathcal P)}\|h\|_{\ell^2(\mathcal P)}.
\end{equation}
Indeed each of $\E(R(f)R(h)|q)$ and $\E(R(f)|q)\E(R(h)|q)$ is bounded in
$L^p$ by $\|R(f)\|_{2p}\|R(h)\|_{2p}$. This estimates the actual conditional covariance
of the EXTRACTED residues. It does not replace or bound the full ordinary conditional
covariance $\Cov(r_H(f),r_H(h)|q)$.

For a genuine nonnegative selected subprobability $1_E\mu$, restriction gives the same
unnormalized $p$th-moment integral upper bound; normalizing by $\mu(E)$ may cost
$\mu(E)^{-1/p}$, and conditional laws under selection need their own treatment.
A signed or complex selected kernel is not covered by probability contraction.
For a bath-measurable $q$, the local scalar $\E(v_{12}|q)$ has its original $L^p$ bound
by conditional Jensen. All saved physical factors and old meshes remain literal.

\paragraph{Remaining distinction.}
The exact decomposition still reads
$\Cov(O_H)=K_{\cal P}^{O}+\Cov(S_H)$, with a positive residual form.
The present matching bound controls the first term and its specified conditional responses.
It gives neither a summable spatial covariance bound for $S_H$ nor the full MT36 norm,
complex-domain admission, sharp-selector current partners, or interacting normalization.
XP3 still has $A_{12}^{\rm bare}=0$. Removing $v_{12}$ or its expectation is an explicit
renormalized source choice, as stated in the local source transition calculation.
