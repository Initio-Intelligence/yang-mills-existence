\paragraph{Input and the nonlinear gauge distinction.}
In the fixed-coefficient Ward--Schur identity of the original covariant hessian and ward schur calculation, interior Haar
integration by parts gives the original covariance blocks, their pseudoinverse
regression formula retains the exact residual variance, and the oriented
prefix-transport plaquette Hessian is correct. Its pseudoinverse handles true
null directions of the deterministic interior-score covariance matrix.
An ACTUAL nonabelian gauge vector, however, has left-frame coefficient
$w_b(U)=\xi_{s(b)}-\operatorname{Ad}_{U_b}\xi_{t(b)}$. It is field-dependent
and need not be a deterministic null vector of that covariance matrix. The
following identities handle that distinction without a gauge chart.

\paragraph{An exact common realization for a changing exterior.}
Let $S_t(u)=S(u;\eta(t))$ be the ORIGINAL compact Wilson block action on a
fixed product of interior Haar spaces, with every incident plaquette/collar
included. Let $\Phi_t$ be a smooth interior diffeomorphism flow with Eulerian
velocity $W_t$, and $J_t$ its Haar Jacobian. Then exactly
\[
 Z_t=\int e^{-S_t(\Phi_tu)}J_t(u)du.
\]
Define $D_t=\partial_t+W_t$ and the corrected score
\[
 G_t=D_tS_t-\operatorname{div}_{\rm Haar}W_t.                            \tag{1}
\]
Its pullback along the flow is the derivative of
$S_t(\Phi_tu)-\log J_t(u)$. With expectations in the SAME original fiber at
time $t$, the boundary effective action $\Gamma_t=-\log(Z_t/Z_0)$ obeys
\[
 \Gamma'_t=E_tG_t,\qquad
 \Gamma''_t=E_t(D_tG_t)-\operatorname{Var}_tG_t.                          \tag{2}
\]
All time derivatives of the chosen velocity, its divergence and the boundary
path occur in $D_tG_t$. In particular no boundary acceleration or Jacobian
term is omitted. The normalized pullback density has score
$-G_t+E_tG_t$ and Fisher information
\[
 \mathcal I_t^{\Phi}=\operatorname{Var}_tG_t.                            \tag{3}
\]
Consequently its affinity with the initial density satisfies the rigorous
path bound
\[
 \operatorname{Aff}(\widetilde\mu_0,\widetilde\mu_1)
 \ge1-\frac18\left(\int_0^1\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}_tG_t}\,dt\right)^2. \tag{4}
\]
This follows by differentiating the normalized square-root density in $L^2$;
its speed is $\frac12\sqrt{\mathcal I_t^{\Phi}}$. The possibly negative lower
bound in(4) is simply vacuous. An observable/output comparison requires the
flow to induce the intended actual output map or commute with its projection;
an arbitrary measure transport does not provide that identification.

\paragraph{What a field-dependent harmonic extension must pay.}
At a base point extend a chosen smooth interior field $W$ constantly in $t$,
write $F=\partial_tS_t|_0$, $H=\partial_t^2S_t|_0$, and set
$L_W=WS-\operatorname{div}W$. Haar integration by parts gives
\[
 E L_W=0,\qquad E(Wf)=E(fL_W).
\]
Thus the exact expected second term in(2) is
\[
 E(DG)=E H+2E(F L_W)+E L_W^2
      =\Gamma''+\|F-EF+L_W\|_{L^2(\mu)}^2.                            \tag{5}
\]
Equation(5) extends the constant-coefficient score regression of the original covariant hessian and ward schur calculation to
actual field-dependent vector fields, with the divergence retained. If $W_t$
varies, its extra Stein term has zero mean but remains present pointwise in(2).
No estimate for a covariant harmonic extension follows merely by writing(5).
It must have a proved local/coarse geometric meaning, controlled divergence,
and a compatible output map. Allowing arbitrary global transport would hide
the original problem in the construction of that transport.

\paragraph{A complete nonabelian block cancellation that DOES hold.}
Take fixed site generators $\xi_x$ and act on every original oriented link by
\[
 U_b(t)=g_{s(b)}(t)U_bg_{t(b)}(t)^{-1},\qquad g_x(t)=e^{t\xi_x}.
\]
Transform the prescribed exterior/collar record by the SAME site gauges, or
set the gauges to identity on its incident vertices when that record is held
fixed. On free links this is a product of left and right Haar translations:
$\operatorname{div}W=0$ and $J_t=1$ exactly. Every original plaquette transforms
by conjugation at its base point. Therefore
\[
 D_tS_t=0,\qquad G_t=0,\qquad Z(\eta(t))=Z(\eta(0)),\qquad
 \widetilde\mu_t=\widetilde\mu_0.                                      \tag{6}
\]
The exact affinity after this actual gauge alignment is one, independent of
block size, coupling and exterior roughness. This is a genuine changing-frame
comparison of original measures with all raw normalizers retained. It handles
only gauge-equivalent exteriors; it cannot change gauge-invariant curvature
or the eigenvalues of a noncontractible holonomy.

The left-trivialized link velocity and acceleration are
\[
 v_b=\xi_s-\operatorname{Ad}_{U_b}\xi_t,\qquad
 \dot v_b=-[\xi_s,\operatorname{Ad}_{U_b}\xi_t].                         \tag{7}
\]
Thus the ordinary left-geodesic Hessian in direction $v$ alone need NOT vanish
along a curved gauge orbit. For the boundary effective action its correct
identity is $\operatorname{Hess}_{\rm left}\Gamma(v,v)+d\Gamma(\dot v)=0$.
At a flat stationary reference the acceleration contribution vanishes and
one recovers the usual linearized longitudinal null directions.

At each plaquette the full gauge path is
$P(t)=e^{t\xi_x}Pe^{-t\xi_x}$, so
$P''(0)=[\xi_x,[\xi_x,P]]$ and $\operatorname{ReTr}P''(0)=0$.
This explicitly cancels the signed prefix-commutator/curvature terms of
the original covariant hessian and ward schur calculation TOGETHER WITH the acceleration contributions in(7). Setting those
terms separately to zero, or replacing the field-dependent gauge vector by
one fixed Euclidean gradient on arbitrary links, would miss this cancellation.

\paragraph{The actual block Gauss identity.}
For interior vertices whose exterior gauges are fixed, let $F_b$ denote the
left-link gradient of the complete original action. Differentiating the
pointwise gauge invariance and collecting arbitrary $\xi_x$ gives
\[
 \sum_{b:s(b)=x}F_b-
 \sum_{b:t(b)=x}\operatorname{Ad}_{U_b^{-1}}F_b=0.                       \tag{8}
\]
This exact covariant divergence identity includes every incident plaquette.
If only a selected color or an incomplete collar is retained, the omitted
links supply boundary currents; they cannot be erased from(8).
Exact marginals obey the corresponding gauge Ward identity whenever both the
integrated domain and the retained/exterior transformation are the actual
common gauge action. Arbitrary holonomy or physical transverse modes are not
removed by this statement.

\paragraph{What remains after this cancellation.}
The gauge part of an actual frame alignment is now controlled with ZERO cost,
not the extensive norm cost of a generic translation bound. This is a real
improvement for longitudinal/gauge directions and is compatible with the Gauss
reduction of the complete Gaussian block in the complete block schur and physical stiffness calculation. The physical transverse
Dirichlet-to-Neumann stiffness is still unproved in the interacting law.
A covariant extension for a transverse/coarse exterior change must be specified
as an actual field-dependent flow. Its corrected score(1), variance(3),
divergence and action on the retained record need bounds at the physical block
scale. Neither exact gauge cancellation nor the earlier same-law plaquette
moments supplies those bounds. No full S94 or mass-gap conclusion is made.
