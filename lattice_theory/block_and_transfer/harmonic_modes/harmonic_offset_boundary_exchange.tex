\paragraph{Analysis of the positive example.}
Consider the nonempty cycle exchange compensation calculation. Its interior gauge is a bijection
of the original spatial-link conditional fibers and preserves their
RAW Haar partition functions. The two temporal plaquettes give cubic
force $2\phi(x)\times\phi(x+\hat i)$. On its specified four-cycle
this is twice an original curl boundary, so the projection bound
$j^*A_I^{-1}j\le4/R$ and the complete interval
$-4/R\le T_{4,\rm red}\le-2/R$ are valid. This is a genuinely
compensated nonempty example, not merely a raw-vertex estimate.

The proposed extension to arbitrary harmonic boundary data has a
different outcome. Co-closedness of the cubic force alone does not
control its low spatial relative modes. The following specifies the
exact original record and the missing boundary/offset control.

\paragraph{1. Actual harmonic boundary family.}
On a side-$R$ four-cube retain all temporal links and integrate all
free spatial links, exactly as in the nonempty cycle exchange compensation calculation. Now prescribe the actual temporal
record, including its spatial-boundary values, by
\[
 U_0(t,x)=\exp(\alpha\phi(x)),\qquad
 \phi(x)=c e_1+\frac{x_1}{R^2}e_2,\quad c\ne0,
 \qquad U_i|_{\partial}=1 .                                \tag{1}
\]
The first tangent is $a_0=\phi$, $a_i=0$. It is harmonic in the
full original Maxwell problem: $\Delta_3\phi=0$, and the free
spatial forces from successive temporal plaquettes cancel.
All fixed-only plaquettes are included in its physical energy:
\[
 E(a)=\frac{(R+1)^2}{R^2}.                                  \tag{2}
\]
Thus this is a genuine harmonic boundary shear with uniformly bounded
quadratic curvature energy, not an arbitrary off-shell tree insertion.

For two neighboring temporal logarithms $u,v$, the original SU(2)
Wilson quartic is
\[
 -\frac{|u-v|^4}{24}-\frac{|u\times v|^2}{6}.
\]
Only the $01$ planes contribute. Therefore the complete physical raw
quartic along (1) is exactly
\[
 T_4(a)=-\frac{c^2(R+1)^2}{6R^2}
             -\frac{(R+1)^2}{24R^6}<0.                     \tag{3}
\]
The original cubic force on free spatial links is
\[
 j_1(t,x)=\frac{2c}{R^2}e_3,
 \qquad j_2=j_3=0.                                         \tag{4}
\]
It is co-closed at every original interior vertex. Nevertheless it
is not the local cycle source of the nonempty cycle exchange compensation calculation. Its boundary-induced relative
curl and its low-mode overlap are retained below.

\paragraph{2. An exact original relative eigenmode.}
The spatial conditional precision remains
$A_I=\Delta_t^D+H_3$, $H_3=d_3^*d_3$.
Take the actual free-link mode
\[
 r_1(t,x)=\sin(\pi t/R)\sin(\pi x_2/R)\sin(\pi x_3/R)e_3,
 \qquad r_2=r_3=0,                                         \tag{5}
\]
constant in the normal coordinate $x_1=0,\ldots,R-1$.
This has exactly the original boundary conditions: Dirichlet in
$t,x_2,x_3$, Neumann in its own component coordinate. Its divergence
vanishes because the normal component is constant in $x_1$.
Hence $H_3$ equals the relative vector Laplacian on this mode, giving
\[
 A_Ir=\lambda_Rr,\qquad
 \lambda_R=12\sin^2(\pi/(2R)),\qquad \|r\|^2=R^4/8.         \tag{6}
\]
Put $\theta=\pi/(2R)$. The exact finite sine sum yields
$\langle j,r\rangle=(2c/R)\cot^3\theta$.
Projection onto just this one actual mode proves
\[
 \boxed{j^*A_I^{-1}j\ge
       \frac{32c^2\cot^6\theta}{R^6\lambda_R}
       \asymp c^2R^2.}                                     \tag{7}
\]
The asymptotic coefficient is $2048/(3\pi^8)$.
No infinite-volume kernel or assumed spectral gap is used.

Since (3) is negative, the COMPLETE conditional classical quartic
plus exchange satisfies
\[
 T_{4,\rm red}=T_4(a)-\tfrac12j^*A_I^{-1}j
                        \le-\kappa c^2R^2                 \tag{8}
\]
for a fixed $\kappa>0$ and all sufficiently large $R$.
Consequently an $R$-uniform bound by $CE(a)^2$ is false even for
this specified NONEMPTY integration and actual harmonic first tangent.
This is stronger than the raw-coordinate diagnostic of the raw tree quartic energy obstruction calculation, but remains
only a statement about the specified classical boundary-source family.

\paragraph{3. Why removing the constant is not free.}
The constant color $c e_1$ costs no linear curvature energy. However,
it does not commute with the varying color. The honest boundary gauge
$g(t)=\exp(\alpha t c e_1)$ transforms (1) to a time-dependent record.
Writing $\psi=x_1 e_2/R^2$, its temporal logarithm begins
\[
 \alpha\psi+\alpha^2(t+\tfrac12)[c e_1,\psi]+O(\alpha^3).
                                                               \tag{9}
\]
The second-order original boundary source has not vanished. Its
spatial differences have size $t|c|/R^2$ and retain the low-mode
effect. Replacing (9) by the straight family $\exp(\alpha\psi)$
would change the physical boundary path. The global/root and boundary
data cannot be silently reset to obtain an energy-only bound.

The negative coefficient (8) is compatible with a nonnegative full
action: it is a Taylor coefficient, and its validity is local in
$\alpha$ at fixed $R$. In particular $\alpha R\to0$ can keep the
displayed gauge/source expansion within a small physical chart.
No divergence of a complete quantum coefficient or failure of S94
is inferred. Rather, a proposed general classical energy norm must
also control these nonlinear boundary/offset partners, or restrict
the original class with a verified preserved condition. That input
has not been established for the main all-history problem.

The acceleration in (9) multiplies the vanishing first derivative
at this quantum source order. The same holds for each COMPLETE

critical Coulomb norm for that physical background. The obstruction
