\paragraph{Input and scope.}
Use the complete harmonic kinetic matching calculation. The 18-mode symmetry, energy projection and
boundary-only Stokes map of the complete constant curvature coarse sector calculation are used here. In particular
an internal zero-boundary field has ZERO constant-curvature projection on
its whole block; the harmonic estimate cannot be applied to that leg.
This note uses genuine retained SUBblock boundaries instead, under the
original flat relative Gaussian law. This is the actual quadratic
reference for coefficient calculations, not a substituted nonlinear
Wilson or native conditional law.

\paragraph{1. Exact harmonic/zero-boundary decomposition and the raw Schur law.}
Use a common original relative gauge/forest so the physical Gaussian
precision is positive. Partition its variables into disjoint block
interiors $I$ and the retained skeleton $Y$. In these actual coordinates
write the precision as $A$. Its conditional harmonic lift is
\[
 W=-A_{II}^{-1}A_{IY},\quad E=\binom W I,\quad
 S=A_{YY}-A_{YI}A_{II}^{-1}A_{IY}.
\]
The exact quadratic decomposition and covariance are
\[
 x=(r,0)+Ey,\qquad
 x^*Ax=r^*A_{II}r+y^*Sy,
\]
\[
 \boxed{A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}A_{II}^{-1}&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}
                                  +ES^{-1}E^*,\qquad
        \det A=\det A_{II}\det S.}                              \tag{1}
\]
Every original plaquette is assigned once in this precision. Complete
subblock plaquette sets used below are comparison norms and may overlap;
fixed-only face energies remain in their original skeleton scalar.
The raw Gaussian integration factor
$(2\pi)^{\dim I/2}(\det A_{II})^{-1/2}$, together with the original
canonical/Haar constants, stays attached to the induced skeleton law.
The Schur precision $S$ is not reset to Wilson form.

For a complete subblock $B$, the 18 normalized constant-curvature
coordinates are
\[
 c_{B,i}(x)=\langle d_B\psi_{B,i},d_Bx\rangle,
 \qquad\langle d_B\psi_{B,i},d_B\psi_{B,j}\rangle=\delta_{ij}.
\]
They depend ONLY on the retained complete boundary, by literal Stokes.
Consequently they annihilate the zero-boundary term in (1). Their actual
joint covariance is that induced by $S^{-1}$, with all correlations and
normalizers retained; independence across blocks is not asserted.

\paragraph{2. A joint covariance and current bound without a block count.}
Let $\xi$ be the original centered canonical relative Gaussian with
energy $\mathcal E(\xi)=\|d\xi\|^2$. For any coefficients $a_i$ on one
block, energy Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 |\sum_i a_i c_{B,i}(w)|^2
       \le |a|^2\mathcal E_B(w)\le |a|^2\mathcal E(w).
\]
The exact finite Gaussian dual-energy formula therefore proves
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(c_B)\le I_{18}.                              \tag{2}
\]
If complete subblocks have plaquette overlap at most $q$, their
orthogonal energy projections give
\[
 \sum_B|c_B(w)|^2\le\sum_B\mathcal E_B(w)\le q\mathcal E(w),
 \qquad \operatorname{Cov}((c_B)_B)\le q I.                      \tag{3}
\]
For a literal four-dimensional cubical tiling $q\le4$: a plaquette can
lie on grid hyperplanes in its two transverse directions. This count
includes shared faces and corners.

The matched uncut kinetic coefficient of the complete constant curvature coarse sector calculation is $c_R$, with
$|c_R|\le C\log(2+R)$. Allow coefficients $\kappa_B$ for comparison and retain
the declared local kinetic piece
\[
 Q(\xi)=\sum_B \kappa_B|c_B(\xi)|^2,\qquad
 \kappa_*:=\sup_B|\kappa_B|.
\]
For a deterministic physical mean $h$,
Gaussian centering and (3) give
\[
 |E Q(\xi+\zeta h)-E Q(\xi)|\le \zeta^2 q \kappa_*\mathcal E(h),
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Var}\left(\partial_\zeta Q(\xi+\zeta h)|_0\right)
              \le4q^2\kappa_*^2\mathcal E(h).                         \tag{4}
\]
Indeed the current is twice the joint linear functional
$\sum_B\kappa_B\langle c_B(\xi),c_B(h)\rangle$; (3), not independence,
controls its variance. Similarly the second source derivative of
$\operatorname{Var}Q(\xi+\zeta h)$ is bounded by
$8q^2\kappa_*^2\mathcal E(h)$, since the Gaussian quadratic-linear third
moment vanishes. These are logarithmic and logarithmic-squared bounds
for the RETAINED kinetic piece, not for the full nonlinear packet.
Its source-zero expectations and variances can be extensive and are
stored separately rather than dropped.

\paragraph{3. A stronger exact cancellation for a coherent flux and uniform tiling.}
Suppose the original cube is tiled uniformly by identical complete
subblocks, with the SAME matched coefficient $c_R$, and let $h$ carry
coherent constant plaquette flux. For a zero-outer-boundary fluctuation
$\xi$, Stokes gives the exact identity
\[
 \sum_Bc_R\langle c_B(\xi),c_B(h)\rangle=0.                      \tag{5}
\]
For each plane orientation the constant flux factors out. The remaining
sum is a sum of plaquette curls. Complete-block multiplicities depend
only on the two transverse coordinates, so on each plane slice they
are constant. The plane sum telescopes to the original OUTER boundary,
where $\xi$ is zero. Shared faces are not deleted or counted incorrectly.
Hence
\[
 \boxed{Q(\xi+\zeta h)=Q(\xi)+\zeta^2Q(h).}                      \tag{6}
\]
The linear current vanishes pointwise, not only in expectation. All
higher centered cumulants of this one kinetic piece are unchanged by
that source shift. Its deterministic curvature is bounded by
$2q|c_R|\mathcal E(h)$.

For an explicitly declared coefficient-insertion parameter $\lambda$,
whenever the Gaussian integral exists, (6) also gives
\[
 {E e^{-\lambda Q(\xi+\zeta h)}\over E e^{-\lambda Q(\xi)}}
                     =e^{-\lambda\zeta^2Q(h)}.                   \tag{7}
\]
The entire source-zero Gaussian factor stays in the denominator; it is
not estimated by a root-count bound or set equal to one. For example
$2|\lambda|q|c_R|<1$ suffices for integrability. This is an identity for
this declared Gaussian kinetic piece, not a modification or proof of
control of the original nonlinear measure.

\paragraph{4. Exact residuals are still present.}
For each original subblock field restriction write
$x_B=h_{\overline F_B}+r_B$ using the boundary-only projection of the complete constant curvature coarse sector calculation.
The FULL completed uncut self-energy is
\[
 x_B^*\widehat K_Bx_B
 =c_R|c_B(x)|^2+2h_{\overline F_B}^*\widehat K_Br_B
                                      +r_B^*\widehat K_Br_B.      \tag{8}
\]
The last two terms are not controlled by (2)--(7). Energy orthogonality
does not make their $\widehat K_B$ cross term vanish. They include other
harmonic boundary modes as well as zero-boundary fluctuations.
Original source changes also vary covariance/vertices beyond this flat
Gaussian mean-shift channel. Heat-cutoff kernels require their explicit
regulator and marked-ghost partners; $c_R$ is the matched UNCUT coefficient
and is not assigned to each cutoff by fiat. Global holonomies outside
the chosen relative problem remain retained data.

\paragraph{5. Literal original-grid verification.}
The finite calculation uses a complete $R=4$ original relative Gaussian and its 16
complete $R=2$ subblocks. All 96 single-color coarse coordinates are built
from original incidence; colors then give three identical copies.
It verifies exactly the plane-current row cancellations in (5), the
maximum plaquette overlap $4$, and an energy-projection inequality.
Direct covariance computation checks both $I-\operatorname{Cov}(c_B)$
and $4I-\operatorname{Cov}((c_B)_B)$ are positive; their minimum elimination
pivots are respectively above $.869$ and $3.856$ in this test.
The projected coherent mean energy is $9/4$ versus global energy $25/16$,
consistent with the retained overlap bound, not a false disjoint norm.

Thus a genuine retained-boundary kinetic piece can be carried through
