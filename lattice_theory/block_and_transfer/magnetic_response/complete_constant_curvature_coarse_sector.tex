\paragraph{Full constant-curvature family.}
Work in the common flat linearized frame on the complete original
four-dimensional relative cube of side $R$. Let $F_{\mu\nu}^a$ be an
antisymmetric tensor, with $\mu<\nu$ and $a=1,2,3$ the color index.
Define the centered affine one-form
\[
 (h_F)_\mu^a(x)=\frac1{2R^2}\sum_\nu
          F_{\nu\mu}^a(x_\nu-R/2).                              \tag{1}
\]
It has constant plaquette curl
$(d_1h_F)_{\mu\nu}^a=F_{\mu\nu}^a/R^2$, zero interior divergence,
and zero interior Maxwell force. Thus it is the genuine physical
harmonic extension of its COMPLETE boundary restriction, not a new
boundary condition assigned to an arbitrary field.
With $N_R=R^2(R+1)^2$ plaquettes per orientation,
\[
 \mathcal E(h_F)=\frac{N_R}{R^4}\sum_{\mu<\nu,a}|F_{\mu\nu}^a|^2
      =E_R|F|^2,\qquad E_R=(R+1)^2/R^2.                         \tag{2}
\]
All fixed-only plaquettes are included. This is an 18-dimensional
constant-curvature sector modulo its irrelevant gauge choices.

\paragraph{A genuine complete-boundary coarse projection.}
For any one-form $w$ on this same block, define
\[

       \frac{R^2}{N_R}\sum_{p\parallel\mu\nu}(d_1w)_p^a.          \tag{4}
\]

\[
 \langle d_1(w-h_{\overline F}),d_1h_G\rangle=0

\]
Writing $r=w-h_{\overline F}$ therefore gives
\[
 \mathcal E(w)=E_R|\overline F|^2+\mathcal E(r).                  \tag{5}
\]

 \boxed{w|_{\partial}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
