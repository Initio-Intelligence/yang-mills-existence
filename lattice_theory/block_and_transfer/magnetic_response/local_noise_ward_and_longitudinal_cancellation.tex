\paragraph{Local gauge Jacobian and boundary source.}
The BCH signs, local Jacobian and coefficient formula of the local gauge completion first nonlinear coefficient calculation are used. The
Jacobian is measured in LEFT-INVARIANT gauge tangents, not in Euclidean
logarithms of gauge parameters; thus an extra gauge-Haar polynomial must
not be appended to it. Full-free-link Haar is already present. Its claim
is local equality of fixed-block Laplace coefficients, not a global gauge
substitution or a size-uniform complementary-integral bound.
The boundary-source Ward identity handles the complementary longitudinal terms.
Here we remove the longitudinal vector sectors of the FULL first nonlinear
coefficient before spatial estimates. This is an exact cancellation for
every block size, but it does not bound the surviving transverse terms.

\paragraph{1. Keep the normalized local gauge-noise integral.}
At the actual critical minimum write $H D=0$, $g=(D^*D)^{-1}$ and
$F(z)=D^*z$. Replace the local penalty of the local gauge completion first nonlinear coefficient calculation by
$\kappa |F|^2/2$, with $\kappa>0$, retaining its normalized Gaussian
prefactor $(\kappa/2\pi)^{m/2}$, where $m$ is the interior gauge dimension.
The actual local gauge Jacobian remains
\[
 M(z)=D^*D+\alpha M_1(z)+\alpha^2M_2(z)+\cdots,\quad
 \ell_1=\operatorname{Tr}gM_1,\quad
 \ell_2=\operatorname{Tr}gM_2-\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}gM_1gM_1,
 \qquad\alpha=B^{-1/2}.
\]
It is independent of $\kappa$. The inverse-function construction can be
made with cutoffs equal to one near the minimum orbit and near zero gauge
noise. For each FIXED block, background in its admitted neighborhood, and
fixed $\kappa>0$, local change of variables along each gauge orbit gives
\[
 E_{\rm local}\varphi(F)
   = E_{N(0,\kappa^{-1}I)}\varphi+\text{exponentially small terms}   \tag{1}
\]
for every fixed polynomial $\varphi$. Indeed $\det M$ is precisely the
Jacobian from the left-invariant orbit measure to $dF$; the action and
physical tree record are constant along that orbit. The normalizing
Gaussian over $F$ integrates to one. Its physical cutoff complement has
the fixed-block action gap; its gauge cutoff complement has Gaussian cost
$e^{-c_{R,\kappa}B}$. Polynomial insertions or a fixed number of derivatives
change only fixed-block prefactors. Constants are NOT uniform in $R$ or
in $\kappa$. The normalization includes the constant gauge-orbit Haar and
canonical factors; none of them supplies an extra order-$\alpha^2$ vertex.
Equation(1) is used only as an identity of fixed-block asymptotic
coefficients. It is not a claim about the full original gauge distribution.

\paragraph{2. The completed mean current is exactly transverse.}
Let
\[
 G_\kappa=(H+\kappa DD^*)^{-1},\quad
 T_3={1\over6}t_{abc}z_a z_b z_c,\quad
 \ell_1=l_a z_a,\quad
 \tau_a=t_{abc}(G_\kappa)_{bc},\quad b=l-\tfrac12\tau.              \tag{2}
\]
The original local density's first odd correction is
$\alpha(\ell_1-T_3)$. Its mean is therefore
\[
 E z=\alpha G_\kappa b+O_R(\alpha^2).
\]
Taking $\varphi(F)=F$ in (1) makes the displayed coefficient vanish
exactly. Since $HD=0$,
\[
 D^*G_\kappa=\kappa^{-1}gD^*,\qquad
 \boxed{D^*b=0.}                                                  \tag{3}
\]
The ghost-Jacobian linear vertex and the cubic tadpole are needed for this
identity; neither is separately asserted transverse. At a flat reference
color symmetry gives $l=\tau=b=0$. Differentiation along the actual
background path then gives $D_0^*b'=0$, with no omitted $D'$ term because
it multiplies $b=0$. At a nonflat background the exact differentiated
identity is instead $D^*b'=-(D^*)'b$.

\paragraph{3. Exact contraction of the complete first coefficient.}
With $T_4=u_{abcd}z_a z_b z_c z_d/24$ and symmetric tensors, Wick pairing
of the COMPLETE bracket of the local gauge completion first nonlinear coefficient calculation gives
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal C_R(\kappa)=
 {1\over8}u_{abcd}G_{ab}G_{cd}-EJ_2-E\ell_2
 -{1\over12}t_{abc}t_{def}G_{ad}G_{be}G_{cf}
 -\tfrac12 b^*G b,\quad G=G_\kappa.}                              \tag{4}
\]
There are six all-cross pairings between the two cubic vertices and nine
pairings with one internal pair at each vertex. The latter combine with
$T_3\ell_1$ and $\ell_1^2/2$ into exactly $b^*Gb/2$.
At flat background the second derivative of this last term is
$-b'^*G_\kappa b'$. By (3) it has no longitudinal vector propagator.
This signed current must still be combined with the differentiated
quartic, Haar, nonlinear Jacobian and all-cross cubic terms in (4).
It is not by itself the full source response.

\paragraph{4. All longitudinal vector powers cancel in the FULL bracket.}
The normalized gauge-noise insertion leaves the physical local integral
unchanged to every fixed-block Laplace order. Hence
\[
 \mathcal C_R(\kappa)=\mathcal C_R(1)\quad\text{for every }\kappa>0.
                                                                    \tag{5}
\]
Let $H^+$ be the inverse of $H$ on the orthogonal complement of its gauge
kernel, extended by zero on that kernel. Exact orthogonal decomposition
at the critical minimum gives
\[
 G_\kappa=H^++\kappa^{-1}Dg^2D^*.                                 \tag{6}
\]
All tensors and $g$ in (4) are independent of $\kappa$, and (4) has degree
at most three in $G$. Thus substitution of (6) gives a literal polynomial
\[
 \mathcal C_R(\kappa)=\mathcal C_{R,\perp}
       +\kappa^{-1}A_1+\kappa^{-2}A_2+\kappa^{-3}A_3.
\]
Equation(5) proves
\[
 \boxed{A_1=A_2=A_3=0,\qquad
 \mathcal C_R=\text{the complete expression (4) with }G=H^+.}      \tag{7}
\]
These are exact cancellations, valid at every finite $R$, of ALL
longitudinal vector insertions in the first coefficient, not estimates
on separate diagrams. The scalar ghost propagator $g$ and the Haar and
Jacobian vertices remain; (7) does not remove them.

No growing gauge parameter is inserted into the original measure:
first establish the coefficient identity for each fixed $\kappa$, then
use its finite polynomial dependence. In particular no uniform-in-$R$
Laplace remainder is smuggled into the limit. Differentiating (7) in the
background retains the derivatives of the physical projection in $H^+$,
as well as those of $D,g$ and every vertex. Replacing them by derivatives
of a fixed transverse basis would omit partners.

Every finite-$\kappa$ computation also checks $d^*G_\kappa d=\kappa^{-1}$.
This is a test of the cancellation, not its general proof.

\paragraph{Remaining scale-sensitive term.}
The full first coefficient is now expressible with physical relative
vector propagators, scalar ghost propagators, local original action/Haar
vertices and the connected differentiation formula of the connected first nonlinear flux response calculation. Gauge modes
are not the remaining obstruction. The all-cross cubic term has three
propagators between two local vertices; its source derivatives involve a
joint multivertex current, not the single return loop controlled in the mixed boundary magnetic loop bound calculation.
The transverse completed mean-current term and the quartic/Jacobian/Haar
partners must be estimated together. Neither transversality nor (7)
implies a logarithmic bound on that combination. A uniform physical-energy
estimate and a uniform original differentiated remainder remain open.
No global holonomy sector is replaced or removed, and no S94 or scale
induction is asserted.
