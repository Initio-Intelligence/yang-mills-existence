\title{Difference entropy on the full cube fiber without a false convexity claim}\author{}\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle
The sharp convex native domain in \cite[Section 3]{ND} differs from the complete compact Wilson fiber in the complete conditional cube calculation. This note proves a nearby-bath difference estimate on the latter and shows explicitly why a uniform global Poincare claim cannot be imported from the former.

\section{Differences are controlled by actual conditional moments}
Take two baths in the fixed admitted neighborhood of the complete conditional cube calculation, with coordinate distance $\delta$. Put $\Delta S=S_{b_1}-S_{b_0}$ and interpolate the actual actions on the same compact cycle:
\[
 S_t=S_{b_0}+t\Delta S,\qquad
 K_t=D_t^{-1}e^{-\beta S_t}dU,\qquad w=\beta\Delta S.
\]
Although $S_t$ need not equal the action at a linearly interpolated bath, it remains uniformly close to the flat action in every fixed derivative norm. Thus the same unique-global-minimum, local Hessian and compact exterior-gap proof applies uniformly in $t$. In particular, for its actual minimum $U_t^*$,
\[
 K_t\operatorname{dist}(U,U_t^*)^2\leq C/\beta.
 \tag{1}
\]
Smooth dependence of the 54 fixed plaquette words on the bath gives
$\|\nabla_U\Delta S\|_\infty\leq C\delta$. Subtracting its value at the minimum proves
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_{K_t}(w)
 \leq K_t|w-w(U_t^*)|^2
 \leq C\beta\delta^2.
 \tag{2}
\]
This uses neither global convexity nor a spectral-gap estimate.

Exact exponential-family identities now yield
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(K_0\Vert K_1),\ D_{\rm KL}(K_1\Vert K_0)
 \leq C\beta\delta^2,
 \tag{3}
\]
\[
 \log(D_1/D_0)=-\beta K_0\Delta S+R,
 \qquad0\leq R\leq C\beta\delta^2.
 \tag{4}
\]
The complete first-order scalar remains. It includes the order-$\beta$ induced bath action found in the induced boundary coupling calculation. Entropy controls its nonlinear remainder, not the absolute action.

For an actual common carrier globally Lipschitz in the free group variables with constant $L_F$, (1) gives $\operatorname{Var}_{K_t}F\leq CL_F^2/\beta$. Covariance Cauchy--Schwarz and (2) therefore imply
\[
 |K_1F-K_0F|\leq CL_F\delta.
 \tag{5}
\]
This is sharper than the total-variation bound for such a regular carrier. A changed carrier contributes its full $K_1(F_1-F_0)$, and old mesh factors remain in $L_F$. These estimates compare changes; they do not treat the whole boundary interaction as small.

\section{An explicit obstruction to transferring native global convexity}
For $N\geq5$ let $z=e^{2\pi i/N}$ and fix the literal flat bath. Set every free cube link equal to $zI$. Each internal plaquette is the identity, while the 48 exterior single-link plaquettes have action $1-\cos(2\pi/N)$. This is a stationary point of the complete conditional action with positive energy
\[
 E_z=48[1-\cos(2\pi/N)].
\]
Its exact Hessian is
\[
 H_z=N^{-1}[4\cos(2\pi/N)I_{12}+d^Td]\otimes I_{d_G}>0.
 \tag{6}
\]
Hence it is a secondary strict local minimum. The identity configuration remains the unique global minimum; there is no contradiction with the Laplace estimate of the complete conditional cube calculation.

Choose a smooth function equal to one near this secondary minimum and zero outside a slightly larger small ball. Strict local convexity supplies a fixed barrier $b>0$ on the support of its gradient. The variance is bounded below by $cD_\beta^{-1}e^{-\beta E_z}\beta^{-n_i/2}$, while its Dirichlet form is bounded above by $CD_\beta^{-1}e^{-\beta(E_z+b)}$. Therefore the optimal full-fiber Poincare constant obeys
\[
 C_P(\beta)\geq c\beta^{-n_i/2}e^{\beta b},\qquad n_i=12d_G,
 \tag{7}
\]
for sufficiently large $\beta$. The normalized mass of this well is exponentially small, but the relaxation out of it is also exponentially slow. Thus a good approximation in probability by one Gaussian does not prove a uniform spectral gap for arbitrary full-fiber observables.

The native law of \cite[Section 3]{ND} is restricted to its original sharp convex small-field coordinate domain and has a verified pointwise Hessian floor there. The secondary well in (6) lies outside that domain. Its Poincare theorem is not contradicted; the example identifies the domain hypothesis that must be preserved. Equations (2)--(5) remain valid on the full compact fiber because they estimate the specified smooth action differences by actual distance moments, rather than arbitrary observables by a global gap.

\section{Implication for S94}
If an original complete-record comparison supplied a small bath mismatch $\delta$, (3)--(4) would control the entropy and second-order scalar remainder with the actual factor $\beta\delta^2$. No distance decay is generated here. Generic fluctuation-sized mismatches $\delta\asymp\beta^{-1/2}$ leave order-one entropy, and the first-order scalar remains. Matching copied records and controlling the induced bath action and wrapped records are still required for the full pressure comparison.
