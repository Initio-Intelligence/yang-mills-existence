\paragraph{Analysis of the actual flat compact transfer.}
For the flat-bath compact transfer operator, the principal-log Haar scalar is
$(2\pi^2)^{-1}B^{-3/2}$, the exterior factors give an integrable Gaussian majorant,
and both Hilbert--Schmidt and trace convergence hold. The limiting three-dimensional
Mehler ratio is $3-\sqrt8$. Using the EXACT leading eigenvalue after these bounds,
not a per-step asymptotic for it, is what makes its conclusion uniform in chain
length. This is a conditional one-link trajectory, not a replacement of the
full gauge theory or its global sector by three free rotors.

\paragraph{The four actual SU(2) staples combine exactly.}
For a selected spatial link at time $t$, write its four original incident spatial
plaquette actions as $s(u S_{t,r}^{-1})$, $r=1,\ldots,4$, after the literal
orientation adjustments; $s(u)=1-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(u)/2$.
The $S_{t,r}$ are the actual unitary bath staples. Using unit quaternions, put
\[
 \sum_{r=1}^4 S_{t,r}=\kappa_t W_t,\qquad
 0\le\kappa_t\le4,\quad W_t\in SU(2)\quad(\kappa_t>0).
\]
Linearity of the trace gives the exact identity
\[
 \sum_r s(u S_{t,r}^{-1})=(4-\kappa_t)+\kappa_t s(uW_t^{-1}). \tag{1}
\]
The scalar $4-\kappa_t$ is part of the ORIGINAL conditional normalization and
must not be discarded. No claim that arbitrary SU(N) matrix sums have this
scalar-unitary form is made.

\paragraph{Compatible transport is an exact Haar change, not deletion of a twist.}
Let the temporal plaquette between $u_t,u_{t+1}$ have action
$s(u_t g_t u_{t+1}^{-1}h_t)$ with the actual temporal bath links $g_t,h_t$.
Assume the literal centers satisfy
\[
 W_t g_t W_{t+1}^{-1}h_t=I.                            \tag{2}
\]
Set $R_0=I$, $R_{t+1}=g_t^{-1}R_t$, $L_t=W_tR_t$, and change variables
$u_t=L_t w_tR_t^{-1}$. Left/right Haar invariance has unit Jacobian. Equation(2)
implies $h_t=L_{t+1}L_t^{-1}$, and consequently the onsite and temporal actions
become $s(w_t)$ and $s(w_tw_{t+1}^{-1})$ respectively.

For a period $m$, $W_m=W_0$ but the frames need not close. Write
$G=g_0g_1\cdots g_{m-1}$. Then $R_m=G^{-1}$, $L_m=W_0G^{-1}$ and the ORIGINAL
periodic condition $u_m=u_0$ becomes
\[
 w_m=G w_0G^{-1}.                                    \tag{3}
\]
Thus the Polyakov transport is retained as an endpoint conjugation. Compatible
nontrivial $G$ is realized, for example, by a flat spatial bath and constant
temporal link $P$ with $P^m=G$. It has not been gauged to the identity.

\paragraph{Uniform strength and an exact twisted trace estimate.}
Suppose $\kappa_t=\kappa\in[\kappa_*,4]$, $\kappa_*>0$. Define the actual
symmetric positive transfer
\[
 T_{B,\kappa}(u,v)=e^{-B\kappa s(u)/2}
                 e^{-Bs(uv^{-1})}e^{-B\kappa s(v)/2}.
\]
With $C_G f(w)=f(G^{-1}wG)$, the original selected-chain normalization is
\[
 Z_m(\omega)=e^{-B m(4-\kappa)}
             \operatorname{Tr}(T_{B,\kappa}^{m}C_G).  \tag{4}
\]
Any fixed-bath factors outside this selected chain remain separately in the
full bath measure. Formula(4) retains all scalar and holonomy data of this fiber.

The compact-transfer proof of the exact flat bath compact transfer calculation is uniform for $\kappa\in[\kappa_*,4]$.
After the same exact coordinate change its limit has kernel
\[
 H_\kappa(x,y)=\exp[-(\kappa+2)(|x|^2+|y|^2)/4+x\cdot y]
\]
and Mehler ratio
\[
 q_\kappa=\frac{\kappa+2-\sqrt{\kappa(\kappa+4)}}2<1.
\]
The uniform external majorant is
$\exp[-\kappa_*(|x|^2+|y|^2)/\pi^2]$; uniform convergence on compact sets and
uniform tails prove operator/trace convergence uniformly in $\kappa$.
The limiting leading eigenvalue is continuous and strictly positive on this
interval. Thus for any fixed $r>q_{\kappa_*}$ with $r<1$, for sufficiently large
$B$ uniformly in $\kappa$, the ORIGINAL eigenvalues satisfy
$\lambda_1/\lambda_0\le r$, $\operatorname{Tr}T/\lambda_0\le1+C$.

The positive unique leading eigenfunction is conjugation invariant, because
$T_{B,\kappa}$ commutes with every $C_G$. Hence it contributes exactly
$\lambda_0^m$ to the twisted trace, and unitarity on the remaining eigenspaces gives
\[
 \left|\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(T_{B,\kappa}^m C_G)}{\lambda_0^m}-1\right|
 \le C r^{m-1},                                     \tag{5}
\]
uniformly in the ACTUAL $G$. The twisted trace is a positive path integral,
but its excited spectral terms can have either sign. It is not called the purity
of a positive density operator. For $Cr^{m-1}\le1/2$, (5) bounds its logarithmic
error by $2Cr^{m-1}$. Repeating the same bath doubles the scalar in(4) and replaces
$G$ by $G^2$; these original factors, rather than an invented common normalization,
are the ones to compare.

\paragraph{Scope.}
This extends the exact flat conditional result to transported centers, coherent
nonzero net staple strength, and retained nontrivial holonomy. It is an actual
pointwise conditional statement. It does not show that the original random bath
satisfies(2) or has constant strength; zero strength and arbitrary time-dependent
frustration are excluded here. It does not estimate the remaining bath affinity.
The next note treats a genuine time-dependent small perturbation without
pretending that a product of different transfers has a stationary spectrum.
