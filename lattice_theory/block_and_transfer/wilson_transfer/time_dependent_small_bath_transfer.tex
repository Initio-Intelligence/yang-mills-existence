\paragraph{Actual kernels and the precise small-bath class.}
Keep one selected spatial-link trajectory in the ORIGINAL $SU(2)$ Wilson measure.
Its four spatial staples at time $t$ are $S_{t,r}$, and its temporal twists are
$g_t,h_t$. With $s(u)=1-\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}u$, define
\[
 M_t(u)=\exp[-\tfrac B2\sum_{r=1}^4s(uS_{t,r}^{-1})],\qquad
 T_t(u,v)=M_t(u)e^{-Bs(ug_tv^{-1}h_t)}M_{t+1}(v).       \tag{1}
\]
Every original onsite scalar is inside $M_t$. Bath-only action outside this
selected trajectory remains in the original bath density.

Assume, in a declared ORIGINAL frame or after an explicit left/right Haar
transport as in the compatible staples and holonomy calculation, that the principal-log vectors satisfy
\[
 S_{t,r}=e^{i\mathbf s_{t,r}\cdot\sigma/\sqrt B},\quad
 g_t=e^{i\mathbf g_t\cdot\sigma/\sqrt B},\quad
 h_t=e^{i\mathbf h_t\cdot\sigma/\sqrt B},\qquad
 |\mathbf s_{t,r}|,|\mathbf g_t|,|\mathbf h_t|\le\delta.  \tag{2}
\]
The parameters may vary arbitrarily with time; no stationary spectrum is assumed.
A retained endpoint holonomy is allowed as $C_Gf(u)=f(G^{-1}uG)$ with arbitrary
$G\in SU(2)$. For a period $m$, the exact conditional period operator is
\[
 W_m=T_0T_1\cdots T_{m-1}C_G.                         \tag{3}
\]
A plain original periodic chain has $G=I$ and $M_m=M_0$. If a transported frame
leaves $G\ne I$, its endpoint consistency is the literal one from that transport;
(3), not a discarded holonomy, is used. The kernel is strictly positive, but
$W_m$ need not be self-adjoint. Denote its exact positive Perron eigenvalue by
$\rho_m$.

\begin{theorem}
There are $\delta>0$, $B_0<\infty$, $C<\infty$ and $r=2/5<1$ such that every
actual kernel sequence satisfying(1)--(2), $B\ge B_0$, obeys
\[
 \left|\frac{\Tr W_m^q}{\rho_m^q}-1\right|
       \le C r^{mq-1},\qquad m,q\in\mathbb N.          \tag{4}
\]
All constants are independent of the period length, the time-dependent parameters
and the retained conjugation $G$. The leading scalar is the EXACT Perron scalar
of the WHOLE product, not a product of local eigenvalue approximations.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Step1: uniform control of the actual rescaled operators.}
Use the exact Haar coordinates of the exact flat bath compact transfer calculation,
$du=c_Bj_B(x)dx$, $c_B=(2\pi^2)^{-1}B^{-3/2}$,
$u=e^{i x\cdot\sigma/\sqrt B}$, $|x|<\pi\sqrt B$.
After the unitary coordinate map, $c_B^{-1}T_t$ has a kernel $H_{B,t}$ on this
ball, extended by zero to $L^2(\mathbb R^3)$.
Its uniform compact-parameter limit has kernel
\[
 \exp\left[-\tfrac14\sum_r|x-\mathbf s_{t,r}|^2
            -\tfrac12|x-y+\mathbf g_t+\mathbf h_t|^2
            -\tfrac14\sum_r|y-\mathbf s_{t+1,r}|^2\right].       \tag{5}
\]
This is a kernel limit used to bound operators, not a replacement of the original
partition integral.

The unitary norm triangle inequality gives
$s(uS^{-1})\ge\tfrac12s(u)-s(S)$. Thus
$M_t(u)\le e^{\delta^2}e^{-Bs(u)}$ and the rescaled kernels have the common
integrable majorant
\[
 |H_{B,t}(x,y)|\le e^{2\delta^2}
              e^{-2(|x|^2+|y|^2)/\pi^2}.              \tag{6}
\]
Uniform convergence on bounded sets, followed by the uniform tails in(6), proves
Hilbert--Schmidt convergence uniformly over the compact parameter set(2).
At zero parameters the limit is the pinned Mehler operator $H_0$ with
\[
 \ell_0=\left[\frac\pi{3/2+\sqrt2}\right]^{3/2},\qquad
 S_*=H_0/\ell_0,\quad \|S_*\|=1,\quad
 \|S_*|_{v^\perp}\|=3-\sqrt8<1/5,                   \tag{7}
\]
where $v$ is its normalized positive radial ground vector. Taking $\delta$
sufficiently small and then $B_0$ sufficiently large proves
\[
 \|S_t-S_*\|\le\epsilon=1/20,
 \qquad S_t=c_B^{-1}\ell_0^{-1}T_t
 \quad\hbox{in the unitary rescaled coordinates}.     \tag{8}
\]
The phrase in parentheses is essential: the displayed scalar does not replace
the coordinate unitary or its Haar Jacobian.

There is also a uniform trace-norm bound $\|S_t\|_1\le K$.
Indeed the twisted temporal operator is $Q^{1/2}U_tQ^{1/2}$, where $U_t$ is a
left/right Haar unitary commuting with central convolution $Q$.
Hilbert--Schmidt factorization gives
\[
 \|T_t\|_1\le
  (\int M_t^2du)^{1/2}(\int M_{t+1}^2du)^{1/2}
 \le C c_B.                                         \tag{9}
\]
Here $k_B(I)=1$ for the original temporal kernel, and
$\int M_t^2\le e^{2\delta^2}\int e^{-2Bs(u)}du\le Cc_B$.
No trace estimate depends on how many steps will later be multiplied.

\paragraph{Step2: common cone and a product Perron lower bound.}
Work in the real Hilbert space, and let
$\mathcal C=\{a v+z:a\ge0,\ z\perp v,\ \|z\|\le k a\}$, $k=1/4$.
From(7)--(8), the new $v$ coefficient under any $S_t$ is at least
\[
 a'\ge[1-\epsilon(1+k)]a=\alpha a,\qquad\alpha=15/16,
\]
while its orthogonal part has norm at most
$[\epsilon+(1/5+\epsilon)k]a\le k\alpha a$.
Thus every $S_t$ preserves this cone and increases its positive coefficient by
at least $\alpha$. The same holds for each adjoint. Since $C_Gv=v$ and $C_G$
is unitary, it also preserves the cone.

Put $B_m=S_0\cdots S_{m-1}C_G$, with reference $S_*$ as in(7).
Repeated cycles preserve the cone and give
$\|B_m^n v\|\ge\alpha^{mn}$. Hence its spectral radius $\rho\ge\alpha^m$.
At fixed cutoff the actual compact-group product has a strictly positive kernel;
its positive right and left Perron vectors exist uniquely. Iteration from $v$
and from $v$ under the adjoint places both in the closed cone. Their rank-one
spectral projection $\Pi$ consequently has
\[
 \|\Pi\|\le\frac{1+k^2}{1-k^2}=17/15=:C_\Pi.        \tag{10}
\]
The zero extension outside the principal-coordinate ball only adds zero modes;
it does not change this Perron statement on the original compact-group space.

\paragraph{Step3: a trace bound for the whole product, not accumulated step errors.}
Exterior powers give
\[
 \|\wedge^2S_t\|\le1/5+2\epsilon+\epsilon^2=121/400=:\kappa,
 \qquad \|\wedge^2S_t\|_1\le K^2/2.                  \tag{11}
\]
The first inequality follows by subtracting the two tensor squares; the second
follows from singular values, $\sum_{i<j}s_is_j\le(\sum_i s_i)^2/2$.
Thus
\[
 \|\wedge^2 B_m\|\le\kappa^m,\qquad
 \|\wedge^2 B_m\|_1\le(K^2/2)\kappa^{m-1}.           \tag{12}
\]
These are product identities/trace-ideal estimates, valid without self-adjointness
or commutativity of the steps.

Here is a direct way to pass to the Perron complement. Normalize its right vector
$v_m$ to unit norm and its left functional $l_m$ by $l_m(v_m)=1$.
On $\mathcal H_0=\ker l_m$, $Jx=v_m\wedge x$ has norm at most one and inverse
on its range of norm at most $C_\Pi$. If $R=B_m|_{\mathcal H_0}$, then
$(\wedge^2B_m)J=\rho J R$. Equations(10)--(12) imply
\[
 \|R/\rho\|\le C_\Pi(\kappa/\alpha^2)^m\le r^m,
 \quad
 \|R/\rho\|_1\le C(\kappa/\alpha^2)^{m-1}\le Cr^{m-1},  \tag{13}
\]
where $r=2/5$ works since
$C_\Pi\kappa/\alpha^2<2/5$.
The bounded direct-sum decomposition into the Perron line and $\mathcal H_0$
gives $\Tr B_m^q=\rho^q+\Tr R^q$.
Using $|\Tr R^q|\le\|R\|_1\|R\|^{q-1}$ proves(4).
Returning to original coordinates multiplies $W_m$ and its Perron eigenvalue by
the SAME $(c_B\ell_0)^m$, so(4) retains the exact raw scalar. In particular no
$O(\delta)$ or $O(B^{-1/2})$ vacuum error has been multiplied by $m$ and dropped.

\paragraph{Interpretation and precise limits.}
The actual periodic conditional normalization is $\Tr W_m>0$.
For non-self-adjoint or holonomy-twisted products, excited traces need not be
nonnegative, so (4) is an absolute relative-error bound, not a claim that this
ratio is a positive-density purity. Once $Cr^{mq-1}\le1/2$ it gives the
corresponding logarithmic error bound $2Cr^{mq-1}$.
At physical trajectory length $m\asymp t_*/a$, the error is summable even after
multiplication by a polynomial number of independent selected trajectories.

The hypotheses concern an ACTUAL explicit small tube of bath staples and twists.
No probability lower bound for that tube under the original random bath has
been proved, and demanding it at every time may be costly. Coherent large
transported centers are covered only after their exact frame change and retained
endpoint holonomy have been verified. Arbitrary unscaled frustration and the
remaining bath-affinity factor of the conditioned wilson chain affinity calculation are outside this theorem. The result
advances the conditional spectral estimate to time-dependent original kernels;
it does not close the full physical-torus S94 comparison or a mass gap.
