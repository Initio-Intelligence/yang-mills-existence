\section*{A genuine vacuum-weighted exponential bound}
For $0\le\theta\le1$, define the actual spatially deformed transfer
\[
 A_\theta=e^{\theta V/2}A e^{\theta V/2}
 =e^{-(1-\theta)V/2}QP e^{-(1-\theta)V/2}.
\]
Since the finite-cutoff action is bounded, every multiplication below is bounded. Gauge invariance ensures it preserves the original physical space. The Rayleigh principle and the equal nonzero spectra of $CC^*$ and $C^*C$ give
\[
 \lambda\int\psi^2 e^{\theta V}dX
 =\langle\psi,A^{1/2}e^{\theta V}A^{1/2}\psi\rangle
 \le\|A^{1/2}e^{\theta V}A^{1/2}\|
 =\|A_\theta\|.                                      \tag{1}
\]
In particular $A_1=QP$, so
\[
 E_{\psi^2}e^V\le c/\lambda\le1/z,\qquad
 \mathbb P_{\psi^2}\{V\ge u\}\le\min(1,e^{-u}/z).     \tag{2}
\]
The last inequality uses the actual trial lower bound $\lambda\ge cz$. Neither Haar measure nor a free toron measure replaces the ground law. The scalar $c$ cancels only between the two bounds. More precisely, for any $\theta\in(0,1]$,
\[
 \mathbb P_{\psi^2}\{V\ge u\}
 \le e^{-\theta u}\|A_\theta\|/\lambda.               \tag{3}
\]
The partition-pressure difference in (3) is retained; it has not been estimated by zero. On a compact group of dimension $d_G$, the flat-ball estimate gives $-\log z\le (d_G/2)L\log\beta+CL$ for $\beta\ge1$, with $L=3n^3$. Thus (2) genuinely suppresses action much larger than this cutoff-extensive threshold, but does not itself give a physical-volume occupation bound.

\section*{Why ground typicality does not center the needed row observable}
For any positive continuous invariant trial $f$ let
\[
 R=Af/f,\qquad P_f(X,Y)=A(X,Y)f(Y)/(R(X)f(X)),
 \qquad p_0(X,Y)=A(X,Y)\psi(Y)/(\lambda\psi(X)).
\]
Their stationary probability laws are $\nu_f\propto f^2R$ and $\pi=\psi^2$. Put $h=\psi/f$. The exact ratio is
\[
 \frac{p_0(X,Y)}{P_f(X,Y)}=\frac{R(X)}\lambda\frac{h(Y)}{h(X)}.
\]
All ratios and logarithms are bounded at fixed cutoff. Stationarity cancels the difference of $\log h$ in the integrated transition entropy, proving
\[
 E_\pi\log(R/\lambda)
 =\int\pi(dX)\,D_{\rm KL}(p_0(X,\cdot)\Vert P_f(X,\cdot))\ge0,\tag{4}
\]
\[
 E_{\nu_f}\log(\lambda/R)
 =\int\nu_f(dX)\,D_{\rm KL}(P_f(X,\cdot)\Vert p_0(X,\cdot))\ge0.\tag{5}
\]
These are opposite biases under different ORIGINAL induced laws, not interchangeable concentration centers. Even a useful action tail (2) cannot be substituted for a bound on the accumulated positive loop fluctuation in the original trial ground row ratio calculation. For $f=M$, $R_M(X)\le c$ pointwise, so the unconditional upper bound is only $\log(R_M/\lambda)\le\log(c/\lambda)\le-\log z$. This remains cutoff-extensive. Suppression of the rough LOW-row fields in \texttt{original\_trial\_ground\_row\_ratio.tex} does not estimate the HIGH-row contribution. No assertion is made that it diverges in the true slab measure.

\section*{Analysis of the open-slab replica calculation}
The filtered wilson slab replica calculation holds on its stated finite-cutoff scope. For $f_s=A^sM$ and $Z_k=\langle M,A^kM\rangle$, the identity $D_s=Z_{2s}Z_{2s+2}/Z_{2s+1}^2-1$ is exactly the relative row variance under $f_s^2/Z_{2s}$. The stationary law is instead $f_sf_{s+1}/Z_{2s+1}$, under which $\operatorname{Var}(\log R_s)\le D_s$ follows from two independent copies and $\cosh u\ge1+u^2/2$. Log-convexity gives
\[
 \sum_{s<S}\log(1+D_s)\le\log(\lambda/(Z_1/Z_0))\le-\log z.
\]
The temporal scalar cancels exactly in the final inequality. The mean-bias sign in that note is consistent with (5), not with replacing its law by $\pi$. The resulting budget is cutoff-extensive and controls neither the loop return law nor states orthogonal to the boundary vector $M$.

\section*{Scope and remaining indispensable estimate}
Equations (1)--(5) are actual interacting Wilson bounds and identities without a gap assumption or limiting vacuum construction. They preserve the original global sector and raw normalization. They do not prove the cutoff-uniform ground-relative thermal trace required by S94. This trial route now requires a quantitative bound for the original vacuum-weighted return kernel, or for both the trial-loop exponential and its return trace; ground spatial-action typicality alone does not supply either. At present there is no proved estimate in the supplied material that closes this step.
