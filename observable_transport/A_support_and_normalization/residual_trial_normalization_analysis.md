\[
 C_h\subset C_h^{+3}\subset A:=Z''_{h+1}
     \subset C_h^{+8}\subset Z_{h+1}\cap Y.

The literal p.195-196 annular preparation makes A one rectangle per final component. Thus all ancestors are in one actual h+1 component, whose size is at most 100 a_{h+1}. This does not bound the much older rank of the original T_h and does not contradict the large-rank example RN.2.

The normalization and geometry extend from a single unmerged witness to **every class produced by the literal per-component rectangular-chain preparation**.
