# The literal narrow regular cutoff: an exact compact product kernel

## Result and scope

For the literal later regular cutoff in CMP119 (3.16), the free variable has already been scaled in (3.15), but its printed good radius is delta_k=g_k P_k. On this convention the exact regular conditional law admits a particularly simple reference: the normalized product of its actual free-coordinate balls.

The retained linear force is small on those balls. There is no displaced complex Gaussian, no determinant branch for a new reference covariance, and no lower bound on the unnormalized volume of the cutoff event. The volume factors cancel exactly at each fixed retained datum.

The theorem below proves the normalized unmarked and one-root compact-product expansion from genuine zero-source physical interaction coefficients. It resolves the additional Gaussian-reserve issue U2 in regular_marked_root_with_payload.md for this literal narrow-cutoff sector. It does **not** supply missing physical carriers or retained currents for a completed inherited boundary operation. Those are separate inputs U1/U3, and their source-specific status is retained.

This note does not apply the narrow radius to the initial broad-cutoff convention, and it does not repair a printed radius by inference.

## 1. The exact fixed-geometry probability

Let I be the actual independent integrated free-coordinate blocks in the regular region. Each block has fixed real dimension d_i<=d_*. Write its literal ball as

\[
 D_i=r_i B_i,\qquad
 0<r_i\le c_D g_k P_k,
 \tag{CP.1}
\]

where B_i is the fixed source unit norm ball. The radii, norm conventions and coordinate dimensions are fixed external labels on this geometry. They do not vary with the continuous retained datum.

Define

\[
 \pi_D(dz)=\bigotimes_{i\in I}
       \frac{{\bf1}_{D_i}(z_i)\,dz_i}{|D_i|}.
 \tag{CP.2}
\]

The actual law is

\[
 q_v(dz)=
 \frac{e^{H(z;v)}\pi_D(dz)}
      {\pi_D(e^{H(\cdot;v)})},
 \quad
 H=-\tfrac12z^TK_{II}(v)z+b(v)^Tz+
              W(z;v)-W(0;v).
 \tag{CP.3}
\]

The retained force b=-K_IO y is present. W is the complete action in C07/C08, including old Gaussian-density responses, E/B/R, coarea and the actual relocation. Every factor depending only on v cancels in this conditional quotient and remains in the outer measure.

Indeed the original Lebesgue numerator and denominator both contain exactly

\[
 \prod_{i\in I}|D_i|
   =\prod_{i\in I}r_i^{d_i}|B_i|.
 \tag{CP.4}
\]

Their cancellation proves (CP.3). It does not estimate (CP.4), and it does not bound it below by a fixed-radius Gaussian event. Any source coarea or normalization factor depending only on the retained datum is treated in the same exact manner, without a second count of its powers of g.

The source fit of (CP.1) is literal: CMP119 (3.15) has already made A_k=g_k A_k, and (3.16) then prints the test |A_k(b)|<delta_k. The C-07 construction deliberately retains that printed convention. The initial construction has its separately recorded radius and is not included by changing its notation.

## 2. Smallness of the exact force and quadratic action

The actual current precision has uniformly bounded entry rows on the admitted source geometry. The actual retained current exterior satisfies |y_i|<=C P_k, with the fixed A_0/A_1 ratio included. Therefore

\[
 |b_i|\le C P_k,\qquad
 |b_i z_i|\le C g_kP_k^2,
 \qquad
 |z_i K_{ij}z_j|\le
 C g_k^2P_k^2\,\|K_{ij}\|.
 \tag{CP.5}
\]

Both g_kP_k^2 and g_k^2P_k^2 tend to zero. This remains true at a site immediately adjacent to the retained exterior; a small conditional Gaussian mean is not required.

To obtain a carrier theorem, retain the actual coefficient decompositions. Suppose

\[
 K_{ij}(v)=\sum_\alpha K_{\alpha,ij}(v_{X_\alpha}),
 \qquad
 b_i(v)=\sum_\alpha b_{\alpha,i}(v_{X_\alpha}),
 \tag{CP.6}
\]

where each full connected physical carrier X_alpha contains the matrix endpoints it uses and every raw dependency footprint. Different coefficient tags remain distinct. Define

\[
 k_\alpha=\sum_{i,j}\|K_{\alpha,ij}\|,
 \qquad f_\alpha=\sum_i |b_{\alpha,i}|.
 \tag{CP.7}
\]

The indices in these sums are the current independent coordinate blocks actually touched by that tag. A source operator row bound becomes such a scalar coefficient bound after the fixed polynomial endpoint count is paid by spare carrier decay.

For rates nu_0>nu>0 assume the actual coefficient budgets

\[
 \sup_b\sum_{\alpha:\,b\in X_\alpha}
 e^{\nu_0(|X_\alpha|+t(X_\alpha))}k_\alpha\le C_K,
 \quad
 \sup_b\sum_{\alpha:\,b\in X_\alpha}
 e^{\nu_0(|X_\alpha|+t(X_\alpha))}f_\alpha
       \le C_b\,{\cal P}_k .
 \tag{CP.8}
\]

Here mathcal P_k is an exposed fixed polylogarithm. If y is an independent retained current coordinate in the chosen filing, one can take mathcal P_k=P_k in the second estimate. If the displayed y includes the actual exterior relocation as a derived map, its whole raw carrier family is included in (CP.6). The C05 larger native tube may conservatively give mathcal P_k=C ell_k^q instead of P_k. This is still a fixed polylogarithm; its powers are not hidden in a scale-independent constant.

The quadratic and force vertices of (CP.3) consequently have the small value budget

\[
 \epsilon_{\rm quad+force}
 \le C\{g_k^2P_k^2 C_K+
                g_kP_k\,C_b{\cal P}_k\}
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{CP.9}
\]

Thus the possible loss of the sharper complex mean estimate is harmless here.

## 4. A compact-product theorem with an explicit convergence reserve

The following finite-volume theorem is independent of the special source notation. Its reference is exactly (CP.2).

Let B be the block graph of current physical variables. A vertex alpha has a connected carrier X_alpha, and a bounded complex function h_alpha on the product cutoff and retained domain. Put

\[
 H=\sum_\alpha h_\alpha,\qquad
 \|h\|_{\kappa,a}
 =\sup_b\sum_{\alpha:\,b\in X_\alpha}
   e^{\kappa(|X_\alpha|+t(X_\alpha))+a|X_\alpha|}
   \sup|h_\alpha|.
 \tag{CP.13}
\]

Every finite auxiliary field or local compatibility collar is included in X_alpha. Coefficients with the same set but different origins are not merged before taking this norm.

### Theorem CP.1

For each fixed a>0 and graph degree there is epsilon_*>0 such that, if
||h||_{kappa,a}<=epsilon_*, the following hold.

1. The exact partition function pi_D(exp H) has an absolutely convergent connected polymer logarithm and is nonzero on the entire specified complex coefficient family.
2. The complete normalized law (CP.3) has an exact rooted expansion for every single inserted function supported on a finite carrier Z.
3. If that root has absolute majorant M_Z on the product support, the sum of its absolute rooted coefficients, with a fixed smaller physical carrier weight w_{kappa'}, is at most C w_{kappa'}(Z) M_Z exp(c epsilon_* |Z|). Equivalently the bound C M_Z exp(c epsilon_* |Z|) uses the relative weight w_{kappa'}(Y)/w_{kappa'}(Z). The original root weight is present even when H=0. There is no factor epsilon_*^{-1}.
4. The same assertion holds for separately inserted direct or kernel-score records whenever their actual compatible coefficient majorants are supplied. Their initial support is the full union of the insertion, record and coefficient footprints, and its weight is retained in the corresponding bound. Kernel normalization is differentiated as part of the exact ratio.

All constants are independent of |I|, the radii r_i, and the product volume in (CP.4).

**Proof.** For each primitive vertex put

\[
 f_\alpha=e^{h_\alpha}-1,\qquad
 \|f_\alpha\|_\infty
       \le e^{\|h_\alpha\|_\infty}\|h_\alpha\|_\infty .
 \tag{CP.14}
\]

Expand the finite product of 1+f_alpha. Group a chosen set of vertices by the connected components of its overlap graph. Since pi_D is a product probability measure, integrations over disjoint component supports factor exactly. This gives the standard hard-core polymer partition function, with each polymer activity the sum of the integrals of the connected overlapping vertex sets assigned to it.

The positive connected-set/tree bound for these activities uses their suprema before integration. At a root block the sum of a new attached vertex is at most a fixed multiple of ||h||_{kappa,a}; the sum over its possible attachment blocks costs |X|. The factor exp(a|X|) in (CP.13) pays those incidence factors by the ordinary rooted tree induction. Choosing epsilon_* below the resulting fixed threshold gives the Kotecky–Preiss inequality with, for example, half of that a reserve left. The connected Ursell series is then absolutely convergent and equals the partition logarithm first near zero, hence throughout the same polydisc by normal convergence. Its exponential is nonzero. All estimates are performed with nonnegative majorants; no cancellation of different tags is used.

For a marked root, replace one factor in the same expansion by the root function and retain its full support. Every connected component containing the root is a root polymer. The identical positive tree induction, starting with w_{kappa'}(Z) M_Z instead of an unmarked vertex amplitude, costs at most exp(c epsilon_*|Z|). Submultiplicativity of the physical weight on overlapping connected unions retains that initial factor and assigns the remaining weights to the attached activities. Clusters disjoint from the root cancel through the exact partition-function ratio. Equivalently apply the exact rooted identity SR20.9–SR20.12 with this product-reference activity family. This proves both equality and the stated absolute bound.

No step divides by an individual unmarked h_alpha or f_alpha. The argument remains valid when H=0 and only the root is present. It therefore has no inverse-small-activity loss.

A direct record is another single root. A kernel variation differentiates one actual density coefficient, and the denominator produces its centering term. At finite differences use the rectangular two-copy convention M.30 and the telescoping product formula SR20.17–SR20.19. The same rooted tree bound applies to the one distinguished current insertion with its separately supplied norm. This proves the fourth assertion without identifying the direct and kernel records. ∎

The proof can also be applied after a finite exact resummation of primitive vertices. It must retain a true supported activity family satisfying (CP.13); an estimate only for the final sum H is insufficient.

## 6. Ambient extension on fixed geometry

Compact support has another useful consequence. Suppose the actual supported action coefficients and the reconstruction have been extended on a fixed geometry, by local real cutoffs, to bounded real functions with the same carrier budgets. Then (CP.3) defines a positive normalized kernel everywhere on that ambient retained space. Off the admitted support the extended quadratic coefficient need not remain coercive: H is bounded on each finite product cutoff, and Theorem CP.1 provides the uniform normalized expansion.

This permits coefficientwise real extensions without a new global positive-precision construction. However it does not create their retained derivative bounds, and the reconstruction used by every generated mark must be the same extended map. A generated scalar is not independently redefined after integration.

For the incoming current compact links, one possible reconstruction convention is a unitary local reference lift multiplied by exp(i Theta_ext), where the real extended correction is Hermitian and agrees with the actual source correction on the active buffered chart. The retained cutoffs and each correction coefficient carry their full raw footprints. The source's fixed-L current link comparison supplies this chart on the active regular region. Actual gauge and old-payload conventions must be kept in its chosen implementation.

The theorem here needs only the exact on-support law and is not itself a proof that every full inherited action has already received this compatible ambient extension.

## 7. Different geometries and normalizers are separate

Equation (CP.4) cancels for each individual fixed-geometry conditional. It does not say that unnormalized masses for two geometries have the same product volume or the same coarea factor. Those factors remain in the source history law.

For equal coordinate dimensions but different fixed radii, the change z_i=r_i u_i transports each normalized ball law to the same normalized unit-ball law. The factors r_i^{d_i} cancel within that conditional. Its finite kernel current is still the difference of the two complete pulled-back functions and potentials. If coordinates are activated or removed, a common padded unit-ball construction is possible only after the actual coordinate map, inactive convention and full geometric dependency footprints are specified.

In particular this observation does not eliminate an M03 discrete history current, and it does not repair a discontinuous default inactive-fiber extension at a continuously crossed selector boundary.
