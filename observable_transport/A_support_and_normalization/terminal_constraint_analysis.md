# Analysis of the terminal constraint section

This note analyses [terminal constraint chart](terminal_constraint_chart.md),
TQ.1–TQ.15. The local terminal section and its polynomial
coarea ledger hold on the admitted promoted raw chart, with the explicit
available-exceptional argument below. This discharges the terminal
coarea part of ER's coordinate condition; admission of the full
restored chart and the energy identification remain separate.

## 1. Source geometry and depth

The source LFR I (1.10), (1.73) and quiet CMP119 (3.5) give
S=C+B_{8M}, Λ=S⊖B_{4M}; hence C+B_{4M}⊂Λ without any false
distributivity of erosion. All earlier Z_j lie in C. Fixed current
averaging footprints outside Λ have bounded current size, absorbed
by M. Therefore they meet only O(log R) native levels, as TQ.1–2
state. The deeper N=R ancestry inside Λ cannot enter this local
terminal inversion. PM.12–14 records the same proof.

## 2. Why the chosen exceptional entries are available

This point needs more than short depth. Let Ψ_j be the promoted
master sequence. At the top level a terminal bond assigned to Ψ_k
is already a held current raw datum; its equation is redundant in
the conditional reference measure and is not another delta to solve.
A nonredundant bond c is absent from the incident-bond set Ψ_k.
Since Ψ_k is a union of aligned blocks, both adjacent averaging
blocks then lie in Ψ_k^c, in the available Y footprint.

Choose the actual face-crossing b₀(c). At a lower level, either the
selected bond is assigned to Ψ_j, in which case it is a variable
j-shell raw entry (it is outside Ψ_{j+1}), or it is absent from
Ψ_j, in which case both of its lower adjacent blocks are again in
the finer complement. Recurse in the second case. The descendants
stay inside the original two-current-block footprint and its fixed
incidence collar. They cannot hit a held raw exterior entry. PM.13
ensures termination before reaching the older fine cores. A fixed
current average on another bond remains fixed because the one-step
footprint contains no exceptional b₀(c′) from a different coarse
bond. Thus the section simultaneously respects all unchanged coarse
values.

This proof uses full promoted raw variables before any additional
forest gauge is imposed. An extra gauge may be imposed afterward
by its exact Haar/Faddeev–Popov identity, or must be shown to
preserve the terminal centers. It is not permissible to choose an
exceptional entry which has already been independently specified by
an unrelated tree convention.

## 3. The one-step coefficient is correct

In CMP98, printed p.36, equation (122) has the
factor L multiplying Q(V₀)A; the flat first term (125) weights each
translated path by L^{−(d+1)}. A face exceptional bond is used by
L starting points. Thus the derivative of the actual raw-angle
logarithmic average is

L · L^{−(d+1)} · L = L^{1−d},

as TQ.3 states. The other linear terms in (124) vanish at flat
background and are O(C_L ε) on the source regular chart. The
coefficient therefore has a fixed positive inverse bound. The
actual one-step reconstruction solves the nonlinear group average,
with its exceptional determinant retained; QCA=0 is not substituted
for the nonlinear equation.

## 4. Coarea and normalization

The backward depth is O(log R), so the crude fixed-L inverse and
complex radius grow/shrink by at most a fixed power of R. The
finite-dimensional right-inverse splitting in TQ.9–12 then gives
an exact nonlinear terminal fiber, with reciprocal chart radius
polynomial in R and the actual native dimension D. The constant
linear coarea factor is bounded by the right-inverse singular-value
estimate, and the nonlinear determinant remains I+T with
||T||≤1/2 on the smaller chart. This gives the stated
O(D log(R+D+2)) nonconstant ledger.

The g^d coordinate factors and g^{−r} constraint factors must remain
in the original source normalization exactly once. Redundant deltas
are removed by disintegration, not evaluated at the identity. On
this reading TQ.13–15 give a genuine coarea estimate and do not
bound a delta by a Haar volume.

The application still requires the same admitted physical chart
for its action and selectors. TQ is a local section theorem. It is
not a proof that arbitrary restored data satisfy every historical
small-field cutoff, that all remaining completed densities coincide,
or that all alternative failures have the required energy cost.

## Conclusion

The quantitative fixed-terminal constraint/coarea input holds
