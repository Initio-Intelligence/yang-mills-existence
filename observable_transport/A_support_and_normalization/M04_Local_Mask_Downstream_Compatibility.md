# Local masks: exact downstream compatibility and the erasure gate

This note examines what the MASK and GMK constructions
can supply to the M.10/M.28 algebra and to
the M06/M07/H04 induction interfaces. It does not alter the M04 statement or
claim a proof of it.

**Conclusion.** The local-mask kernels can feed the same exact
value/direct/current algebra while the masks are retained as primitive
coordinates. An unused old history can be erased at zero mark cost if
the whole tagged triple and the continuing kernel genuinely factor
through the kept state. Erasing the local masks and returning to their
derived-history representation is not a bounded norm conversion.
Averaging masks in a genuine reset requires a new posterior-current
estimate; GMK or MP does not supply it.

## 1. Which existing interfaces carry over

Use the primitive mask state and full padded regulator of
[M04 Local Mask Interface](M04_Local_Mask_Interface.md) (MASK). The actual law is unchanged on
its consistency graph. The carrier exponent still charges current
block volume and tree length, but a mask is now a local primitive
coordinate rather than a derived domain with the old construction
footprint.

The following statements carry over algebraically, with those explicit
changes of coordinate/footprint/version data:

* **M.10:** δA=B+J is an exact identity for each tagged activity.
* **M.23–M.25:** Hölder grading, finite-difference products and the
  carrier-union weight inequalities use no independence of the state
  law and are unchanged.
* **M.28–M.32:** reconstruction is an explicit pullback; its normalized
  conditional law has a signed finite-difference current of mass zero.
  Changing rank is handled by the common padded space or its physical
  pushforward.
* **M.35–M.39:** coordinatewise reset projectors give the same compatible
  localization identities. MASK LM.16–LM.17 uses only coordinates
  actually present in the coefficient, preventing an unused globally
  stored flag from creating an artificial closure cost.

These are an **amended M03-form interface**. They are not the unchanged
specified M03 norm: MASK §7 proves that restriction back to the old
derived-history coordinates is not uniformly bounded.

GMK supplies a complete absolute physical-carrier value/current bound
for the primitive untruncated flat Gaussian quadratic mark, conditional
on its stated actual outer moment reserve for the averaged part. The
same result holds with its local saturated retained chart. MP supplies
its compact-product bound under the displayed genuine primitive
coefficient/current assumptions and the literal narrow cutoff. Both
are valid inputs to the amended algebra at their stated scopes.

Neither theorem, merely by having compatible output records, is an
operator theorem for every recursively transported M03/M06 triple.
An old direct/current record must be transported separately through
M.30. A primitive physical-root filing with B_new=0 does not authorize
discarding or relabeling pre-existing records. The actual input
regulator pullback and the M.28a chart-path cost are still required.

## 2. Erasing an unused old history while retaining masks

Write the amended current state as (v,m,h), where h is a collection
of old history coordinates to be erased and (v,m) is kept. Let
π_{v,m}(dh) be the **actual** conditional posterior, with all original
source weights and normalizers retained.

### Lemma DC.1 — transparent erasure

Suppose every tagged coefficient and its direct/current records have
an actual common ambient factorization

\[
 A_X(v,m,h)=a_X(v,m),\quad
 B_X(v,m,h)=b_X(v,m),\quad
 J_X(v,m,h)=j_X(v,m).
 \tag{DC.1}
\]

Assume the numerical native coordinates and regulator needed by those
tags are retained. Choose the ambient erasure version to act on
these common functions, including zero-posterior labels. Then erasing
h preserves each tagged triple and introduces no new kernel current.
At the same physical scale its amended norm is unchanged.

**Proof.** Normalization gives π_{v,m}a_X=a_X. For any kept-coordinate
perturbation, the generated posterior current is

\[
 (\pi_{\tau(v,m)}-\pi_{v,m})a_X(v,m)
       =a_X(v,m)(1-1)=0.
 \tag{DC.2}
\]

The old direct and current records are constant in h and are preserved
as well. All discarded-coordinate differences were zero. The kept
coordinate marginal, its pointwise expression, shifted regulator and
carriers are identical. Consequently the moment and pointwise norm
terms agree. ∎

This is a supported identity, not an appeal to probabilistic decorrelation.
The posterior may have arbitrarily complicated dependence on (v,m).
It cancels because the particular triple does not use the erased
variable. Independence only after summing different tags is
insufficient for an absolute tagged bound: (DC.1) must hold for the
representation whose separate records are being charged, or a new
compatible representation must actually be constructed.

At the first flat common chart, GMK and MASK LM.19 do not use the
original R labels once the independent mask and retained numerical
array are fixed. Thus DC.1 applies to those label directions. In MP,
old history may still be among the parameters p of the complete
primitive density. It can be erased by this lemma only after an
actual factorization removes that dependence. Its name or storage
location does not establish (DC.1).

### Continuing the chain requires a separate sufficiency identity

A zero-cost mark erasure at one stage does not by itself make (v,m)
a sufficient Markov state for all later source operations. If T is a
future transition and p is the proposed retained projection, a simple
sufficient condition is

\[
 p_*T((v,m,h),\cdot)=\overline T((v,m),\cdot)
 \quad\hbox{for every admitted }h.
 \tag{DC.3}
\]

Then integrating h gives the same transition, and induction proves
all finite projected path and marked identities. This is the usual
direct kernel factorization, proved here simply by conditioning and
iterating the equality. It is not implied by (DC.1) for one mark.

If (DC.3) fails, an exact conditional mixture at one marginal time
need not preserve every multitime marked law under subsequent
iteration. The actual hidden posterior must be retained, or the
correct enlarged/path conditional construction must be supplied.
Introducing that posterior as one global coordinate would have its
own support cost. General source reset/old-history sufficiency has
not been proved by GMK or MP.

## 3. Erasing masks back to derived geometry is unbounded

Suppose the old history h is retained and the actual law satisfies
m=M(h). Erasing the redundant primitive mask is exactly

\[
 (Ef)(v,h)=f(v,M(h)).
 \tag{DC.4}
\]

For source order one, take the dimension-four normalized function
f_h=h⁻⁴m_b with a local anchor at b. Its amended norm is bounded
by a fixed constant: after multiplication by the norm's h⁴ factor,
its value and single binary difference are at most one. Its moment
bound does not require any property of the state law. This also
specifies the native-unit normalization of the membership-bit
comparison in MASK §7; for the unscaled bit, both norm bounds acquire
the same factor h⁴.

For the actual first-step R histories in the GO proof, the membership
M_b(h) changes near the extended hole face when a remote R seed is
added. All nearby primitive data are unchanged; the actual shifted
native regulators are at most C(1+P²). GO.19 gives

\[
 \|E f_h\|_{\rm frozen\ M03}
 \ge c\frac{e^{c'\kappa MR_1}}{1+P^2}.
 \tag{DC.5}
\]

Thus (DC.4) is not uniformly bounded from the amended norm into the
specified norm. This uses actual positive source sectors, not a synthetic
probability example. Law-level redundancy of m is not norm redundancy.

Consequently an argument cannot use GMK/MP to obtain cheap local-mask
carriers, discard those masks as redundant metadata, and invoke the
old specified M04/M06 estimate on the result. The old remote-history
footprint returns exactly at that interface.

## 4. A genuine reset which averages masks

Let a source reset retain y and average u, where u includes old
numerical data, histories and masks. Its correct marked output is

\[
 (Q_R A)(y)=\pi_y(A),
 \tag{DC.6}
\]

for the actual normalized posterior π_y. The exact finite difference is

\[
 \delta_\tau Q_RA
 =\pi_{\tau y}(B_\tau+J_\tau)
    +\frac{\pi_{\tau y}-\pi_y}{t}A,
 \tag{DC.7}
\]

with denominator one for a discrete direction. The last term can be
centered by subtracting π_yA. It does not vanish merely because A had
a small carrier in its primitive mask coordinates.

A sufficient downstream reset theorem must bound the actual
inclusion-exclusion signed kernels of M.37–M.39, now for π_y and
the amended input carrier/regulator. In particular it must pay:

1. the pushed-forward input regulator and every shifted version;
2. the direct and previously accumulated current records;
3. the new normalized posterior current in (DC.7);
4. full physical output footprints and connector/volume weights;
5. the actual outer marginal moments and all source orders required
   by M06/H04, without replacing a normalized old posterior by fresh
   reference mass or using rarity twice.

The specified M.40 proof remains a valid *conditional criterion*: insert
the input pointwise regulator bound into the absolute signed kernel,
then sum its actual output weights. GMK estimates the ordinary
Gaussian integration kernel at a retained mask, and MP estimates its
compact analogue under its primitive assumptions. Neither estimates
the posterior π_y which erases that mask.

H03's law-level spatial cumulants remain true because the
positive law is unchanged. Its recorded scope, however, uses whole
component supports and a distance scaled by the source R geometry.
The H-03 statement explicitly leaves ambient currents, shifted regulators,
full-volume weights and all-history induction downstream. Those facts
do not provide the missing amended reset estimate in (DC.7).

### Fresh posterior resampling cannot undo the averaging

If a fresh descriptor m′ is sampled from a normalized law κ_y after
the old posterior has been averaged, the correctly transported old
mark is π_yA, independent of m′. This is another passive lift and
has zero fresh-mask current. It is not generally A(m′,y).

For any future test G(m′,y), replacing the correctly averaged mark by
the fixed-descriptor mark changes the integral by

\[
 \kappa_y(A G)-\kappa_yA\,\kappa_yG
       =\operatorname{Cov}_{\kappa_y}(A,G)
 \tag{DC.8}
\]

in the case where κ is the corresponding descriptor posterior.
Thus even equality of the one-mark mean would not justify that
replacement in all marked integrals. This is the same qualification
as the exact H03 resampled-descriptor construction: fresh labels
do not recover information about the erased old mark for free.

## 5. What final expectation can consume without conversion

There is one useful bound requiring no return to the old footprint
interface. If an already constructed source-order-m coefficient has
an absolute amended carrier representation, then its averaged value
part gives

\[
 \sum_Xw_h(X)|\mathbb E A_X|
 \le m^m h^{-4m}\sum_Xw_h(X)v_{h,m,X}(A_X)
 \le m^m h^{-4m}\|\mathbf A\|_{h,\kappa}.
 \tag{DC.9}
\]

Indeed use p=1 in M.8 and |A_X|≤A_X^sharp. The expectation is
under the unchanged actual supported law. No independence of masks
and fields is assumed.

This pays the **linear** final average of a full coefficient already
constructed with the required absolute budget. It does not permit
commuting a logarithm or a connected cumulant through that final
average. Outer correlations of conditional means can connect
previously disconnected tags. Their connector/volume costs are the
H05 and full-jet reset obligations, not consequences of (DC.9).

Likewise a function local in the amended mask state is not automatically
a local function of the original physical compact links: on support
the mask was constructed from the distant primitive tests. Physical
support assertions which use that old reconstruction require its
actual pullback or posterior influence estimate. The exact marked-law
identity alone does not supply it.

## 6. Concrete downstream gates

| Interface | What is now proved | What remains before downstream use |
|---|---|---|
| Passive source selection/lift | MASK LM.1 preserves every old tagged norm and finite jet while all old coordinates remain | Do not remove old energies before actual elimination |
| M.10/product/localization algebra | Exact on independent local masks with full padded regulator | Use the amended footprint/version data explicitly |
| Ordinary Gaussian step | GMK full physical-carrier theorem for primitive quadratics, with outer reserve for averaged terms | Broad cutoff, complete nonlinear density, nonflat/reference variation, recursive decorated roots |
| Compact ordinary step | MP under its genuine primitive carrier/current hypotheses and literal narrow cutoff | Verify those hypotheses for every actual old-payload and completed boundary factor; retain current/rank normalizations |
| M.28 source chart | Exact signed-current identities in either stated filing | Actual χ path/regulator and post-relocation conversion bounds; carry old records |
| Erase unused old h, keep m | DC.1 gives identity and no new current | Per-tag factorization and future-kernel sufficiency DC.3 |
| Erase m, return to derived h | Exact formula DC.4 | No bounded general conversion: DC.5 is an actual obstruction |
| Joint reset/erasure | Exact posterior formula DC.7 | Full amended M.37–M.40 weighted kernel/current estimate |
| M06/M07/H04 iteration | Same formal finite-jet and law identities | Uniform all-order budgets, all sectors, old-mask counts and actual outer responses |
| F06/F07/S06/hierarchy | Exact physical observables and numerical normalizations are unchanged | Contact/spatial-order and weighted remainder estimates in the revised induction; no unproved return to the original support |

The compatible route is to keep the independent masks through every
step where their local carrier theorem is used, erase only genuinely
unused histories by DC.1, and prove an actual reset theorem before
averaging mask-dependent marks. That route is mathematically
well-defined. The GMK and MP results do not yet close it.
