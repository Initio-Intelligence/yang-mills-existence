# Regular marked roots with a retained old payload

## Outcome and scope

This note supplies the marked-root part of a regular one-step argument. It gives a genuine input-side analytic budget, a source-buffer reduction which avoids adding unlisted mixed-plaquette energies to M-03, and an absolute marked Gaussian/Mayer estimate. The hypotheses on the complete **unmarked** regular action are stated separately. They include its true physical supports, normalized Gaussian reserve and current majorants; no marked-root bound or desired output estimate is assumed.

The primitive incoming Wilson scalar satisfies the analytic input budget below, with its original scalar and identity coefficients exposed. A general previously transported input can be used if it has that budget. Proving its propagation through earlier resets is not part of this regular one-step result.

The admitted fixed-geometry native theorem is distinct from the full ambient extension and discrete history currents. The complete inherited B action must satisfy the unmarked assumptions in the actual chosen filing. Those issues are not declared solved here.

## 1. A physical-tube incoming budget

Let `h=L^k eta` be the incoming current spacing and `h'=Lh`. A current compact-link fluctuation is written `exp(i g_k z_e)U_e`; the fixed source normalization has `beta_k g_k^2` bounded above and below by positive constants. Old retained coordinates are denoted by `w`. They remain actual arguments of the incoming function, not averaged under a new reference law.

Give the incoming scalar a specified tagged expansion

\[
 F_k(U,w)=\sum_{X,\ell}F_{X,\ell}(U_X,w_X).
 \tag{RP.1}
\]

The carrier contains every true dependency footprint, including any old coordinate used by that tag. Keep its compatible input direct and old-current records. Write `s_X=|X|+t_h(X)`.

Fix a physical tube size `gamma_k` which contains `C_* epsilon_k` and `C_* g_k P_k`, where `C_*` is chosen from the source reconstruction/decoupling constants before the coupling ceiling. It can be capped by a fixed small geometric constant. The following is an **input** condition, expressed in raw incoming coordinates:

\[
 |F_{X,\ell}(e^{i g_k z}U,w)|
 \le b_{X,\ell}h^{-4}
 \left[1+E_X(U,w)+\sum_{e\in X}|z_e|^2\right]
 \tag{RP.2}
\]

on the stated compact-centered tube `g_k|z_e|<=gamma_k`. A fixed stencil enlargement is implicit. The function is holomorphic there. The old retained slots which are complexified for a continuous-current estimate have their separately specified incoming buffers; their actual physical labels are retained. Its two input record families have the corresponding degree-3/2 bound with coefficients `b^B_{X,ell}` and `b^J_{X,ell}`. They are not combined before norms.

One useful explicit budget is

\[
 \mathcal B_{\rm in}
 =\sum_{X,\ell}e^{\kappa_{\rm in}s_X}
       (b_{X,\ell}+b^B_{X,\ell}+b^J_{X,\ell}),
 \tag{RP.3}
\]

with the chosen input moment budget and the true incoming law recorded separately. The spatial slack required below is explicit. It can come from a stronger fixed input rate, or from the geometric contraction of the block cover under `h -> Lh`. It is not hidden in the marked output.

This hypothesis is stronger than a fixed **normalized** radius about one compact center. The source decouplers can move a background by `O(epsilon_k)=O(g_k P_k)`, rather than merely `O(g_k)`. The required input domain must admit that actual displacement.

### Lemma RP.1 — the primitive scalar has this budget

For the exact F-01 scalar

\[
 F_h(U)=h^{-4}\alpha\beta_k
             \sum_{p\in\mathcal P(S)}a_p s(U_p)-c,
 \qquad s(P)=N-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}P,
 \tag{RP.4}
\]

the estimate (RP.2) holds on any fixed sufficiently small physical tube, with

\[
 b\le C\bigl(|\alpha|+h^4|c|\bigr).
 \tag{RP.5}
\]

The constant is independent of `g_k`, scale and compact center.

**Proof.** Use the holomorphic extension `mathsf s(P)=N-tr(P+P^{-1})/2`. On the radial path which inserts the factors `exp(i t g_k z_e)`, the first trace derivative at a compact center has a traceless generator. Subtracting the identity gives the bound

\[
 |\partial_t\mathsf s(P_t)|_{t=0}
 \le C g_k\sqrt{s(P_0)}\sum_{e\in\partial p}|z_e|.
 \tag{RP.6}
\]

Every compact factor has norm one and the added factors have norm bounded by a constant depending only on the physical tube and the fixed plaquette stencil. Hence

\[
 |\partial_t^2\mathsf s(P_t)|
 \le C g_k^2\left(\sum_{e\in\partial p}|z_e|\right)^2.
 \tag{RP.7}
\]

Taylor's formula, `beta_k g_k^2=O(1)` and `2ab<=a^2+b^2` prove (RP.2). The number of links per plaquette is fixed. The original constant `c` contributes exactly `h^4|c|` to the coefficient. No final running normalization is used. The ordinary normalized link differences follow from the same calculation, or from Cauchy on a fixed spare normalized subdisc. ∎

## 2. Which regular roots are changed by the operation?

 QF(y)=q_y\bigl(F\circ\chi(\cdot,y)\bigr).

### Lemma RP.2 — the regular integration buffer is literal

CMP119, printed p.269, (3.20), gives

 \Lambda_{k+1}
 \subset\Omega_{k+1}^{\sim-2}.
 \tag{RP.9}

The removed layers have side `LMR_{k+1}` in the current units. Replacing a small component of `Lambda_{k+1}^c` by its rectangular hull can only shrink `Lambda_{k+1}`. Thus every integrated current coordinate, after its fixed averaging/constraint stencil is included, is separated from `Omega_{k+1}^c` by `cMR_{k+1}` current/coarse units, after fixing the source geometric constants sufficiently large.

**Proof.** The displayed formula is the complement of the twice-enlarged `Omega_{k+1}^c` together with the enlarged R-test cover. Both operations enlarge the complement. A bounded local stencil consumes only a fixed number of current cells. ∎

This assertion does not depend on whether the normalized good R radius is the literal smaller one or an intended rescaled one. It is a geometric statement about the source-selected domains.

## 3. Primitive raw support and two different energy estimates

Use the graded compact bundle reference GR.1 on the admitted mixed determining support. Its local graph construction has bounded raw arity at each native scale; its mixed force and averaging mismatch are retained in the source variational equation. The background fixed point and the exact current constraint map are decoupled by the same source component construction as in CMP116 §1. Choose a common fixed enlargement for their primitive blocks.

The construction has the following consequences, used before any Gaussian integration.

1. Every decoupled component is a function only of the raw determining and retained free coordinates in its declared physical footprint. This follows from the component equations and the raw-local reference, not from a numerical decay estimate.
2. On the enlarged decoupler polydisc the source fixed point has a fixed contraction constant. Its force and mismatch input are not dropped.
3. The actual current reconstruction differs from its decoupled background by at most `C g_k` times the local current free-coordinate norm. The map keeps the critical-cancelled normal form of the **law** separate from the reconstructed field used by the mark.

The statement below uses only these primitive variational/constraint facts; it does not require a completed old-R quotient.

### 3.1 Interior components

Suppose the component and its fixed primitive halos do not meet the mixed determining interface. On the relevant current/coarse region the raw determining data are the current coarse compact links. The GR local loop defects reduce to a fixed sum of their ordinary plaquette defects. Its projection, connection and exact local mismatch satisfy a quadratic energy bound by those defects: every formula is uniformly Lipschitz about the flat local comparison, and it vanishes there.

Here `a_bg` means the **relative reconstruction/nonlinear correction in the gauge-compatible local frame**, including the actual local averaging mismatch. It is not the absolute connection potential. A flat connection with nontrivial transport can have zero curvature energy and a nonzero absolute potential; no estimate of that potential by curvature is used.

The force-containing contraction then gives

\[
 \beta_k\,|a_{\rm bg}|^2
   +\beta_k\sum_{p\in X}s(U_{{\rm ref},p})
 \le C(1+s_X+n)^q E_Y(y).
 \tag{RP.10}
\]

Here `n` is the number of added source localization blocks, `Y` is their full footprint together with the incoming carrier, and `E_Y` contains only the actual output current/coarse plaquettes and retained current free-coordinate squares in that footprint. Held old payload energies already used by the input remain the same native quantities, with their true footprints. The fixed exponent `q` allows replacing maxima by local sums; it is not an exponential-in-depth constant.

To see the scale cancellation explicitly, a connection curvature controlled by a local coarse holonomy defect at scale `h'` contributes `O((h/h')^2)` times that defect to a current plaquette. The current/coarse ratio is the fixed `L`. The ordinary source average estimates are uniform in the microscopic depth. Also `beta_k/beta_{k+1}` is bounded in the admitted one-step coupling window. Thus the right side of (RP.10) has the actual output energy normalization.

If the current/coarse reconstruction uses retained current fluctuations outside the integration domain, their contribution is `C sum |a_i^{ret}|^2`, by the exact local `C`/constraint map. These squares are actual M.5 entries. A mixed loop is not independently inserted into the regulator by this argument.

Combining (RP.2), (RP.10) and the fluctuation estimate gives

\[
 |F_{X,\ell}(\chi_Y(z,y))|
 \le C b_{X,\ell}h^{-4}(1+s_X+n)^q
       [\widehat R_{h',Y}(y)+\sum_{i\in Y}|z_i|^2]
 \tag{RP.11}
\]

on such an interior component. The same estimate, with power `3/2`, applies to the incoming direct and old-current records.

### 3.2 Components reaching a mixed interface

This is a crude amplitude estimate, not an output energy identification. The constants of GR and of the source weighted fixed point are independent of depth. The source's old coupling comparison and the monotonicity of `g(log g^{-2})^{p_0}` at sufficiently weak coupling permit one common current ceiling; no product of one-step map constants is used.

By Lemma RP.2, a connected component containing an affected current root and reaching that interface obeys

\[

If the incoming carrier itself already reaches the interface, its size is included on the left. It is incorrect to claim that all the distance must then be paid by newly added blocks.

## 4. Paying the crude interface amplitude by actual spatial slack

Cauchy's decoupling estimate gives the factor `exp(-nu n)`, with a fixed rate obtained from the spare source polydisc. Tags and connected-set entropy are retained in the ensuing absolute sum. Suppose the input and output rates and physical footprint conversions leave fixed positive slack `delta_X,delta_n` such that

\[
 w_{h',\kappa}(Y)e^{-\nu n}
 \le C e^{\kappa_{\rm in}s_X}
           e^{-\delta_X s_X-\delta_n n}.
 \tag{RP.14}
\]

This is a deterministic carrier condition. For a strictly local primitive scalar, `s_X` is fixed and all interface distance is in `n`. For extended inputs one can require a stronger fixed incoming carrier rate; alternatively a sufficiently large fixed block factor gives slack under coarsening of a connected fine carrier. The chosen option must be stated in the one-step input budget.

### Lemma RP.3 — the buffer absorbs every fixed cutoff power

For each fixed `a,q` and positive slacks in (RP.14),

\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbf1_{\{s_X+C_{\rm geom}n\ge c_{\rm geom}R\}}
  P_0(g_k)^a(1+s_X+n)^q
          e^{-\delta_Xs_X-\delta_n n}\\
 &\hspace{25mm}\le C_{a,q}
          e^{-\delta_Xs_X/2-\delta_n n/2}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{RP.15}
\]

The constant is uniform in the admitted weak-coupling range.

**Proof.** The quantity `delta_X s_X+delta_n n` is at least a fixed positive multiple of `s_X+n` and, on the indicated event, of `R`. The source radius is at least a fixed positive power of `log g_k^{-2}`, whereas `log P_0(g_k)=O(log log g_k^{-2})`. Therefore any fixed power of `P_0` and any fixed polynomial in `s_X+n` are absorbed by half the exponential slack. On the remaining compact coupling interval increase the fixed constant. ∎

Applying this lemma to (RP.12) proves the same weighted/summed pre-Gaussian bound as (RP.11). In particular the unresolved mixed-plaquette dictionary is not needed for these buffered changed-root terms. It has not been asserted for an arbitrary unbuffered deterministic retained pullback.

## 5. Continuous direct envelopes before Gaussian integration

The primitive component maps admit simultaneous normalized raw-link polydiscs on their buffered source domains. The local compact reference has bounded raw arity, and its normalized physical increments are smaller than its geometric margin. Incoming old-coordinate analyticity is used only where it was explicitly included in (RP.2); this note does not create an old-A analytic tube for the completed unmarked boundary action.

The following elementary lemma is useful because M-03 sums separate finite-difference suprema.

### Lemma RP.4 — diagonal finite differences from a simultaneous polydisc

If `f` is holomorphic on `|z_i|<rho`, `rho>1`, with `|f|<=M`, then

\[
 \sum_i\sup_{0<|t|\le1}
       \left|\frac{f(te_i)-f(0)}t\right|
 \le\frac{M}{\rho-1}.
 \tag{RP.16}
\]

The estimate is independent of the number of scalar coordinates.

**Proof.** Write `f=sum_alpha a_alpha z^alpha`. For each degree `m>=1`, the homogeneous part has sup norm at most `M rho^{-m}` on the unit polydisc, by a radial Cauchy integral. Average it over independent `m`th roots of unity in the coordinates. Only the pure monomials `a_{m e_i}z_i^m` remain. The averaging is contractive, and the supremum of this diagonal polynomial is `sum_i |a_{m e_i}|`. Thus that sum is at most `M rho^{-m}`. Sum the diagonal Taylor series for the finite differences and then sum the geometric series over `m`. ∎

For group directions use ordered exponential coordinates, one scalar coordinate per specified generator. A pure coordinate axis is exactly the M-03 elementary shift. A fixed group/stencil norm conversion reduces the spare polydisc but introduces no volume factor.

For one elementary shift, the compact energy bound is `R(t y)<=C R(y)`. On a simultaneous polydisc in **current/current-coarse** coordinates it is instead `R(y(z))<=C[R(y)+|Y|]`, up to the fixed number of such coordinates per current block. The additional carrier polynomial is retained and paid by the spatial slack; it is not called a dimension-free energy bound.

Arbitrarily many held older coordinates may occur inside one current block. RP.4 is not applied by simultaneously shifting every one of those payload slots and then hiding their rank in `|Y|`. Use two copies: first change the held payload in the incoming function and transport its already budgeted direct/old-current records; then freeze that payload while varying the reconstruction's raw determining arguments. The latter variation uses the primitive map's simultaneous normalized domain and the current-field energy estimate above. An old-coordinate variation of the **kernel** remains part of U3. This keeps the existing incoming rank and footprint costs in their stated input records.

With these qualifications, RP.4 controls the direct reconstruction finite-difference envelopes by the same pre-Gaussian majorants, or the degree-3/2 bound for an incoming record. A smooth ambient retained cutoff cannot be treated as holomorphic: its explicit product differences must be added separately. If its supported coefficient and cutoff difference have the stated carrier majorants, the same argument applies to their two product-rule terms.

**U4. ## 7. Absolute marked Gaussian coefficients

### Theorem RP.5 — marked-root estimate on the stated regular family

Assume the genuine input budget (RP.1)–(RP.3), the admitted primitive source maps and buffer of §§2–4, the spatial slack (RP.14), and the complete unmarked inputs U1–U3. Then the source one-root numerator has exact tagged coefficients `R_Z`, with compatible direct, old-current and new-kernel-current records. They have absolute coefficient majorants with value grade one and record grade `3/2`. With U4, their M-03 sizes `r_Z` satisfy

\[
 \sum_Z w_{h',\kappa+\delta}(Z)\,r_Z
 \le C L^4\mathcal B_{\rm in},
 \tag{RP.17}
\]

for a fixed positive `delta` below the retained localization slack. The constant does not contain the inverse of an unmarked small-activity parameter or an old rare-sector mass. It is independent of cutoff depth and total volume in the stated admitted family.

**Proof.** First decompose each incoming tag through the primitive reconstruction and background component equations. The component formula is exact before its coefficient estimates. Cauchy yields `exp(-nu n)` for the added blocks. Interior components obey (RP.11); components reaching the mixed interface obey (RP.12) and then RP.3. Thus, after retaining a strict portion of the carrier slack, every pre-Gaussian tag has an absolute bound

\[
 a_{X,Y} b_{X,\ell}h^{-4}
 (1+s_X+n)^q\,[R_Y+|z_Y|^2],
 \tag{RP.18}
\]

where the weighted sum of `a_{X,Y}` is bounded by the right side of (RP.14), with a remaining exponential margin. The input direct and old-current records have the same bound with the appropriate power `3/2` and their separate coefficients. RP.4 gives the direct root pullback records where the common analytic input applies.

Insert this root into the **same** localized Gaussian integral and the same unmarked Mayer factors. First account for its exact conditional mean as required in U2, using `|z_Y|^2<=2|xi_Y|^2+2|m_Y|^2`. U2 then pays the centered fluctuation square by a trace polynomial in the local rank. For the degree-3/2 records it pays the corresponding third absolute Gaussian moment, again a fixed polynomial in the rank. For a kernel derivative it pays the quadratic score and its determinant trace while retaining the grade-1/2 retained coefficient from U3. Multiplication by the value root therefore has output grade `1+1/2=3/2`. The score's normalization term is included; it is not replaced by an uncentered action derivative.

For clarity, this step only uses inequalities of the form

\[
 \int (R+|z|^2)^a(1+|z|^2)^b
          e^{-c|z|^2}\,d\gamma(z)
 \le C_{a,b}(1+r)^{a+b}R^a,
 \tag{RP.19}
\]

with `R>=1`, fixed `a in {1,3/2}`, fixed nonnegative integer `b`, and local rank `r`. The unused quadratic reserve makes the constants uniform on the localized complex family. There is no requirement to exponentiate a degree-3/2 incoming current; it is one linear inserted root.

The connected marked Gaussian/Mayer expansion then attaches only unmarked activities to this root. The unmarked KP tree bound from U3 sums those attachments with at most an `exp(c epsilon |Y|)` loss and fixed polynomial factors for the specified current insertions. This is the usual one-root tree sum and is linear in the root majorant. It does not divide by an unmarked activity amplitude: a root may exist even when all unmarked activities vanish. The remaining source/input slack pays the attachment factor, all local-rank polynomials, physical footprint conversions and connected-set entropy. This proves the pointwise coefficient/regulator version of (RP.17).

Under U4 the value majorant has `L^p` growth `Cp`; the record majorants have `Cp^{3/2}`. The fixed polynomial carrier-size factors are paid by the same unused spatial slack. Multiplication by the output factor `h'^4` gives exactly `L^4 h^4`. This proves the averaged and pointwise parts of the root norm in (RP.17), with the input record types kept distinct. ∎
