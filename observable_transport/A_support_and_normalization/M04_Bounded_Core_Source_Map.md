# The prepared residual operation has a bounded integration core

This note examines the geometric premise of [bounded core quotient](bounded_core_quotient.md) against the literal Balaban operation. The premise is substantially correct **after the preliminary annular integrations**. Every continuous variable still integrated in the prepared component operation has its direct physical support in one rectangle of side at most 100M in current units. An arbitrary earlier rank can survive inside that rectangle; it does not force an arbitrary current physical integration footprint. The dependence on retained fields, retained geometric labels, and the already generated boundary interactions remains to be localized. No normalized marked estimate is inferred just from the support statement.

The most useful additional identity is that, on the literal quiet eligible branch, the final component Y is a deterministic function of its prepared core Lambda. This is a possible way to remove a redundant geometric argument, but it does not by itself prove the footprint of a resampled core label or the locality of its posterior.

## 1. Three stages which must be distinguished

Let Y be one eligible component at step k, put h=k-N and k0=k-N0, and use the source's common physical lattice when comparing domains at different scales.

1. **Before preparation**, the old operation T_k(Y) contains annular variables throughout Y. The preliminary integrations in LFR I pp.179-180 remove variables in Z_{j+1}\Z''_{j+1}, for h<=j<k. Their union is not confined to the eventual prepared core.
2. **After those N integrations**, the remaining determining sequence is B''_k and the new action contains the boundary terms generated by the removed annuli. Equations (1.71)-(1.72) of LFR I retain only the small nested regions Z''_j, together with the unchanged T_h(Z_h).
3. **The final prepared replacement** integrates the current field on Lambda and the remaining mixed old variables there. LFR I (1.76), followed by its exact coordinate changes and LFR II (1.71), is the operation to which the bounded-core quotient route could apply.

Consequently it would be false to say that the entire R preparation only integrates variables in Lambda. It is correct to say that the **remaining prepared old integral** has that property. Any incoming mark must already have been transported through the preliminary annular integrations, with their exact direct and kernel records.

The two displayed integration domains are disjoint. The first old-core integral is explicitly left unchanged. The nesting on pp.195-196 places Z_h and its inner preparation collar inside Lambda.

 \Lambda_0=\Lambda\cap\Omega''_{h+1}{}^{\sim2},
 \qquad B_0=\mathbb B''_k\cap\Lambda_0.

This is the printed p.195 definition. It should not be replaced by a shorthand with an unverified complement or padding exponent. The relative field satisfies

\[
 V''=V'V_0\quad\hbox{on }B_0,
 \qquad V_0=M_{\mathbb B''_k}(U_0).

Bonds crossing the outer boundary of Lambda belong to B0; bonds crossing the inner boundary of Lambda0 do not. Thus a fixed current-M collar covers the exact bond footprint. The tree T0 is one mixed-scale tree on B0. Its relative variables B or B' are supported there. The remaining V_h variables occur on the inner old region, including the collar exposed when B0 is separated from that region.

### Proposition CG.1 — direct support of the prepared old state

For a fixed literal eligible component, every raw compact variable, ordinary fluctuation variable, and mixed preparation variable still integrated in the final old operation is supported in a deterministic fixed current-M enlargement D0 of Lambda. In particular

\[
 |D_0|\le C_M
 \tag{CG.4}
\]

in current physical blocks, with C_M independent of k, N, the ultraviolet cutoff, the torus, and the number of coordinates in T_h.

**Proof.** The variables newly integrated at (CG.2) are confined to Lambda. The exact change to B0 and the last V_h integral only repartitions this domain; boundary-crossing bonds require at most the stated fixed collar. CMP119 (2.19)-(2.22), printed p.258, defines T_h(Z_h) as an operation supported in Z_h in the literal sense that its integrations and variables are restricted to that region. Any surviving earlier coordinates of that operation are therefore confined to Z_h, which is nested inside Lambda. Previously erased variables are not reintroduced: their effects are effective vertices in the action. The source's hard test footprints are included in its padded large-field regions; the explicit h-scale seam test has radius O(L^{-N}MR_h) in current units and is contained in the prepared collar because N is much larger than N0. This proves (CG.4). It is a support bound, not a bound on the old coordinate rank. \(\square\)

This reconciles the bounded-core route with Proposition RN.2 of [M04 Residual Normalizer Test](M04_Residual_Normalizer_Test.md). An enormous pre-h operation may survive without earlier replacement. Its raw rank can be arbitrarily large, while all those coordinates occupy a bounded current physical core after the quiet horizon shrinks their physical region.

## 4. The source already isolates the three remaining old-dependence channels

LFR II (1.50), printed p.370, writes the old reconstructed background using:

- the unchanged determining data outside Lambda;
- B in the prepared mixed annulus;
- the older inner variables V'' in the old core.

The immediately following sentence states that the old and new determining sets and fields **coincide on Lambda's complement**. Thus all changes in the reconstruction originate inside Lambda. Equations (1.51), (1.54), (1.57) and (1.58) separate the change into four precise variational comparisons:

1. Remove B.
2. Separate the older inner variables across the old core boundary, using the criticality identity (1.55).
3. Replace the remaining inner averages by the prepared reference V_Lambda.
4. Insert the fresh current field on Lambda.

Every source term depending on these changes can be written as an exact difference. For example, (1.59)-(1.60) subtract the same effective vertex evaluated with B=0. The remainder is localized by an expansion whose every nonzero carrier meets Lambda; the other terms vanish because B is zero off Lambda. The source gives full d_k carrier decay at this intermediate stage, before the later component contraction/resummation. The first, old-variable-independent term can be pulled out and canceled when normalizing the old law, provided the entire numerator/denominator use the same actual convention.

There are two useful consequences and one unfinished step.

First, preliminary boundary vertices lying far out in Y\Lambda do not automatically force an old integration footprint there. Their **variation with the remaining old state** is the relevant object. If their retained-only part cancels, the remaining dependence starts at Lambda and has a variational connection to their former localization domain.

Second, the opaque older T_h need not be expanded in its individual microscopic coordinates merely to obtain current physical support. It can remain inside the positive core reference law. What is required of that law is its actual marked moment and score budget, not a fictitious bound on its dimension.

The unfinished step is to construct the same exact localization **in raw retained variables**, for all three old-dependence channels and the complete boundary exponential, without the later forced inclusion of the whole component Y. The source begins the reconstruction of shell terms outside Y, then treats terms meeting Y by choosing a domain Y1 that contains the whole relevant component; see (1.61) and the resummation leading to (1.67)-(1.69). That final choice deliberately loses full physical volume control inside Y. It is not a proof that such control is impossible, but its published grouped estimate is not the coefficient estimate required by BC.2.

## 5. An exact geometric redundancy on quiet branches

Here the radius problem can be sharpened beyond the vague assertion that Y is a large retained label.

Let C be the union of the next-grid covers Z'_{k0} of all k0-scale ancestors of the final component Y. Its cube mesh in current physical units is exactly M, by the defining choice of N0. Work on the corresponding cube-index lattice. Write B_q for the integer sup-norm ball of radius q, write C+B_q for adding q cube layers, and write S minus B_q for erosion, meaning the set of cube indices z for which z+B_q is contained in S. In the quiet branch, CMP119 (3.2)-(3.5) gives the old hole

\[
 S:=\Omega_{k0+1}^c\cap Y=C+B_8.
 \tag{CG.5}
\]

The eroded-hole descriptions in LFR I (1.10),(1.73) give

\[
 Z''_k=S\mathbin{\ominus}B_5,\qquad
 \Lambda=S\mathbin{\ominus}B_4.
 \tag{CG.6}
\]

For an arbitrary union C it is not generally legitimate to infer Z''_k=C+B_3 or Lambda=C+B_4 by canceling erosion and dilation. The exact substitute is the following elementary identity. If S=C+B_8 and 0<=q<=8, then

\[
 C+B_{8-q}\subset S\mathbin{\ominus}B_q,
 \qquad (S\mathbin{\ominus}B_q)+B_q\subset S.
\]

The first inclusion, followed by dilation by B_q, gives the reverse inclusion for the second one. Hence

\[
 S=(S\mathbin{\ominus}B_q)+B_q.
 \tag{CG.6a}
\]

This uses only B_{8-q}+B_q=B_8 and is valid for disconnected unions as well. In particular S=Lambda+B_4 and S=Z''_k+B_5.

There is no clipping error from Y. Physical nesting places the pre-hull next region C+B_10=S+B_2 in final Y. Thus S has a regular two-layer collar inside Y. Erosion at a point of S cannot be changed by a foreign hole outside Y: any cube path from that point out of S meets the regular collar before it can leave Y. The source's complements and enlargements therefore give the same componentwise erosions in (CG.6).

The source's pre-hull next large-field region, CMP119 (3.20), is consequently

\[
 C+B_{10}=S+B_2=\Lambda+B_6.
 \tag{CG.7}
\]

The source states on p.192 that Lambda on one final component is one rectangle. Thus (CG.7) is already a rectangle and its rectangular-hull step is inert, giving Z_{k0+1} intersect Y=Lambda+B_6. Every possible joining ancestor was included in C; no new bad region is created during the remaining quiet interval. Every subsequent region is therefore the quiet S iterate: next-grid cover followed by ten layers of the new MR mesh. It follows that

\[
 Y=\mathscr D_k(\Lambda)
   :=S_{k-1}\circ\cdots\circ S_{k0+1}(\Lambda^{+6}),
 \tag{CG.8}
\]

with the evident empty-composition convention. The meshes, scale labels and radii entering this deterministic map are fixed source parameters.

### Proposition CG.2 — final-component redundancy

On every literal eligible quiet branch of LFR I Section 1, once the prepared rectangle Lambda and the scale parameters are fixed, the final component Y is fixed by (CG.8). Several different prepared cores can have the same Y, as in the residual-strata construction; the converse ambiguity does not occur in this branch.

**Proof.** The exact erosion-dilation identity (CG.6a), not a false inverse-dilation rule, gives (CG.7) for the union of all ancestors. The source's prepared Lambda is one rectangle, so all of that union is one rectangle by k0+1 and the hull step is inert. All ancestors of Y have been included, and no new P, Q or R region is introduced during the remaining quiet interval. Iteration of S proves (CG.8). This argument does not require that the older ancestry was already one component. A broader preparation with several separated prepared Lambda pieces would need its corresponding union-label statement. \(\square\)

This has an exact consequence for an ambient extension. If the actual quotient on active lifted fibers can be written as f(v,Lambda,Y) with Y redundant through (CG.8), it can be extended as

\[
 \widetilde f(v,\Lambda,Y)=f(v,\Lambda,\mathscr D_k(\Lambda))
 \tag{CG.9}
\]

wherever the right side has the admitted buffered extension. Such an extension is independent of the separately stored Y coordinate. One must still preserve the exact kernel normalization and whole finite source jet, as in RS.4.

Equation (CG.9) does **not** automatically assign the Lambda coordinate a bounded footprint. The following facts still need proof in the actual selected augmentation:

- that the resampled core label can be defined from a bounded physical old descriptor, with no surviving global eligibility flag in its definition;
- that its permitted old-history set and conditional measure have no extra dependence on remote geometric labels after the stated cancellations;
- that any within-support mixture of distinct fresh kernels is retained, or has a genuine common-kernel cancellation;

These are substantive locality identities. They cannot be replaced by merely storing the old Y flag at the core.

## 6. The localized action cutoff and the reference are not absent

The fresh h/H kernel contains the cutoff zeta_Y and the reference minimum U0. Within a common fresh-reference class that whole fresh factor cancels from the old quotient. However, the source classical cancellation also generates old-dependent boundary corrections involving the cutoff and the reference. These are among C_k and V in LFR II (1.49), not all common retained constants.

For instance, (1.5) and (1.29) isolate terms involving derivatives of the localized action cutoff near the outer boundary. The field being varied is supported in Lambda and the distance to that boundary is of order MR. The source uses the resulting exponential tail. That estimate is promising, but the unlocalized full component label cannot be charged to a thin carrier merely because its numerical coefficient is exponentially small in R: exp(-cR) does not pay exp(kappa R^4). Either (CG.8)-(CG.9) must genuinely remove the redundant label, or the cutoff must have an exact local geometric representation with all associated currents.

Similarly U0=U_{k,Y}(V_Lambda) is a variational function of the whole retained shell. It is not a core-only raw datum. The source variational expansions supply exponential influence from distant data, and the core criticality/Hessian estimate controls the minimizing reference. To use BC.1, one needs a core-local positive reference law and an exact expansion of the change from that law, with absolute coefficient and current sums. Numerical closeness of two completed quotients is insufficient.

## 7. What has and has not been established for the quotient route

The literal source fit now supplies the following verified facts:

1. The final prepared old integration state has bounded current physical direct support, despite arbitrary earlier rank.
2. In the native source coordinates the old hard integration support is fixed under the independent background variation used in LFR II (1.73)-(1.75).

These facts strengthen the bounded-core proposal. They do not yet prove BC.1-BC.3 for the actual residual posterior. In particular the following remains an explicit theorem to prove:

> After choosing and recording a permissible support-class lift and ambient kernel version, the complete normalized old law admits a core-local positive reference measure and a raw-retained, connected-carrier log-density expansion with uniformly small absolute weighted coefficients and the corresponding actual direct/kernel current bounds. The reference law has the required microscopic marked moment budgets, and all geometric dependence obeys the same bounded-core/localized decomposition.

The complete cross-boundary exponential must be included in this theorem. Its omission would replace the actual conditional by the self-operation. Successful-component geometry also remains in the H03 positive mixture and cannot be specified to a chosen success branch.

Assume the literal source preparation, including its inner per-final-component rectangular chain as used in CG.2. Conditional on the outer component admission, H_{k,r}(Y) can be represented as a finite union of sectors whose **undetermined geometric data and remaining old coordinates have direct footprints in a fixed enlargement D0 of Lambda**. They have no additional dependence on unreplaced exterior geometric values. All retained-background dependence not common to numerator and denominator remains in the source density and coordinate reconstruction.

**Proof.** There are four points.

First, the factorization at p.201 and the component sum (1.71) separate the outer admissibility of the chosen components from the internal descriptor sums. The restrictions of globally nested domains glue across the component boundary through the source's regular collar. CMP119 (2.1) gives nesting; its padding and the definition Y=Lambda_k^c put the current bad determining domain strictly inside Y. Second, for j<=k0 all old large-field sets Z_j belonging to this final component are physically nested inside their k0 ancestors. These ancestors lie in C, hence in Lambda. The undetermined complements Omega_j^c and the corresponding old S_j regions are inside the same padded old large-field regions. Their boundary tests use only a fixed enlargement of Lambda: in current units their test radii are bounded by a fixed multiple of L^{j-k}R_j, and the native scale comparison together with the definition of N0 bounds this uniformly for j<=k0. This statement also follows directly from the source's localized T_h and prepared determining set. Previous histories already erased by an R operation are not restored as new hidden coordinates.

Third, the later original large-field geometry is fixed by Lambda. At k0+1 the union formulas give Omega_{k0+1}^c=Lambda^{+4} and Z_{k0+1}=Lambda^{+6}; all subsequent quiet domains are deterministic. Their free-field collar labels have their prescribed good alternatives, since no new P, Q or R test is introduced in this interval. Fourth, the actual old native support after preparation consists of T_h's retained internal selectors, chi' on B0, and chi_{h,1/2}(1,V_h). Proposition CG.1 puts their direct footprints in D0. ## 10. A concrete local code and the finite discrete kernel algebra

Take one discrete coordinate at every current block x. Its finite alphabet consists of "empty" and the source's permitted rectangles having x as their **canonical corner**, of side at most 100M, together with the actual fresh graph/orientation and any remaining finite fresh choice. It does not contain arbitrary locations elsewhere on the torus. The data carried by one nonempty value have a fixed footprint D(x) of bounded current diameter.

For a padded array which proposes overlapping bounded cores, define one local priority operation: retain a proposed core exactly when its priority is smaller than every overlapping proposed core's priority. Priorities are fixed and ties are broken by the fixed anchor order. This one-round rule is local; it is not an iterated greedy maximal-set algorithm. Any two retained cores are disjoint, and every actual admissible array of disjoint cores is unchanged. A replacement at one block x can affect only proposed cores whose bounded footprints overlap D(x), hence only a fixed enlargement D*(x). No long-range recursion occurs.

For each admitted local label ell, CG.3 supplies a common padded old space U_ell. Embed all these spaces in one finite-cutoff product/disjoint-union space, keeping inactive coordinates at fixed reference values. The padding includes all ranks, finite old descriptor sectors, and the exact reconstruction maps; no common-rank differential is assumed.

There is a necessary distinction between a **fixed-native** reference and a **raw-core-local** reference probability. Substituting (U,J)=(1,0) into the p.379 comparison gives the first, with the same native hard support. It does not by itself prove the second. CMP119 p.253 explicitly says the conditional Gaussian expressions depend on the old background U1, and (1.26),(1.29),(2.21) retain their Schur precisions. LFR I p.194 leaves T_h unchanged. A dependence specified as an old parameter in the native continuation can still be a function of raw retained exterior fields. The statement that T_h integrates variables in Z_h is not a statement that every coefficient of its density is raw-local there.

A positive raw-local reference can be *chosen* on the localized padded support, for example by replacing all remaining exterior-dependent coefficients by a specified isolated-core reference and normalizing the resulting positive law, or by another explicit positive local density on the same support. Such a choice changes no actual law only when the **entire density ratio** is restored in the perturbation Sigma. Its smallness, its actual marked moment budgets, and its score bounds are not automatic. In particular, replacing only the displayed current native pair by (1,0) must not silently omit the old precision ratio. The actual old Schur/background localization is part of the raw carrier bridge still to be proved.

In the following finite algebra, nu_ell denotes a genuinely raw-core-local positive reference probability **once this reference and its exact residual density ratio have been specified**. Empty slots carry a fixed positive reference probability. The algebra applies at fixed cutoff independently of how small the reference normalization is; uniform quantitative budgets remain separate.

For the **reference core laws**, use the product of these probabilities over the locally retained cores and the reference laws on inactive padded slots. This is one positive normalized kernel on every padded label array. On an actual admitted array it is the intended product reference law; it is not yet the full interacting old posterior. The latter must be obtained by restoring the actual complete density perturbation, including cross terms. If that perturbation has the supported extension required by BC.1-BC.2, its exponential times this product, divided by its actual integral, gives a single positive extension of the complete kernel. This construction is compatible with all source coefficients because the whole incoming finite source jet is transported by the same kernel before taking its finite logarithm.

### Proposition CG.4 — exact discrete reference-current bound, conditional on a local reference

Suppose the reference probabilities have the raw locality just stated. Let ell and ell' differ in one anchored block coordinate. Put K=nu_ell and K'=nu_ell' on the common padded old space, including the unchanged product factors. Let chi_ell and chi_ell' be their actual physical readouts. For an old mark F define H_ell(u,v)=F(chi_ell(u,v)). Then the discrete change has the exact compatible split

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 B&=K'[H_{\ell'}-H_\ell],\\
 J&=(K'-K)H_\ell,\\
 K'H_{\ell'}-KH_\ell&=B+J.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{CG.10}
\]

There is no division by a continuous step parameter. For every nonnegative envelope W on the padded space,

\[
 \int W\,|K'-K|\le K'W+KW.
 \tag{CG.11}
\]

Thus supplied endpoint moment/value budgets control the discrete current without an inverse history probability. The direct record is controlled by the actual readout difference or, more crudely, the two endpoint mark budgets. The changed **reference-law and label** footprint is contained in D*(x). In particular its physical size is bounded uniformly even when the old rank changes.

**Proof.** Equation (CG.10) is add-and-subtract on the common padded space. The measure inequality |K'-K|<=K'+K gives (CG.11). Unchanged product factors cancel and the local priority rule changes only cores in D*(x). The source-state padding supplies the exact old coordinate identities at both endpoints. \(\square\)

This proposition does not declare the full physical readout or the interacting kernel local merely because its reference law is local. The raw reconstruction differences and the perturbation coefficients must still have their proved supported expansions. If a difference in the core label changes a coefficient sigma_X, that coefficient's current must be assigned the actual union of X and D*(x); if it depends on a remote class flag, that flag's footprint must still be included. The point of the local code is that a remote class flag is not needed for the **index set or native support** established by CG.3.

There is also a regulator distinction. A logarithmic-mass estimate bounded by the residual witness somewhere in full Y does not automatically give a reference moment budget with the regulator on D0. If the witness is far outside D0, using it enlarges the carrier. The bounded-core BC theorem still needs the actual localized microscopic marked moment and readout bounds, or an explicit carrier expansion paying the extra witness footprint.
