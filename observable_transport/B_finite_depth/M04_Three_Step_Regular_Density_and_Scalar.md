### Lemma TS.1 — retaining each old colour

Suppose the actual primitive bounds just described give old anchored input budgets \(\varepsilon_0,\varepsilon_1\), with a fixed strict component and decorated connected-cluster margin. Then the connected logarithm terms containing a specified old colour \(i\) have anchored value/once-current bound \(C\varepsilon_i\). The subseries containing both distinguished old colours has bound \(C\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_1\).

**Proof.** Choose a fixed small intensity \(a_*\) within the ordinary component and connected-cluster margins. In the positive majorant multiply each occurrence of old colour \(i\) by \(s_i\). Evaluate the majorant at \(s_i^*=a_*/(2\varepsilon_i)\), after decreasing the coupling so both values are at least two. The inflated total old input is at most \(a_*\), so the same majorant is bounded there by a fixed constant, including the reserved first-current decorations.

Every term containing colour \(i\) has degree at least one in \(s_i\), so returning to \(s_i=1\) gains \(1/s_i^*\). Terms containing both colours gain the product. Coefficients of this majorant are nonnegative, so no cancellation is used in this inference. The factorials of the actual exponential are retained in the component enumeration. No Gaussian action is changed by these counting variables; they multiply already bounded primitive old vertices. This proves the assertions. ∎

## 7. Scope and what the third step does not establish

The completed statement is a native theorem at three fixed homogeneous
regular levels, for the explicitly R-averaged density operation and one
microscopic scalar source derivative. Its finite currents use the amended
diagonal path convention. It does not identify them with the specified
canonical endpoint rectangles.

The finite number of coefficient-rate losses, real/complex admission collars, rank tilts, conditional trace stores and geometric covering constants are chosen for these three levels. This proof does not claim that they are uniform when the number of levels grows. The new real AP/SG estimates remain useful separate inputs but do not, by themselves, replace the complex interacting and moving-domain certificates.

The original support-interface obstruction, ambient and averaged M03 requirements, complementary P/Q sectors, arbitrary histories/resets, complete outer connected hierarchy and continuum Yang--Mills existence remain outside this fixed-three statement.
