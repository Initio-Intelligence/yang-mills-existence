# Simultaneous dilation of the three actual smooth layers

This note compares the complete three-layer smooth microscopic mark, and each of its four owned current origins, with the product of the three inherited flat conditional Gaussians. The auxiliary parameter dilates all three physical innovations simultaneously. At its Gaussian endpoint every kernel is independent of its retained input; only the microscopic copy carries a field-dependent quadratic term. Consequently **all three kernel-origin linear jets are radiative** at this fixed depth: each is bounded by a two-coupling-power error. The leading microscopic term is the actual composed Maxwell minimizing lift, with all supplied coupling ratios retained.

The interpolation need not be an actual renormalization flow away from parameter one. Each inherited action is the fixed actual action, evaluated on the dilated physical field. At one the nested integral is exactly TR's smooth mark. The theorem does not give constants uniform in the number of layers, moving-Q transport, or a full nonlinear native irrelevant norm.

## 1. The literal three normal forms

The radii are those of TC/TR: first P, second auxiliary P, third auxiliary P. They have the actual polylogarithmic growth and are held fixed throughout the interpolation.

\[
 V_i=\Xi_i(V_{i+1},B_i),\qquad
 V_{i,*}(V_{i+1})=\Xi_i(V_{i+1},0).
 \tag{SD.1}
\]

Let \(E_i\) be their fixed-depth native analytic coefficient bounds, with \(E_0=0\) in the bare-action assignment. Let \(\mathscr G_{i,V}\) be the complete physical soft-gauge potential at this integration. It has zero value and zero fiber derivative at \(V_{i,*}(V)\), and its quadratic is the literal full averaged sampler. For a retained physical field V and \(t\ne0\), define

Every denominator is its own exact integral. The coordinate power \((tg_i)^{d_i}\), any retained-only classical factor and the constant normalization of the source gauge insertion cancel identically between numerator and denominator before this holomorphic conditional is defined. They remain in the corresponding physical subdensity. All field-dependent Jacobians and all inherited determinant pieces displayed in (SD.3) are kept.

Only the current classical and soft-gauge terms carry \(t^{-2}\). At t=1 every displayed conditional is its exact actual counterpart.

 \|\mathcal H_t\|_{\kappa,4P}\le C_LB_0P^2.
 \tag{SD.14}

**Proof.** The physical maps \(\Xi_i\) are jointly analytic in their admitted physical arguments, with a zero at the common flat center. Their fixed-depth row bounds give
\[
 \|\log V_{j,t}\|_\infty
   \le C_L|t|g_0P,

 \tag{SD.15}

The right side is inside a fixed smaller physical tube throughout the disc in (SD.12). This follows from \(\eta_*\ge C_Lg_0P^3\), choosing c sufficiently small. Every lower conditional is kept in its own critical-canceled normal form, with the full force and target. PJ/TC's actual physical admission therefore applies to the recursive arguments (SD.7)--(SD.8) and to every common component restriction.

At level i the fiber action in (SD.3) has no first physical fiber variation. After the explicit \(t^{-2}\) factor, its cubic prefactor is \(t g_i^3/\gamma_i^2=t\lambda_i g_i\). Every ordinary species is small on the full disc, while fixed-P failures have their original Gaussian tails at \(P_i\).

### Lemma SD.2 — the limiting conditional product is centered

On the recursively dilated arguments, the limit of the i-th conditional is
\[
 \mu_i^{P_i}(dz_i)=

      {\int\chi_i(z)e^{-\frac12 z^{\mathsf T}K_i(I)z}\,dz},
 \tag{SD.17}

with the actual inherited force-corrected flat precision
\[
 K_i(I)=\mathsf C_i^*
     \{\lambda_i\Delta_{i,\mathrm{cl}}(I)
                      +c_{\mathrm{gf},i}\tau_i^*\tau_i\}

 \tag{SD.18}
\]
It is independent of all retained copies and all other innovation variables. The joint limiting law is the product \(\mu_2^{P_2}\mu_1^{P_1}\mu_0^{P_0}\), and each factor has mean zero.

**Proof.** Fiber criticality removes the apparent \(t^{-2}\) singularity in each level's action exactly as in SC.1. At fixed physical retained V the limit would use the precision at V. In the present recursive family every retained V tends to I by (SD.15), independently of all remaining fixed normalized variables. Thus its limiting precision is (SD.18). Every inherited action difference vanishes; the limiting Jacobian factor is a constant and cancels in its own denominator. The limiting domains are exactly the original fixed \(P_i\) domains. \[

 \tag{SD.27}

## 8. Limits that remain material

The conclusion is about the actual smooth three-layer construction and its original current ownership. The all-layer dilation is an analytic device with the correct physical endpoint, not a claim that every intermediate t is a new physical renormalization trajectory.

Each kernel-origin linear jet is small, but its matrix need not be symmetric or obey a Ward identity in the current-direction slot. An all-depth sum of such rows, their nonlinear native completion, and the full per-origin first-current regulator still require separate bounds. Fixed three-layer carrier conversions and analytic margins are not asserted uniform in depth.

The fixed symmetric P domains are essential. Their Gaussian traces are independent of the retained fields at t=0, even though their covariances differ from the untruncated ones. Moving hard-Q domains and their shell currents are not replaced by these domains. Finite-torus winding terms retain the source's separate exponential bound; the marginal comparison in (SD.31)--(SD.32) is stated for the embedded infinite-lattice local kernel and its explicit Ward projection.
