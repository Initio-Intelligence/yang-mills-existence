# Native nonlinear remainders with every smooth current origin retained

This note combines the actual simultaneous dilation SD with the support-preserving completion GC. It bounds the nonlinear complement of the entire Hessian on the actual weak-curvature native class, including each microscopic and kernel current origin separately. The quadratic completion is subtracted explicitly from the microscopic origin only. The three original kernel origins are left as their actual currents.

The common representation is material. Gauge averaging and dilation parity are implemented on one complete four-copy coefficient family, with all denominators, reference data and physical ownership retained. At the Gaussian endpoint each complete tag, not merely the total scalar, is independent of the three kernel copies. This preserves the small `eta_*^2` linear-row envelopes when gauge projection is performed. The native estimate is obtained after all conditional integrations; no pointwise age gain for a held old innovation is asserted.

The result isolates a small nonlinear remainder. GC completes the **whole** Hessian, including its higher-momentum quadratic part. That retained completion still needs a transfer estimate in the physical volume-plus-tree energy/current norm. LA's pair norm alone does not provide that estimate. Thus this note does not complete M04.

## 2. One jointly equivariant and parity-compatible family

### Lemma NO.1 — projection preserves the actual small owned rows

The complete coefficient family can be chosen with all of the following properties simultaneously.

1. Each four-copy coefficient is invariant under simultaneous real gauge transformations of its four arguments. Its diagonal scalar is gauge invariant, and each diagonal owned covector transforms covariantly under a fixed real gauge.
2. At flat background every owned constant current vanishes. If \(L^r_{n,X}\) denotes the linear field jet of \(j^r_{n,X}\) at I, the three kernel rows have deterministic envelopes \(k_{n,X}\) satisfying
\[
 \sum_{r=0}^2\|L^r_{n,X}\|_1\le k_{n,X},
 \qquad
 \sup_n\sum_{X\ni n}e^{\nu n(X)}k_{n,X}
                  \le C_LB_3\eta_*^2.
 \tag{NO.5}
\]
The matrix norm sums the two complete raw direction lists. Fixed carrier moments can be included at smaller rates.
3. At all admitted native centers, on a fixed relative physical tube in the four copies, there are uniform deterministic envelopes \(m_{n,X}\) such that
\[
 |A_{n,X}|\le g^{-2}m_{n,X},
 \qquad
 \sup_n\sum_{X\ni n}e^{\nu n(X)}m_{n,X}
                           \le C_LB_3.
 \tag{NO.6}
\]
Their normalized first currents have envelopes \(C(1+n(X))^d m_{n,X}/g\). On a smaller fixed flat physical chart the summed third normalized copy derivatives have envelopes \(C(1+n(X))^d g m_{n,X}\), for a fixed finite d.

All three conclusions concern one common endpoint family on the native domain, including nontrivial holonomies. They are not different decompositions chosen after a derivative or an estimating gauge is selected.

**Proof.** We describe the representation at the level of complete primitives and normalized connected coefficients.

**The simultaneous real gauge.** In TR.27 apply one fixed retained real gauge u to all four terminal copies. At each earlier layer use its actual block lift and transform every reference, force, target, constraint input and free colour fiber. The corresponding changes of the original free variables are constant real orthogonal adjoint maps, independent of the varying terminal copies. They preserve the fixed product P domains. The actual source maps are covariant, the microscopic Wilson source is invariant, and every normalized kernel changes with its own complete denominator. Thus the full off-diagonal amplitude, not only its diagonal, is invariant under this simultaneous action.

The native construction and its fixed physical buffers are uniform over these real orbits, as in PJ.1a--PJ.1b and EC. To see the four-copy version of the native bound, repeat PJ.1b with four finitely many independent relative perturbations about a common admitted center. Each occurrence keeps its own critical-canceled normal form. The complete input changes are bounded by \(C_Lr\), the covariance and determinant use the same actual family, and the bounded microscopic Wilson source has norm \(C_LL^{12}|\alpha|g_0^{-2}\). Its inverse-beta local source circle is inserted before the three exact same-gas normalizations. No source energy at one copy is compared to the energy at another. Old nodes are estimated uniformly in the later real variables before that later integral is taken. This gives a common per-tag envelope (NO.6), before varying the real center or performing a gauge integral. It excludes moving-Q payloads.

**Gauge transport of the entire dilation family.** Perform the same fixed u change in SD's complete t-family. Its terminal field is \((e^{tg_0b_r})^u\), its flat center is the fixed pure gauge \(I^u\), and its physical innovation has the transformed adjoint fiber coordinate. The transformation is independent of t and b. The actual t-disc, source envelope, positive comparison and rank costs are unchanged, since real adjoint maps are isometric. In particular the normalized Gaussian endpoint has the precision at \(I^u\), conjugate to the one at I, and the same fixed domains.

There is a stronger endpoint property than a zero of the total kernel current. The complete physical critical-force cancellation is performed before component restriction, and the restricted family is the continuation of that already canceled normal form. No independently decoupled minimum is assumed critical for an undeformed action. At t=0 each complete component formula consequently has its precision, domain, Jacobian and inverse normalizer independent of all kernel-copy b's. Every lower retained physical argument equals its fixed pure-gauge center. The source quotient by \(t^2\) is its actual quadratic bare Wilson source; its only retained-copy dependence is b_M. A fixed-P failure tag or normalization connector is also independent of the kernel b's at this endpoint. Hence **each complete endpoint tag** is independent of b_0,b_1,b_2. Its three kernel rows are zero before summing tags. This remains true in every transported u-chart.

**A global endpoint meaning of the parity projection.** It would be incorrect to define a new native family on arbitrary holonomy fields by writing one global logarithm of U. That is unnecessary. At t=1 the parity partner in SD, written in a flat chart as \(A_X(-1,-\mathbf b)\), is exactly the complete primitive expression in which every original innovation is reflected, \(z_i\mapsto-z_i\), including its occurrences in lower targets and in all normalizers. Define this reflected expression directly for every admitted real native U and its relative physical tube. The fixed domains are centrally symmetric, so the variable change has unit absolute Jacobian and preserves the physical four-copy amplitude. It also preserves each owned current separately, since the reflection is independent of every terminal variable and each original factor keeps its owner. Average a complete scalar coefficient with this reflected coefficient. In every flat or transported pure-gauge chart, the resulting t-family is precisely its parity projection \(\frac12[A_X(t,\mathbf b)+A_X(-t,-\mathbf b)]\). Thus each kernel-row jet is even in t. The uniform large-disc bound and the preceding coefficientwise endpoint zero give the SD even-Cauchy estimate for each tag. It yields the envelopes (NO.5), uniformly in the fixed real u parameter.

Finally take the compact Haar projection of the **complete four-copy coefficients**, with simultaneous gauge action on all four arguments and the complete reference data. Gauge adjoints commute with innovation reflection: \(T_u(-z)=-T_uz\). The projection can therefore be made on the entire reflected t-family before the Cauchy estimates. ## 3. Complete the entire diagonal Hessian and assign its derivative explicitly

 R^M_{n,X}(U;h)=j^M_{n,X}(U;h)-dC_{n,X}(U)[h],

 R^r_{n,X}(U;h)=j^r_{n,X}(U;h),\quad r=0,1,2.

Then
\[
 dN_{n,X}(U)[h]=\sum_{r=M,0,1,2}R^r_{n,X}(U;h).
 \tag{NO.9}

This adds the explicitly displayed quadratic-completion counterterm to the microscopic/background record. It does not reassign any derivative of an original kernel, inherited density or inverse normalizer to another origin.

The left-current version of (NO.11) includes the BCH conversion. Its first and second a derivatives cost \(O(g)\) and \(O(g^2)\), respectively. ## 5. Per-origin nonlinear bounds on the native class

### Theorem NO.2 — infinite lattice or injectively localized carriers

For a smaller positive rate \(\kappa\), independent of volume and weak coupling at these three fixed levels,
\[
 \sup_n\sum_{X\ni n}e^{\kappa n(X)}
          \sup_{U\ {\rm native}}|N_{n,X}(U)|
                       \le C_LB_3gP^3,
 \tag{NO.15}
\]
and, **for each** \(r=M,0,1,2\),
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \sup_n\sum_{X\ni n}e^{\kappa n(X)}
       \sup_{U\ {\rm native}}\sum_d
             |R^r_{n,X}(U;e_d)|
       \le C_LB_3\{\eta_*^2P+gP^2\}.
 \quad}
 \tag{NO.16}
\]
The same deterministic envelopes bound each admitted normalized coordinate path current of length at most one, with P+C in place of P. Every fixed spatial moment can be inserted after a further strict rate loss. The exact sum identity is (NO.9).

**Proof.** Choose \(n_*=D\log(1/g)\), with D fixed sufficiently large for the spare carrier rate. By \(gP^2\le1\), every carrier of size at most n_* has \(n_*^2gP\to0\) and admits (NO.14) inside the fixed flat chart.

For one such short carrier, Taylor-expand each covector at flat background in that estimating gauge. Its constant is zero, its linear jet is bounded by \(k_{n,X}\) up to a fixed factor, and its second derivative has (NO.11). Hence, using the complete direction list,
\[
 \sum_d|R^r_{n,X}(U;e_d)|
  \le C(1+n(X))^{d'}
        \{P k_{n,X}+gP^2m_{n,X}\}.
 \tag{NO.17}
\]
The two powers of n from (NO.14), the coordinate incidence and the loop lengths are included in the fixed polynomial. The BCH conversion from logarithm covectors to left raw covectors has a uniformly bounded matrix on the small physical chart, acts on the same edge, and is applied to the whole difference (NO.8). Its derivative correction at flat vanishes because each constant covector is zero. It therefore changes only that polynomial and a fixed group factor.

Sum (NO.17) with a strict rate loss and use (NO.5)--(NO.6). This gives exactly the right side of (NO.16) for short carriers, separately for every origin. The scalar N has zero jets through order two, so GC.4 gives its short-carrier bound \(C_LB_3gP^3\) in the same family.

For long carriers use the uniform native four-copy derivative envelope and the real-unitary loop bound for the completion. They give \(C(1+n)^d m_{n,X}/g\) for each owned remainder covector, and \(C(1+n)^d m_{n,X}/g^2\) for N. The unused exponential rate bounds their sums by
\[
 C_LB_3g^{-1}e^{-c n_*},\qquad
 C_LB_3g^{-2}e^{-c n_*},
 \tag{NO.18}
\]
respectively. Choose D so the first is at most \(C_LB_3g\) and the second at most \(C_LB_3g\). Since P is at least one, these are absorbed by the terms in (NO.15)--(NO.16). No gauge of a long carrier is used. The same estimates hold along the fixed-gauge admitted short path, while the native envelope controls long-carrier paths directly. This proves the theorem. \(\square\)

### Corollary NO.3 — the actual finite-torus winding terms

On a torus of shortest period \(\ell\), split off carriers whose required estimating box does not lift injectively. Such carriers have size at least \(c\ell\). Their native deterministic bounds give the additional terms
\[
 C_LB_3g^{-2}e^{-c\ell}
 \quad\hbox{in (NO.15)},\qquad

 \quad\hbox{in each (NO.16)}.
 \tag{NO.19}
\]
No small-link estimate is used for these terms. This permits flat nontrivial holonomies and does not bound them by local curvature. A lower bound on the torus period, if imposed in an application, must be stated before absorbing these terms into a coupling-small budget.

**Proof.** This is the long-carrier estimate (NO.18) with the size lower bound \(c\ell\), using the actual native value and first-current envelopes. The counterpart of the injective-box estimate applies to every remaining carrier. \(\square\)

## 6. What has been gained, and the remaining transfer problem

For the actual smooth microscopic block source, the complement of the entire diagonal Hessian now has a native scalar bound and a separate bound for **every** original smooth current origin. Its microscopic origin includes exactly the explicit derivative of GC's completion; the original three kernel origins retain their whole densities, inherited actions and own normalizers. The small kernel-row budget was proved inside the common gauge/parity family, before absolute carrier sums. This is the reason the nonlinear native current estimate can use \(\eta_*^2P\) rather than an uncontrolled linear term.

These are unweighted pointwise native bounds in the actual supported carrier representation. Dividing them by a regulator that is at least one cannot make them worse, but that observation does not establish the requested complete M04 norm: the **retained completion** C still contains the higher-momentum quadratic response as a nonlinear gauge-invariant function. FH and LA control its Gaussian quadratic kernel in energy and local pair norms; a uniform physical volume-plus-tree energy/current transfer for the entire completed function has not been proved by those pair estimates.

The common fixed-depth physical tubes and comparison constants are also not an all-depth induction. Moving sharp-Q domains, their actual shell currents and latent-core evolution remain separate tasks. The present argument avoids the fixed-z moving-base obstruction by estimating the already integrated owned currents, but does not claim that the obstruction disappears pointwise or that its age dependence has been summed.

The result uses the actual source and couplings, the literal four-copy current convention, the source physical buffers, and exact same-gas normalization. Its only new representation choices are simultaneous complete-scalar Haar projection and a complete innovation-reflection projection, both proved to preserve the physical endpoint and its separate owners. No M04 or Yang--Mills existence claim follows.
