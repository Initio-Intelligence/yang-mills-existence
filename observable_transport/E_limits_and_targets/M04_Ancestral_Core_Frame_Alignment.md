# Ancestral core frames: what the real comparison does and does not control

This note examines the next uniform-depth application after the three-step construction. It proves a source-shaped example showing why AP's transporting gauge cannot be dropped, identifies the actual common-complex-frame requirement, and separates that requirement from already controlled core geometry and real admission. It does not modify AP, SG, NC or the three-step proofs.

## 1. The part already controlled

For a birth at level b, CGD keeps every intervening real frame input, every whole-Q reference box and every actual constraint/gauge stencil. Its union enlargement obeys

\[
 r_{j+1}\le L^{-1}r_j+C_L(R_{j+1}+m+1),\qquad
 r_N\le C_LR_N\le C'_LR_b .
 \tag{AF.1}
\]

The final real dependency core therefore has bounded current volume per birth, uniformly in its age. CC separately shows that ordinary fixed-collar carrier covers have a grouped affine contraction in the actual volume-plus-tree size. Neither result bounds an accumulated integral, covariance or gauge-coordinate cost.

At a real homogeneous source step, the full Q fiber makes the preceding P and axial selectors redundant by CMP119 (3.7)--(3.9), before its R split. The fixed strict margins use the stated source hierarchy. The zero-entry Q comparison uses (3.17)--(3.19). NC's two zero completions exploit these statements without replacing the Q fiber by an R-good subevent: they retain the complete older determining inputs and omit complex off-stencil chains from the real completion.

There is no new chronological obstruction in repeating that *real identification* through finitely many layers. Starting with a real completion at a later layer, one can make the preceding real completion using only its own kept full stencil and its actual target. All local data on the saved older core then agree exactly. This observation concerns the identification of real local domains. It does not assert a depth-uniform analytic coefficient registry for the independently continued surrounding factors.

AP proves a genuinely uniform real matrix result in a **transported birth frame**. For real admitted minimal fields `F_j=U_j(V_j)`, its gauges satisfy

\[
 F_j^{u_j}=e^{i\eta A_j}F_{j+1},\qquad
 \|A_j\|_{\mathcal R_b}\le C\epsilon_jL^{-(j-b)},
 \tag{AF.2}
\]

and hence

\[
 \|T_b(u_j)K_b(F_j)T_b(u_j)^*-K_b(F_{j+1})\|_\rho
       \le C\epsilon_jL^{-(j-b)}.
 \tag{AF.3}
\]

The source coupling inequality makes the right sides summable. The full force correction, nonlinear averaged sampler, exceptional embedding and fixed birth normalization are included. SG additionally gives a summed covariant response for invariant Gaussian events. These facts do not supply a small difference between the two matrices in an arbitrary fixed raw free-coordinate basis.

## 2. An actual flat chain with no raw-frame age suppression

The following construction is within the source homogeneous regular family. It concerns the actual first primitive precision and shows that a direct removal of the gauge transports in (AF.3) would be false.

Use the allowed sufficiently large fixed odd blocking factor L, and fix birth `b=0`. Choose any later index `j>=1` and a j-grid vertex y which is not a (j+1)-grid center. On the j-grid set

\[
 v_j(y)=e^{itH},\qquad v_j(x)=I\quad(x\ne y),
 \qquad 0<|t|\le c\delta_j,
 \tag{AF.4}
\]

where H is a fixed noncentral traceless Hermitian generator and c is a sufficiently small fixed constant. In particular `v_j=I` at every (j+1)-center. Let `u` be its literal block-constant microscopic lift on the j-block partition, and put

\[
 F=I^u,
 \qquad V_i=\mathcal M^iF\quad(1\le i\le j),
 \qquad V_{j+1}=I.
 \tag{AF.5}
\]

Every microscopic plaquette of F is identity. Exact endpoint covariance of the nonlinear average gives

\[
 V_i=I^{u|_{T^{(i)}}},\quad i\le j,
 \qquad \mathcal M V_j=I.
 \tag{AF.6}
\]

The source axial covariance (CMP102 (181)) identifies

\[
 U_i(V_i)=F\quad(1\le i\le j),\qquad
 U_{j+1}(I)=I,
 \tag{AF.7}
\]

in the actual blockwise source representatives. For i below j the lifted gauge is constant on every (i+1)-block, so all corresponding ordinary innovations are zero and their averaged gauge variables are identity. At step j the integrated field is the small center-fixed pure gauge `I^{v_j}`. Its relative link norm is at most `C|t|<2delta_j`; its next target and every local comparison minimum are flat. Its soft/axial cutoff is strictly satisfied by `C|t|<epsilon_j`, using the original fixed `delta_j/epsilon_j` hierarchy. All earlier local curvature tests are exactly flat. Thus this is an actual admitted real homogeneous chain, with strict margins and no appeal to a toy conditional event. The R partition can be summed as in the current density-state operation.

For each chosen finite j use a supplied finite weak-coupling trajectory reaching that level, with its microscopic coupling taken sufficiently small. The construction requires no infinite admitted trajectory and makes no claim that an arbitrary-depth interacting density induction has been established.

At the first birth, F is already in the averaged-block gauge: u is constant on each first L-block, so the sampled intrablock transports are identity. The complete actual precision therefore obeys exact source covariance,

\[
 K_0(F)=T_0(u)K_0(I)T_0(u)^*.
 \tag{AF.8}
\]

This is the actual canonical soft-gauge coefficient, not only the relative extension of a noncanonical background. Its Wilson force is zero because F is flat.

### A nonzero physical cross block

Choose two adjacent first L-blocks on opposite sides of one face of the j-block supporting u, away from its corners. The two first blocks have different constant gauge values. On their adjacent fine layers choose matching tangential bonds, all intrablock and away from tangential boundary faces. The matching scalar coefficients form a finite-dimensional space of dimension greater than `4d`; the allowed fixed large L ensures this.

Let a be supported on those bonds on the first side and b on their translates across the face on the second side, with the same scalar array f. The linear first coarse average of a can read only the at most `2d` coarse bonds incident to its block; the same holds for b. These are the literal two-block averaging paths of CMP98. Impose both scalar systems `Qa=0` and `Qb=0`, at most `4d` equations. A nonzero f remains. Normalize `sum f_e^2=1`.

None of these tangential bonds is a distinguished face-crossing exceptional bond. The actual independent-coordinate embedding consequently gives

\[
 C_0a=a,\qquad C_0b=b.
 \tag{AF.9}
\]

The soft sampler is block-local, so its cross pairing between a and b is zero. The only plaquettes in both curl supports are the plaquettes crossing the shared face, and their two tangential sides have opposite orientation. Hence the flat Wilson quadratic gives

\[
 \langle a,K_0(I)b\rangle=-c_{\rm W}\sum_e f_e^2=-c_{\rm W},
 \qquad c_{\rm W}>0,
 \tag{AF.10}
\]

when both scalar arrays carry the same unit Lie colour. All constants are fixed source trace/coupling conventions, independent of j. This uses the complete first precision `C_0^*(d^*d+c_g tau^*tau)C_0`; there is no omitted flat constraint-force term.

For a nonabelian Yang--Mills group, choose unit colours S and T in an `su(2)` subalgebra so that

\[
 \langle S,T\rangle=0,\qquad
 \left|\langle\operatorname{Ad}_{e^{-itH}}S,T\rangle\right|
          \ge c_H|t|\qquad(0<|t|\le t_0).
 \tag{AF.11}
\]

Put colour S on a and T on b. Equations (AF.8)--(AF.11) imply

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \|K_0(F)-K_0(I)\|_{\rm op}\ge c_L|t|.
 \quad}
 \tag{AF.12}
\]

The same lower bound up to a fixed constant holds in the two-sided entry norm, since the test vectors have fixed finite support. Taking `t=c delta_j` shows that a bound `C epsilon_j L^{-j}` in this *unaligned raw basis* cannot hold uniformly in j. The fixed ratio `delta_j/epsilon_j` cannot compensate `L^{-j}`.

### What this example does not say

The aligned matrices in (AF.3) are identical in this example: take the inverse of u, and the difference is zero. A simultaneous adjoint change of Gaussian coordinates, an invariant full-Lie-fiber event, and a gauge-invariant mark leaves the actual probability or scalar unchanged. Thus (AF.12) does not contradict AP, SG, covariance of the original measure, or a possible gauge-covariant carrier theorem. It proves only that the raw matrix entries have a gauge response which the desired common frame must handle exactly. One cannot obtain that handling by applying the numerical right side of AP to an untransported matrix.

## 3. The exact additional issue for complex localization

There is a genuine analytic source input. CMP102 (172)--(180) constructs the relative minimum by an analytic equation on a full small array of determining data. with the same exact target and with the full old Q-core inputs specified real. `H_{b,D}` is a restricted independent-angle frame followed by the exact exceptional solve. An independently selected Prop9 gauge need not preserve (AF.13), those fixed real Q inputs, or their exact separated-component identity.

The real AP comparison removes its gauge by a pointwise orthogonal map. On a complex continuation the corresponding map is a complex adjoint map. Analytic covariance still holds, but its Euclidean norm is no longer one. Multiplying such transports and bounding their norms individually can produce an accumulated factor. Proposition 9 does not identify a component-compatible carrier family for that product which also fixes every saved real core. The exact flat example above shows why ignoring the transporting part is not an admissible shortcut.

There is a second identification issue. AP compares actual critical backgrounds. The auxiliary core frames and independently decoupled source slots need not be stationary for the undeformed action. It is legitimate to continue their already-canceled native normal forms, as TC does at fixed depth. It is not legitimate to invoke the critical-orbit identity for an arbitrary independently decoupled frame and thereby infer an age factor for all its coefficients.

To use the summable `L^{-(j-b)}` in the actual old certificate, the next construction must identify one joint family, rather than only a bound on separately chosen gauges. Its necessary output is explicit:

These are testable requirements on the actual source maps. They are not declared proved here.
