## 4. A rigorous obstruction to an automatic inference from C08 to UV compactness

This section proves a logical nonimplication. It is **not** a counterexample to the Wilson construction, its C01 identities, or the Yang–Mills existence conjecture.

C8A.23 and C8A.26 retain the scaling factors

\[
\|O_{h,x}-\nu O_{h,x}\|_p
\le Ch^{-4}(Q_x\sqrt p+p),
\]
\[
|\kappa_m(O_{h,x_1},\ldots,O_{h,x_m})|
\le C^{m+1}(m!)^2h^{-4m}\prod_iQ_{x_i}
e^{-\theta\tau/(m-1)}.
\tag{A.6}
\]

Their constants are uniform in cutoff depth, but the quantities on the right are not cutoff independent. Even setting every $Q_x=1$ and removing all outer histories does not make (A.6) a uniform bound for physically smeared fields.

### Proposition A.1

There is a family of product convex-support laws with Hessian equal to the identity, uniform moments, and estimates (A.6) for which the variance of a fixed physical smearing diverges like $h^{-4}$.

**Proof.** On the four-dimensional mesh of a fixed box, let the coordinates be independent with density

\[
d\mu_P(z)=Z_P^{-1}e^{-z^2/2}\mathbf1_{[-P,P]}(z)\,dz,
\qquad P\ge2.
\]

The potential Hessian is 1; the support is a product of symmetric convex intervals. The centered linear-coordinate moments are bounded by $C\sqrt p$, uniformly in $P$. Put

\[
X_x=z_x^2-\mathbb E z_x^2,
\qquad O_{h,x}=h^{-4}X_x.
\]

Uniform Gaussian domination gives $\|X_x\|_p\le Cp$ and the corresponding gradient norm $\|2z_x\|_p\le C\sqrt p$. The moment generating function is uniformly bounded in a fixed complex neighborhood of zero and uniformly close to 1 on a smaller disk. Cauchy estimates for its logarithm give

\[
|\kappa_m(X_x)|\le C^m m!.
\]

Every mixed-site cumulant vanishes by independence. Therefore (A.6), including its spatial exponential, holds with $Q_x=1$. The variance $v_P=\operatorname{Var}(z^2)$ is uniformly bounded below for $P\ge2$: the events $|z|<1/2$ and $3/2<|z|<2$ have uniformly positive probabilities and separated values of $z^2$.

For a nonzero smooth compactly supported $f$, the ordinary physical smearing is

\[
F_h(f)=h^4\sum_xf(x)O_{h,x}=\sum_xf(x)X_x.
\]

Consequently

\[
\operatorname{Var}F_h(f)
=v_P\sum_xf(x)^2
\asymp h^{-4}\int f(x)^2\,dx\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

All constants used above can be uniform when $P=P_h\ge2$ grows with the cutoff. This proves the proposition. $\square$

The variance uses the joint test $f(x)f(y)$, which is generally not coincidence-flat. Thus this proposition does not disprove a restricted conditional C08 cumulant bound on coincidence-flat tests, including the separate shell estimate proved in [Physical normalization, ultraviolet bounds and source transport](continuum_bounds.md). It isolates why a conditional microscopic/native estimate needs additional physical matching, renormalization and summability before it becomes a full smeared-field estimate. It also respects the qualification about coincidence-flat tests: on tests vanishing on all coincidence sets, the centered independent example has zero off-diagonal moments. Thus such a restricted hierarchy can be trivial even while the usual smeared variance diverges. The example does not refute the proposed ER.7 estimate (see [physical normalization](../physical_scale/physical_normalization.md)); it shows that neither a nontrivial continuum field nor the full physical distributional hierarchy follows from the displayed C08 bounds alone.

\[

\le \nu\bigl[(\nabla f)^TH_I^{-1}\nabla f\bigr],

\[

### 7.3 Support and outer-only factors are indispensable

OH.17 differentiates an inner normalizer only while its inner support is independent of the same-current $y$ chart. A moving support would add boundary terms. The outer-only factor $q$ remains in the outer density even though it cancels from the inner conditional quotient. These qualifications are mathematically necessary.

For an **exponentially localized** outer covariance theorem, a replacement for product support must supply the weighted boundary-domain compatibility used by C08, not only a nonnegative unweighted boundary form. General convex global constraints can induce long-range correlations despite Hessian $I$. For example, an independent standard Gaussian vector conditioned on $\sum_i y_i\le0$ has off-diagonal covariance $-2/(\pi N)$ on $N$ sites, inconsistent with a fixed exponential bound at arbitrarily large separation. The unweighted convex-domain Brascamp–Lieb inequality still holds. Literal local product factors admit the required block-scalar spatial weights; the revised outer note now states this qualification.

**Outer-law implication.** OH.9–OH.21 is a valid conditional theorem with the explicit inner/mixed derivative and weighted-support hypotheses. It does not establish the actual full retained-history defect or support theorem. Its selected-bad-domain and mixture examples correctly illustrate those missing inferences.
