# Conditional laws and the continuum hierarchy

The conditional estimates on a fixed regular history do not control the covariance of the outer conditional means. The continuum application also requires physical normalization and estimates for the full hierarchy.

The fixed-exterior anchored score is derived in [Gauge covariance of partial integrals and exterior scores](partial_gauge_transport.md). Its implication for the boundary response is explained in [Conditional-law estimates and the continuum application](conditional_to_continuum.md). The seven reflected measurements are treated in [Reflected susceptibilities and physical normalization](../physical_scale/physical_normalization.md).
