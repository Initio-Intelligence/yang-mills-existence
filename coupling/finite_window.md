This follows directly by differentiating the transverse Maxwell two-point kernel \(e^{-|p|t}/(2|p|)\) on a time-separated pair. Any purely instantaneous electric contact is absent on the separated, coincidence-flat tests. With an ultraviolet cutoff on form factors, pull half a heat factor past each annihilation and then remove the cutoff:
\[
 \|a(f_x)e^{-tH}\|\le
 \|e^{-t|p|/2}f_x/\sqrt{|p|}\|_2\,\|H^{1/2}e^{-tH/2}\|
 \le C t^{-2}.
\]
For two annihilations the bound is the product of the two damped \(L^2(dp/|p|)\) norms times \(\|H e^{-tH/2}\|\), hence \(C t^{-4}\). Indeed on every particle sector the sum of ordered pair energies \(\sum_{i\ne j}|p_i||p_j|\) is at most the square of total energy. The creation-creation term follows by adjunction, and the creation-annihilation form is bounded by a product of one-annihilation estimates. These control the entire normal-ordered quadratic form. Spatial smearing and the fixed finite channel factors give \(\|e^{-tH}Q_\alpha(g)e^{-tH}\|\le C_N t^{-4}\|g\|_1\) for every comparison component, uniformly in the degree of the vector. There is no infrared divergence in three spatial dimensions in the displayed norms.

For any fixed positive-time polynomial \(F\) in the comparison's coincidence-flat joint test domain, the reflected shifted pair and its central mollified insertion remain in that domain: the central time is separated from each half by the fixed margin \(t-\epsilon\); collisions internal to either tuple are killed by its flat coefficient. Convergence on these two test products and the free OS realization imply the **limiting cutoff inequality (E.17)** on this particular sequence. Use the free sandwich at \(t/2\) inside (E.18), integrate the nonnegative mollifier, then put the two vanishing fixed-test convergence errors into \(\varepsilon_n(F,t,\epsilon,g,\alpha)\). Its multiplicative constant is independent of \(F\), its degree and \(\epsilon\). This does not bound the finite Wilson Hamiltonian sandwich (E.16); the allowed per-fixed-test \(o(1)\) in (E.17) matters.
